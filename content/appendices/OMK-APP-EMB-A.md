## 1. Purpose

This appendix is the single register of every quantitative setpoint used in the suite. Each value is classified as either:

- **Guideline-anchored** — traceable to a published best-practice reference (ESHRE 2015, ASRM 2022, Istanbul Consensus) or a quality standard (ISO 15189, GHS); or
- **Locally defined 🔧** — set by Oxford Medical Kuwait for its equipment, media and licensing context. Every 🔧 value requires Medical Director sign-off and is reviewed on equipment/media change.

> **No value in any SOP is left implicit.** Where an SOP says "per Appendix A", the operative number lives here. Locally defined values marked **🔧 TBC** are placeholders awaiting clinical sign-off and must be set before the relevant SOP is approved for use.
