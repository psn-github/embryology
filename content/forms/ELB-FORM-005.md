## Purpose

This checklist walks a single treatment cycle through the laboratory from oocyte retrieval to transfer or cryopreservation. Each step is initialled by the performing embryologist and, where the parent SOP requires witnessing (ELB-SOP-002), by a witness. It gives an at-a-glance assurance that every critical step was done, witnessed and recorded.

## Scope

One checklist per autologous cycle, filed in the cycle file behind the cover sheet (ELB-FORM-004). It does not replace the detailed worksheets; it confirms they were completed.

> Witnessing (column **W**) is mandatory at every step where gametes or embryos are identified, combined, moved between vessels or placed into / removed from storage (ELB-SOP-002). A missing or failed witness match halts the step until identity is resolved and the step is re-witnessed.
