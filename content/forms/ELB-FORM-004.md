## Purpose

This cover sheet is the front page of the laboratory file for a single treatment cycle. It carries the couple's verified identifiers, the cycle number and key dates, and an index of every record held in the file, so that the file can be reconciled at any point and at archive. It is an internal record completed by the laboratory.

## Scope

One cover sheet per cycle for autologous treatment. Identifiers are taken and verified per ELB-SOP-016; recordkeeping and retention follow ELB-SOP-001.
