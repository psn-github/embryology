> This form records the couple's election for the fate of their own stored embryos, oocytes or sperm. It is governed by the Informed Consent policy (OMK-POL-EMB-0006) and the cryostorage security & access policy (OMK-POL-EMB-0007); the action taken is recorded against the inventory (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045) and, where applicable, the disposition/transfer record (OMK-FORM-EMB-0054). Storage limits are defined in Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A).
