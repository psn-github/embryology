### SAMPLE PRODUCTION INSTRUCTIONS

1.  Before producing your semen sample, first wash your hands and genital area using the antibacterial soap provided. Please ensure that soap is removed before drying the area thoroughly since soap can be toxic to sperm.
2.  Please use the sterile pot provided to collect your sample.
3.  Remove the lid on the sample pot prior to sample production.
4.  Only semen collected into the pot can be used due to the risk of contamination. Spilt semen should not be retrieved. If spillage occurs, please record in Section 2. If you are producing using a silastic condom then the condom should be sealed with the tie provided and placed within the sterile pot.
5.  Do not use lubricant, which is not supplied directly from the clinic (this can be toxic to sperm).
6.  If producing the sample offsite, please ensure the sample is labelled with full name, DOB and unit number. It should be delivered to the lab within 50 minutes of production, keep the sample at room temperature, and do not expose to light. If you have any problems producing the sample, please call and notify the clinic.

#### Samples will not be processed if they are unlabelled, the label is incorrect or there is any doubt to the identity of the sample.
