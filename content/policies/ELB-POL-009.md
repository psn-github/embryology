## 1. Purpose

To define how informed consent is obtained, recorded and honoured for every laboratory procedure performed on a couple's gametes and embryos, and for the cryopreservation, storage, continued storage and disposition of those gametes and embryos. Consent is the legal and ethical authority for everything the laboratory does with a patient's biological material; this policy ensures that authority is explicit, current, documented and traceable.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all embryology and andrology procedures using the **patient couple's own gametes** (autologous), and to the cryopreservation and storage of sperm, oocytes and embryos. Donor and surrogacy arrangements are out of scope and are not offered. This policy governs the laboratory's reliance on consent; the clinical consent conversation is led by the treating clinician.

## 3. Policy statements

1. **No procedure without valid consent.** The laboratory does not initiate any procedure — insemination, ICSI, biopsy, vitrification, warming, transfer or disposition — unless a current, signed consent covering that procedure is on file and verified against the patient identity (ELB-SOP-016).
2. **Specific and informed.** Consent must be specific to the procedure and to the intended use, fate and storage of the resulting material. Patients are informed of the purpose, the reasonably foreseeable risks, the alternatives, and the consequences of withdrawing consent.
3. **Couple authority for shared embryos.** Embryos created from the couple's gametes require the recorded consent of **both partners** for creation, storage, continued storage, thaw/warming, transfer and disposition. The laboratory acts only where both consents align.
4. **Withdrawal.** Either partner may withdraw consent in writing at any time before the material is used. On receipt of a valid withdrawal the laboratory ceases the affected activity and follows the disposition recorded under the consent and ELB-FORM-003.
5. **Storage period and review.** The agreed storage period, the review/renewal points, and the agreed action at expiry are recorded at the outset (ELB-FORM-001) and tracked against the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016). The locally adopted maximum storage period and renewal interval are recorded in **Appendix A** and applied as required by the licensing authority.
6. **Language and comprehension.** Patient-facing consent is provided **bilingually (English / Khaleeji Arabic)** so that comprehension is not limited by language (ELB-FORM-001, ELB-FORM-002, ELB-FORM-003). Where a patient understands neither working language, an interpreter is arranged and recorded.
7. **Confidentiality.** Consent records are personal health information and are handled per the patient-confidentiality policy (ELB-POL-008) and cryostorage access controls (ELB-POL-007).

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Treating clinician** — conducts the consent conversation, confirms comprehension, and ensures the correct form is completed and signed before the laboratory is asked to act.
- **Embryologist** — verifies that valid, signed, procedure-specific consent exists and matches the patient before performing any procedure; escalates any gap and does not proceed without it.
- **Quality Manager** — audits consent completeness against the cycle and storage records.
- **Medical Director** — owns this policy and signs off the locally defined storage parameters in Appendix A.

## 5. Records & verification

| Record | Document |
|---|---|
| Cryopreservation & storage consent (bilingual) | ELB-FORM-001 |
| Procedure consent acknowledgment (bilingual) | ELB-FORM-002 |
| Specimen disposition election (bilingual) | ELB-FORM-003 |
| Cryostorage inventory (storage period tracking) | ELB-LOG-016 |
| Disposition / transfer record | ELB-LOG-017 |

The embryologist's pre-procedure identity and consent check is itself witnessed and recorded (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).

## 6. Deviations

A missing, expired, ambiguous or non-matching consent is a **stop condition**: the procedure is held and escalated to the treating clinician. Any procedure performed without verified valid consent is a reportable adverse event (ELB-POL-005) and is logged as a deviation (ELB-POL-006).

## 7. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The maximum storage period, the renewal/review interval, the default action at storage expiry or non-renewal, and the witnessing requirements for withdrawal are **locally defined** and recorded in **Appendix A** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director and, where applicable, legal sign-off). The principle of specific, informed, withdrawable consent is **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022).

## 9. Teaching point

Consent is not a signature event, it is a standing authority with a lifespan: it can expire, it can be partial, and it can be withdrawn. The laboratory's discipline is to check that the authority still exists and still covers exactly what is about to be done — every time, for both partners — before a single gamete is touched.
