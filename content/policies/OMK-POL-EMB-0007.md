## 1. Purpose

To define how access to cryostorage is controlled and how stored gametes and embryos are kept secure, intact and continuously identifiable for the full duration of storage. The policy protects irreplaceable patient material against unauthorised access, misidentification, environmental failure and loss, and ensures every entry to a cryostorage vessel is authorised, witnessed and recorded. It works alongside the LN2 tank SOPs (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045, OMK-SOP-EMB-0045).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all liquid-nitrogen storage vessels, their contents and their monitoring systems, and to all staff, contractors and visitors with potential access to the cryostore. It covers physical security, access authorisation, identity and inventory control, environmental monitoring, and contingency. Only the couple's own (autologous) gametes and embryos are stored. The mechanics of LN2 handling and tank monitoring are detailed in OMK-SOP-EMB-0045/037; chain of custody in OMK-SOP-EMB-0008.

## 3. Policy statements

1. **Controlled access.** The cryostore is a restricted area. Access is limited to authorised, trained staff; entry by others is supervised. The room and vessels are physically secured against unauthorised access when unattended.
2. **Authorised entries only.** No cryostorage vessel is opened except for an authorised, recorded purpose — deposit, retrieval, audit, fill or maintenance. Every entry is logged with the date, operator, purpose and the specimens affected.
3. **Identity and witnessing.** Every deposit and retrieval of patient material is identity-checked and witnessed (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002), and reconciled against the cryostorage inventory (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045) so that what is recorded and what is physically present always agree.
4. **Inventory integrity.** A current inventory records, for each specimen, the patient identity, type, location (tank/canister/cane/position), date stored and consent/storage status. The inventory is reconciled on a defined schedule and after every audit.
5. **Continuous monitoring.** Tank level, temperature and integrity are monitored continuously with alarms that reach staff at all times, including out of hours (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045, OMK-SOP-EMB-0061). An alarm is responded to within the local response window.
6. **Contingency and backup.** Spare vessel capacity, an emergency LN2 supply and a documented transfer plan exist so that material can be moved to safety if a vessel fails. Contingency is tested on a defined schedule.
7. **Confidentiality.** Inventory and access records contain personal health information and are protected per the confidentiality policy (OMK-POL-EMB-0005). Access rights are granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed.
8. **Disposition only on authority.** Material is removed from storage for disposition only on verified consent and authority (OMK-POL-EMB-0006), witnessed and recorded; nothing leaves storage on assumption.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **All authorised staff** — access the cryostore only for recorded purposes, witness and log every deposit and retrieval, and respond to alarms within the response window.
- **Laboratory Lead / cryostore custodian** — maintains access authorisation, the inventory and reconciliation schedule, alarm coverage and contingency readiness.
- **Quality Manager** — audits access logs, inventory reconciliation and alarm-response records, and tracks discrepancies to closure.
- **Medical Director** — owns this policy, approves the access-authorisation list, and signs off the locally defined alarm setpoints, response windows and reconciliation intervals in Appendix A.

## 6. Deviations

Unauthorised access, an unwitnessed or unlogged entry, an inventory-to-physical discrepancy, a missed alarm response, or a vessel failure is recorded and managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) using the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Any event in which stored material is lost, damaged or misidentified is escalated as an adverse event (OMK-POL-EMB-0004). An unresolved inventory discrepancy is a stop condition until reconciled.

## 7. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The access-authorisation list, the alarm setpoints and out-of-hours response window, the inventory-reconciliation interval, the contingency-test schedule and the spare-capacity requirement are **locally defined** and recorded in **Appendix A** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off). The requirements for secure storage, continuous monitoring, traceability and contingency are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022; ISO 15189).

## 9. Teaching point

Cryostored material is the one thing in the laboratory that cannot be remade: a single mislabelled cane or an unnoticed empty tank is irreversible. Security therefore has two faces — keeping the wrong people out, and keeping the right information exact. The inventory that always matches the physical contents, and the alarm that always reaches someone, are not bureaucracy; they are the difference between a tank of patients' futures and a tank of unknowns.
