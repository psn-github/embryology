#### Purpose

This SOP outlines the correct procedure for the insemination of oocytes via standard IVF. This SOP includes information on the number of sperm used for insemination, the pre-insemination procedure and the insemination procedure.

#### Scope

This SOP covers the insemination of oocytes by laboratory staff and is applicable to all laboratory staff working in any Oxford Medical Kuwait clinic who are deemed competent to perform IVF insemination.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

#### Definitions

IVF In Vitro Fertilisation

#### Procedure

##### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Requirements

Equipment/Consumables Required

-   Glass Pasteur pipette
-   Bung
-   Pipette tips
-   Pipette
-   Clinical waste bin

Dishes/Media Required

-   None, oocytes are already in 5 well dishes and the sperm stock is already in a conical tube ready for insemination.

##### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the insemination procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point.

##### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number, D.O.B)

Before beginning an IVF insemination check the laboratory notes to ensure that ‘IVF’ is the agreed insemination method and whether there are any ‘special instructions’, for example only a limited number of the oocytes are to be inseminated.

Check whether the patent has elected to have a 50:50 IVF:ICSI split, if so ensure only the designated number of oocytes are inseminated by IVF.



Approve these checks on the RI witness system and both operator and witness must sign for these checks.

At the end of the procedure check the correct number of oocytes/dishes have been inseminated.

##### 5.4 Number of Motile Sperm for Insemination

The standard number of motile sperm for insemination is 75,000/0.7ml well. If a patient has previously had a high number of 3PN’s or a low number of 2PN’s with IVF the insemination number can be increased or decreased accordingly for specific patient cases. **Note:** if the number of motile sperm used for insemination is other than 75,000 (as indicated as above) then the insemination concentration calculation must be adjusted accordingly.

The concentration and motility of the final sperm stock sample is used to calculate the number of microliters of sperm to add to each well.

The final sperm concentration is multiplied by the number of motile sperm (excluding grade C sperm) to give the total motile count (TMC). This figure is then cross referenced to the insemination chart to provide the volume of sperm that needs to be added to each well to give 75,000 motile sperm. See appendix 1 for the insemination chart.

**Example:**

Concentration = 12M/ml

Motility = 91% (A + B)

TMC Calculation: 12 x 0.91= 10.92

Insemination Volume: 75/10.92 = 6.87µl

##### 5.5 Timing of Insemination Procedure

The insemination procedure should be performed approximately 4 hours post oocyte collection ±30 minutes.

The sperm stock sample should be pre-warmed and gassed for 1 hour ±30 minutes prior to insemination.

##### 5.6 IVF Insemination Procedure

Consideration must be given to maintaining temperature, pH and osmolality of the dishes during the insemination procedure. As such, remove one or two 5 well oocyte collection dishes from the gassed incubator at a time and perform the procedure as quickly as possible.

Check the number of microliters of sperm that must be added to each well and set the pipette volume accordingly.

Remove the sperm sample from the incubator and mix thoroughly using a sterile glass Pasteur.

Remove up to two 5 well oocyte collection dishes from the incubator and place on the RI heated stage.

Perform the witnessing checks as described above.

Inseminate each well containing oocytes with the calculated volume of sperm stock solution.

Observe the sperm within the well to check an appropriate number appear to be present and they appear actively motile. Once checked, place a ‘tick’ on the lid of the dish so it is clear which dishes have been inseminated and return the dishes to the incubator.

Repeat the procedure as described above until all the oocytes have been inseminated.

Clear the flowhood of any used consumables. Use Oosafe spray ± sterile water to clean the flowhood surface and wipe the pipette shaft to prevent any cross contamination of sperm samples.

Complete the embryology & witnessing records.

#### Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

2PN ≥60%

1PN <5%

3PN <6%

Failed fertilisation <5%

#### Monitoring

A Oxford Medical Kuwait laboratory manager is responsible for auditing this SOP annually.

2PN, 3PN, 1PN and failed fertilisation rates are all reviewed every 4 months at the KPI meeting. The Vienna consensus values are used to assess competency and benchmark values.
