## 1. Purpose

To define how reusable glassware is cleaned, sterilised and verified before it is used in the laboratory, so that no detergent residue, particulate or microbial contamination reaches gametes, embryos or media. Glassware that contacts embryo culture must be not only sterile but free of embryotoxic residue.

## 2. Scope

Applies to reusable laboratory glassware used in andrology and embryology preparation. Single-use embryo-contacting consumables are purchased sterile and embryo-tested and are not reprocessed. Applies to autologous workflows only; donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

> **Default position.** Embryo-contacting vessels are single-use, sterile and embryo-tested wherever possible. Reusable glassware is reserved for non-embryo-contacting or validated uses; any glassware intended for embryo contact must be confirmed embryo-safe after reprocessing.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing staff** — clean, package and sterilise glassware per this SOP and record the cycle.
- **Laboratory Lead** — approves which glassware may be reused for which purpose and confirms embryo-safety where relevant.
- **Quality Manager** — audits cycle records and reviews any sterilisation failure.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Decontamination | Cleaning to remove soil and residue prior to sterilisation |
| Dry-heat sterilisation | Sterilisation by hot air at high temperature for a validated time |
| Moist-heat sterilisation | Sterilisation by saturated steam under pressure (autoclave) |
| Chemical indicator | Indicator that changes on exposure to sterilising conditions |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Embryo-safe, residue-free detergent and high-purity (e.g. WFI-grade) rinse water.
- Dry-heat oven and/or autoclave (ELB-SOP-012) with calibrated controls.
- Sterilisation packaging/wrap and chemical/biological indicators.
- Labels for contents, cycle date and expiry (ELB-SOP-016).

> **IFU cross-reference.** Detergents, sterilisers and indicators are used per their IFU and the cycle parameters in **Appendix A**. Use only detergents validated as fully rinsable and non-embryotoxic for embryo-contacting glassware.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the steriliser passed its routine verification (ELB-SOP-012) and is in calibration (ELB-LOG-020).
2. Confirm detergent, rinse water and indicators are in date.
3. Confirm air-quality conditions for cooling/storage are acceptable (ELB-SOP-013).

## 7. Procedure

1. Pre-rinse glassware to remove gross soil immediately after use.
2. Wash with embryo-safe detergent; scrub as needed.
3. Rinse thoroughly, finishing with high-purity water, until no detergent remains.
4. Dry and inspect for chips, cracks or residue; discard damaged items.
5. Package with an appropriate chemical indicator.
6. Sterilise by the validated cycle for that glassware (dry-heat or autoclave) per **Appendix A** 🔧 and ELB-SOP-012.
7. Confirm the chemical indicator changed correctly; record the cycle and indicator result.
8. Cool, store sealed, and label with contents and cycle/expiry date.
9. For embryo-contacting glassware, confirm the embryo-safety status defined by **Appendix A** 🔧 before release.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Sterilised glassware correctly labelled (contents, cycle, expiry) before storage | Second-person check (ELB-SOP-002) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Detergent residue is embryotoxic and invisible. Do not release embryo-contacting glassware on a passed sterilisation cycle alone — confirm the rinse and embryo-safety status defined in Appendix A.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept:** thoroughly rinsed, undamaged, correctly sterilised glassware with a conforming chemical indicator, labelled and within assigned expiry.
- **Reject:** chips/cracks, visible residue, failed/ambiguous indicator, expired packaging, or unverified embryo-safety where embryo contact is intended.

## 10. Deviations

Failed cycles, residue findings and damaged-glassware use are handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Sterilisation cycle record (load, cycle, indicator result) — per ELB-SOP-012.
- Calibration records for the steriliser — **ELB-LOG-020**.
- Any deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Cycle parameters (temperature/time for dry-heat and autoclave) 🔧, assigned shelf life of sterilised glassware 🔧 and the embryo-safety verification requirement 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The requirement for residue-free, sterile embryo-contacting surfaces is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015); process control aligns with ISO 15189.

## 13. References

- ESHRE. *Revised guidelines for good practice in IVF laboratories (2015).*
- ISO 15189 — requirements for quality and competence in medical laboratories.

## 14. Teaching point

For embryo culture, "clean" beats "sterile" when forced to choose: a sterile vessel with detergent film will still kill embryos. The rinse is the step that protects the embryo; the autoclave only protects against organisms. Verify both — and prefer single-use, embryo-tested consumables wherever you can.
