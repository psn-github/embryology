## 1. Purpose

To define how air quality in the Embryology Laboratory — particulates and volatile organic compounds (VOC/TVOC) — is controlled and monitored, so that the culture environment is kept within limits that protect gamete and embryo viability. Embryos are exposed to laboratory air at every open step; air quality is a primary culture parameter, not a background condition.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the embryology and andrology working areas, laminar flow hoods, workstations and incubator intake air. Covers particulate air classification, VOC/TVOC monitoring, and the response to excursions. Applies to autologous workflows only; donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Embryologists on shift** — read and record air-quality/TVOC values at the defined intervals and act on excursions.
- **Laboratory Lead** — maintains the air-handling and filtration regime and the monitoring schedule.
- **Quality Manager** — trends the data, manages calibration of the monitor (ELB-LOG-020) and reviews excursions.
- **Medical Director** — signs off the locally defined TVOC ranges in Appendix A.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| VOC | Volatile organic compound |
| TVOC | Total VOC — summed concentration of volatile organics in air |
| HEPA / activated-carbon filtration | Particulate / VOC removal stages in the air-handling chain |
| CODA / in-line filtration | Point-of-use VOC reduction for incubators/hoods |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Air-handling system with particulate (HEPA) and VOC (activated carbon) filtration; point-of-use VOC filtration on incubators/hoods where fitted.
- Calibrated VOC/TVOC monitor and, where used, a particle counter (calibration tracked in ELB-LOG-020).
- Air quality / TVOC log (ELB-LOG-022).

> **IFU cross-reference.** Filters and monitors are operated and replaced per their IFU and the schedule in **Appendix A**. Use only embryo-safe cleaning and construction materials in the air-controlled space (ELB-SOP-014).

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the air-handling system is running and within its operating parameters.
2. Confirm the VOC/TVOC monitor is powered, in calibration (ELB-LOG-020) and reading.
3. Confirm no recent VOC-introducing activity (painting, solvent use, new plastics off-gassing) is uncontrolled in or near the lab.

## 7. Procedure

1. At opening, read and record the TVOC value and particulate status (ELB-SOP-008, ELB-LOG-022).
2. Repeat at the locally defined in-day intervals 🔧 (**Appendix A**) and on any event likely to raise VOCs.
3. Compare each reading against the acceptable range and alert level defined in **Appendix A** 🔧.
4. If a reading exceeds the alert level, increase ventilation/filtration, identify and remove the source, protect open culture (close hoods, defer non-urgent open work), and re-measure.
5. Record the value, any action taken and the re-measured value.
6. Track filter life and replace particulate/VOC filters per the schedule in **Appendix A** 🔧.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

This SOP generates environmental records rather than patient-identity events; no per-step gamete witnessing applies. The decision to defer or continue open culture during an excursion is recorded and countersigned by the Laboratory Lead.

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A TVOC excursion above the alert level is a stop-and-assess condition for new open culture. Protect material already in culture, find the source, and do not resume open work until the reading returns within range.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

The environment is acceptable for open culture when TVOC is within the range in **Appendix A** 🔧 and particulate classification is maintained. A reading above the alert level is a reject condition for new open work until corrected and re-verified.

## 10. Deviations

Excursions, monitor failures and filtration faults are handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Sustained excursions affecting material in culture are escalated.

## 11. Records generated

- Air quality / TVOC log — **ELB-LOG-022**.
- Monitor calibration entries — **ELB-LOG-020**.
- Any deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The TVOC acceptable range and alert level 🔧, monitoring intervals 🔧 and filter-replacement schedule 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The principle of controlled, VOC-reduced air for IVF laboratories is **guideline-anchored to ESHRE 2015 air-quality guidance**; facility control aligns with ISO 15189. Absolute TVOC limits are not fixed by guideline and so are set locally — see Appendix A.

## 13. References

- ESHRE. *Revised guidelines for good practice in IVF laboratories (2015)* — air quality and VOC control.
- ISO 15189 — requirements for quality and competence in medical laboratories (facility/environmental control).

## 14. Teaching point

ESHRE sets the expectation — clean, VOC-reduced air — but not a single universal number, because acceptable TVOC depends on the building, the filtration and the construction materials. That is why this SOP points every limit to Appendix A: the guideline anchors the principle, the local validation anchors the value. Watch the trend, not just the spot reading; a slow rise usually means a new source off-gassing before any single value alarms.
