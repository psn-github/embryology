#### Purpose


#### Scope

Applicable to all embryologists and practitioners deemed competent to perform warming of vitrified of oocytes:

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

#### Procedure

##### 5.1 Equipment and Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   Serological pipettes
-   Rapid I Liquid Nitrogen Box
-   Liquid Nitrogen
-   Pre pulled Pipettes (145-190µm)
-   Timers
-   Straw Cutter/Forceps

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   5 well dishes
-   40mm Culture Dish
-   Rapid Warm Oocyte Media
-   G1+ Post Denudation Dish
-   Oil
-   RI Witness Tags

##### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to performing the warming procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point. Warming is performed at 37◦C.

Fill the Rapid I Cryobox and a dilvac with liquid nitrogen. Ensure appropriate PPE is used when handling liquid nitrogen.

##### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B)

24-48 hours prior to the scheduled thaw date, check the patients thaw has been scheduled on the correct date in their cycle (post LH surge or start of progesterone) and call the patient to check/confirm the following:

-   Confirm number of oocytes in storage
-   Confirm the day of the thaw and planned day of embryo transfer
-   Ask the patient to positively confirm how many oocytes they wish to thaw and how many embryos they wish to transfer. Cross reference this to the information in the consent form
-   If the patient wishes to have DET but meets eSET, explain the local eSET policy
-   If patient is using HRT/progesterone, check the medication has been taken in line with the patient’s protocol
-   Confirm which add-ons will be used i.e TL/Glue/AH/PGT
-   Check the patient is fit and well and is able to proceed with the embryo transfer

Record these checks in a progress note or in the laboratory notes.

On the day of warming, perform local checks to ensure that the warming is being conducted on the correct date, the pre thaw call has been conducted/recorded and the planned thaw is going ahead, and that no messages via progress/portal have been received from the patient/clinical team informing of a cancellation.

Check the patients consent form to determine the number of oocytes to warm. If more than one batch of oocytes have been vitrified, check the correct batch is being warmed.

Ensure there is a viable sperm sample available to inject the oocytes with.

Oocyte warming requires a second witness to be used alongside RI witness.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the Visi/Cassette, Rapid I straws and warming dishes match that in the laboratory notes and RI witness system. Also check the freeze date on the Rapid I straws and the number of oocytes contained within each Rapid I straw matches that in the laboratory records. Approve these checks on the RI witness system and both operator and witness must sign for these checks.


Post thaw the warmed oocytes are moved into a G1+ Post Denudation Dish alongside RI witness alone. Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the warming dish matches the G1+ Post Denudation dish, laboratory notes and RI witness system.

##### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare G1+ Post Denudation dish the afternoon before the patient’s oocyte warm and equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight. Prepare one dish per patient.

Prepare the oocyte warming dishes 30-60 minutes prior to use (validate time period required locally) and allow to warm in an ungassed incubator to 37◦C Prepare one set of oocyte warming dishes per 3 Rapid I’s to be warmed.

Gently invert the Rapid Warm Oocyte solutions 3 times prior to making the dishes. Place 0.8ml of Warm1, 0.8ml of Warm2, 0.8ml of Warm3 and 0.8ml of Warm4 in separate wells of a 5 well dish. Overlay Warm2, 3 and 4 with 0.3ml of oil.

Warm4 + Oil Warm3 + Oil

##### 5.5 Timing of Warming Procedure

Perform warming 3 hours ±30 minutes prior to the ICSI procedure.

##### 5.6 Warming Exposure Times

Vitrolife Rapid Warm Oocyte

Warm1: Exposure time 1 minute

Warm2: Exposure time 3 minutes

Warm3: Exposure time 5 minutes

Warm4: Exposure time 5 minutes

##### 5.7 Procedure

Maintenance of a temperature of 37◦C is crucial during oocyte warming. Temperature can be maintained either using a heated stage or an ungassed G85/incubator.

Perform the witnessing checks to ensure the correct sample has been selected for warming. Transfer the Rapid I’s to the nitrogen filled cryobox. Move the cryobox close to the microscope. The speed of warming is crucial to oocyte survival.

Using the straw cutter, cut the top of the Rapid I straw. Place the warming dish under the microscope with its lid off. Working as quickly as possible, remove the Rapid I from the straw and plunge directly into Warm1. Immediately start the timer for 1 minute and place the oocytes in the centre of the dish.

Priming the pipette with the solution that the oocytes are currently in, expose the oocytes to the following warming regime:

Warm2 for 3 minutes.

Warm3 for 5 minutes.

Warm4 for 5 minutes.

Following warming, transfer the oocytes to the G1+ Post Denudation dish alongside RI witness.

Wash the oocytes through 3 droplets of G1+ before then separating into droplets for culture.

Culture a maximum of 4 oocytes in each drop. Return the G1+ Post Denudation dish to a gassed incubator.

Repeat the procedure above until all the oocytes for thaw have been warmed and transferred to the G1+ Post Denudation dish. Mark the wash drops by crossing through with a line under the dish to prevent further use. Note on the lid of the dish the total number of oocytes contained within.

Return the G1\+ post denudation dish to a gassed incubator until ICSI.

Complete the laboratory and witnessing records.

#### Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

Oocyte Survival ≥85%
