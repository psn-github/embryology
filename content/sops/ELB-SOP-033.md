## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for cryopreserving embryos by vitrification — ultra-rapid cooling in the presence of high-concentration cryoprotectant so that the cell solidifies into a glass-like state without ice-crystal formation — so that supernumerary and elective embryos are stored with maximal post-warming viability and full traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the vitrification of cleavage-stage and blastocyst-stage embryos derived from the **patient couple's own gametes** (autologous). Slow-freezing is not performed. Oocyte vitrification is covered separately (ELB-SOP-034) and warming is covered separately (ELB-SOP-035). Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope and is not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — must be trained and signed off as competent in vitrification (ELB-SOP-005) before working unsupervised. Responsible for the procedure, the witnessing checkpoints, labelling, placement and the records generated.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and specimen identity at every labelling, transfer and placement step (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms consent and storage authority is current (ELB-POL-009), reviews survival outcomes and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Vitrification | Solidification into an amorphous glass-like state by ultra-rapid cooling, avoiding ice crystals |
| CPA | Cryoprotectant agent (permeating, e.g. ethylene glycol / DMSO, and non-permeating, e.g. sucrose/trehalose) |
| ES | Equilibration solution (lower CPA concentration) |
| VS | Vitrification solution (high CPA concentration) |
| Carrier / device | The labelled support (e.g. open or closed device) onto which the embryo is loaded for plunging |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Validated commercial vitrification kit (ES, VS, wash/dilution solutions) used strictly within shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Vitrification carrier devices and protective storage sleeves/goblets, single-use and sterile.
- Stereomicroscope with heated stage; calibrated timer; fine handling pipettes.
- Liquid nitrogen (LN₂), filling vessel and the designated storage tank (ELB-SOP-036, ELB-SOP-037).
- Cryo personal protective equipment (face shield, cryo gloves, closed footwear, apron); LN₂ area oxygen monitoring (ELB-SOP-037).
- Cold-chain identifiers: cryo-resistant labels, canes/visotubes/goblets and the RI Witness reader.

> **IFU cross-reference.** The CPA concentrations, the sequence and the exposure time at each step are defined by the **validated kit IFU** and ESHRE 2015. Do not deviate from the IFU timings; they are the procedure. Do not mix lots or kits within a single embryo's vitrification run.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm valid, signed cryopreservation and storage consent is on file for the couple (ELB-POL-009, ELB-FORM-001).
2. Confirm the embryos selected for vitrification, their grade and stage against the culture record, and that the warming/transfer plan is agreed.
3. Confirm the kit is within shelf life, brought to the working temperature stated in the IFU, and that timers are set.
4. Confirm carrier devices, storage goblets/canes and labels are pre-labelled with the couple's identifiers and verified (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm tank space, the assigned position in the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and adequate LN₂ level (ELB-SOP-037).

> **CRITICAL — identity before you begin.** Only one couple's embryos are open at a workstation at any time. Both the embryo dish and the carrier device pass an electronic-witness identity match before loading (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm the embryo dish and the pre-labelled carrier at the workstation; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. (Blastocyst only) Where artificial collapse before vitrification is used, perform it per the kit IFU and Appendix A 🔧.
3. Move the embryo through the **equilibration solution (ES)** for the exposure time specified in the kit IFU, observing for the expected shrink-and-re-expansion behaviour.
4. Transfer the embryo into the **vitrification solution (VS)**; start the timer. Keep total VS exposure strictly within the IFU window.
5. Load the embryo onto the labelled carrier in the minimal volume specified by the IFU.
6. **Plunge** the loaded carrier directly into LN₂ (open device) or seal and cool per the IFU (closed device) without delay.
7. Confirm the carrier identity again, then place it into its labelled goblet/cane and record the exact tank, canister, cane and position; perform **EW match** (checkpoint W2).
8. Transfer to the assigned storage position in the designated tank under LN₂; confirm immersion/phase per tank type (ELB-SOP-036).
9. Record device type, number of embryos per device, stage, grade, kit lot, times and storage location on the embryo vitrification record (ELB-LOG-013) and update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where embryos are identified, loaded, labelled or placed into storage (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Embryo dish and carrier device belong to the same couple before loading | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Loaded carrier matches its goblet/cane and the recorded position | EW match + second-person |
| **W3** | Storage location entered in inventory matches the physical placement | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Time-critical CPA exposure does not justify proceeding on assumption — if identity cannot be confirmed, complete cooling to protect the embryo, isolate it under quarantine labelling, and resolve identity before any inventory entry. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Vitrify:** viable embryos meeting the locally agreed grade-for-freeze criteria 🔧 (Appendix A), with current consent.
- **Do not vitrify:** embryos below the agreed grade threshold, or where consent is absent, expired or non-matching — escalate (ELB-POL-009).
- A carrier is rejected if labelling is illegible, damaged or unverifiable; re-label and re-witness before use.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP — CPA exposure outside the IFU window, EW downtime, carrier failure, mis-placement, or a tank/LN₂ issue during the run — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Significant clinical events follow the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- Embryo vitrification record — **ELB-LOG-013**.
- Cryostorage inventory update (tank/canister/cane/position) — **ELB-LOG-016**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The CPA sequence and exposure times are **guideline-anchored** to the validated kit IFU and ESHRE 2015. Values tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)** include: artificial-collapse policy for blastocysts 🔧, grade-for-freeze criteria 🔧, number of embryos per device 🔧, the post-warming embryo survival threshold and re-expansion criteria 🔧 (applied at warming, ELB-SOP-035), and the maximum storage period 🔧. Each is flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requires Medical Director sign-off.

## 14. Teaching point

Vitrification is a race against time and a defence against ice. Once the embryo enters the vitrification solution the clock is the procedure: the IFU window exists because the cell is dehydrating in a toxic, hyperosmotic medium and must reach LN₂ before either ice or CPA toxicity does harm. Pre-label and pre-witness everything so that no identity step ever competes with that clock, and record the storage position as you place it — an embryo you cannot find later is, for the couple, an embryo that was lost.
