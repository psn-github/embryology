## 1. Purpose

To define a single, standardised scheme for assessing and grading oocytes, zygotes and embryos at fixed developmental time points, so that morphological assessment is consistent between embryologists and over time, anchored to the **Istanbul Consensus**, and recorded with full traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the morphological assessment of all oocytes, zygotes and embryos derived from the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only), from maturity assessment through cleavage to the blastocyst stage. Provides the grading nomenclature used in selection for transfer (ELB-SOP-032) and cryopreservation. Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — assesses at the defined time points, assigns grades to the Istanbul scheme, and is responsible for the records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent in grading (ELB-SOP-005), including periodic concordance checks.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity at each assessment/transfer point (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — arbitrates borderline grades, monitors inter-observer concordance and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| MII / MI / GV | Oocyte maturity stages (per ELB-SOP-026) |
| PN | Pronuclei (zygote assessment; 2PN normal — ELB-SOP-028) |
| ICM | Inner cell mass (blastocyst) |
| TE | Trophectoderm (blastocyst) |
| Fragmentation | Proportion of anucleate cytoplasmic fragments at cleavage |
| Compaction | Cell–cell adherence preceding blastulation (morula) |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted microscope with Hoffman/relief-contrast optics and heated stage; calibrated graticule or imaging system for size/expansion assessment.
- Time-lapse incubator/imaging where in use, otherwise tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- Pre-equilibrated culture dishes for return to incubation — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** All media, oil and consumables are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and that each cohort is assessed at the Istanbul-defined time points (Section 7; **Appendix A**).
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); minimise time outside the incubator per batch 🔧.
3. Confirm dishes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016).
4. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

## 7. Procedure

Assess at the **Istanbul Consensus time points** (hours post-insemination/injection; exact local hours in **Appendix A**), recording grade and time of assessment at each.

1. Confirm patient dishes at the workstation; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. **Oocyte (post-denudation):** classify maturity — MII, MI, GV — and note cytoplasmic and extracytoplasmic features (e.g. vacuoles, refractile bodies, polar-body morphology). Cross-reference ELB-SOP-026.
3. **Zygote (≈16–18 h):** record pronuclear number (2PN normal — ELB-SOP-028) and, where used, pronuclear/nucleolar pattern and alignment.
4. **Cleavage (Day 2 ≈ 44 ± 1 h; Day 3 ≈ 68 ± 1 h):** record **cell number**, **blastomere size evenness**, **degree of fragmentation** and presence of **multinucleation**, against the expected cell number for the time point.
5. **Morula (Day 4):** record degree of **compaction**.
6. **Blastocyst (Day 5 ≈ 116 ± 2 h; Day 6/7 as applicable):** grade by **expansion stage** (early → expanded → hatching/hatched), **inner cell mass (ICM)** and **trophectoderm (TE)**, each on the Istanbul grade categories.
7. Assign the composite grade for the stage; flag any embryo for review at borderline grades.
8. Perform an **EW match** before returning dishes to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
9. Record stage, time point, all grade components and operator on the embryo culture & development record (**ELB-LOG-009**).

The Istanbul grade categories applied at each stage:

| Stage | Time point (post-insem./inj.) | Graded features | Grade scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oocyte | Post-denudation | Maturity; cytoplasmic/extracytoplasmic features | MII / MI / GV (+ notes) |
| Zygote | ≈ 16–18 h | PN number; PN/nucleolar pattern | 2PN normal; abnormal flagged |
| Cleavage (D2) | ≈ 44 ± 1 h | Cell number; evenness; fragmentation; multinucleation | Good / Fair / Poor (Istanbul descriptors) |
| Cleavage (D3) | ≈ 68 ± 1 h | As D2, against expected cell number | Good / Fair / Poor |
| Morula (D4) | ≈ 92 ± 2 h | Compaction | Compacting / compacted / partial |
| Blastocyst (D5–7) | D5 ≈ 116 ± 2 h | Expansion; ICM; TE | Expansion stage + ICM/TE category |

> **CRITICAL — grade at the right time.** Morphology is meaningful only at the Istanbul time point. Assessing too early or too late changes the apparent grade; record the actual assessment time, not the nominal day.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where embryos are identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of dishes presented for grading vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Embryos returned to the correctly labelled dish/position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling and grade record vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- Grading classifies; it does not by itself accept or reject an embryo. Selection thresholds for transfer and cryopreservation are applied under ELB-SOP-032 and the cryopreservation SOPs.
- Embryos arrested or degenerate at a time point are recorded as such and excluded from use.
- Inter-observer grade discordance beyond the local concordance tolerance 🔧 (**Appendix A**) triggers re-assessment and a competency review.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. assessment outside the defined time window, EW downtime, persistent inter-observer discordance) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Embryo culture & development record (all grades, all time points) — **ELB-LOG-009**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Grading-relevant entries include: the morphology grading scheme (Istanbul Consensus criteria), the assessment time points in hours post-insemination/injection (Istanbul-defined), the maximum embryo time outside the incubator per batch 🔧, and the local inter-observer concordance tolerance 🔧. Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (Istanbul Consensus / ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Grading is only as useful as it is consistent, and consistency comes from two habits: assess at the Istanbul time point, and describe what is actually seen against fixed criteria rather than against a remembered impression. Cell number for the day, evenness, fragmentation and — at blastocyst — expansion, ICM and trophectoderm are the load-bearing features. Periodic concordance checks between embryologists are what keep a grade meaning the same thing tomorrow as it does today.
