## 1. Purpose

To define the routine operation, monitoring and maintenance of CO₂ (and tri-gas) incubators so that gametes and embryos are held in a stable, in-specification environment of temperature, CO₂ and — where applicable — reduced O₂. This SOP covers running an incubator day to day; the independent verification checks are covered in OMK-SOP-EMB-0062.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all CO₂ and tri-gas incubators used to culture or hold the patient couple's own gametes and embryos (autologous only) in the Embryology Laboratory. Covers setpoints, loading, door discipline, gas supply, alarms, cleaning and recovery from interruption. Independent QC checks (OMK-SOP-EMB-0062), gas-analyser use (OMK-SOP-EMB-0063) and pH confirmation (OMK-SOP-EMB-0065) are detailed separately. Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Embryologist on duty** — operates the incubator per this SOP, observes door discipline, responds to alarms, and records routine checks (OMK-LOG-EMB-0061).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — manages loading allocation, authorises return to use after a fault, reviews trends.
- **Quality Manager** — schedules servicing, gas-supply maintenance and verification of the monitoring/alarm system, and maintains calibration status of integral sensors (OMK-LOG-EMB-0063).

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Setpoint | Programmed target value (temperature, CO₂, O₂) |
| Tri-gas incubator | Incubator controlling CO₂ and O₂ (typically reduced O₂) as well as temperature |
| Recovery time | Time for a chamber to return to setpoint after a door opening |
| Alarm threshold | The deviation at which the incubator/monitoring system raises an alert 🔧 (Appendix A) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- CO₂ / tri-gas incubator with integral temperature, CO₂ (± O₂) control and door seals.
- Medical-grade CO₂ (and N₂ for tri-gas) supply with regulators and an automatic change-over/reserve where fitted.
- Humidification reservoir or system per the incubator design.
- Continuous monitoring/alarm system; incubator QC log — OMK-LOG-EMB-0061.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Operate, humidify, clean and service the incubator strictly per its IFU. Use only cleaning agents and methods validated as embryo-safe and approved for the device; never introduce a cleaning residue or fragrance into a culture chamber.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm temperature is at **37.0 °C** (ESHRE 2015) and CO₂ is at the setpoint that achieves media pH **7.2–7.4**; confirm O₂ setpoint for tri-gas (reduced O₂, ESHRE 2015).
2. Confirm the daily independent QC check is in range (OMK-SOP-EMB-0062, OMK-LOG-EMB-0061).
3. Confirm gas supply pressure and reserve are adequate and the monitoring/alarm system is armed.
4. Confirm humidification (where used) is maintained per IFU.

## 7. Procedure

**Daily operation**

1. Pre-equilibrate culture dishes for the time and conditions in the media IFU and Appendix A 🔧 before introducing gametes/embryos.
2. Load dishes into assigned, labelled chamber positions; keep each patient's material grouped and traceable.
3. Minimise door openings: plan handling, open the smallest chamber/inner door needed, and close promptly to limit recovery time.
4. Allow the chamber the IFU recovery time to return to setpoint after each opening before relying on the conditions.

**Gas supply**

5. Monitor cylinder/supply pressure; change cylinders before exhaustion per a retired/absorbed document, and reconfirm CO₂ and pH after any change (OMK-SOP-EMB-0063, OMK-SOP-EMB-0065).

**Maintenance & cleaning**

6. Clean and decontaminate at the scheduled interval per IFU using approved embryo-safe methods; refill/replace humidification per IFU.
7. After cleaning, allow full re-equilibration and confirm temperature, CO₂ and pH are back in range before reloading.

**Alarms & interruption**

8. Respond to any alarm immediately; identify the cause (gas, temperature, door, power) and protect culture material — relocate to a confirmed in-range chamber where clinically appropriate.
9. After a power or supply interruption, do not rely on the chamber until it has re-stabilised and an independent check confirms range (OMK-SOP-EMB-0062).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Routine incubator operation does not require a second-person identity witness, but loading/relocation of identified material follows identity and labelling controls (OMK-SOP-EMB-0007), and routine checks are **signed and timed** (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Do not load or continue to culture in a chamber that is out of range, has an unresolved alarm, or has not recovered after disturbance. Two patients' dishes are never placed so that a labelling mix-up is possible; loading follows the identification SOP (OMK-SOP-EMB-0007).

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Temperature, CO₂ (± O₂) at setpoint before loading | Display + daily QC (OMK-SOP-EMB-0062) |
| **C2** | Media pH confirmed at 7.2–7.4 | pH measurement (OMK-SOP-EMB-0065) |
| **C3** | Chamber recovered to setpoint after disturbance | Recovery time + independent check |

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** a chamber for culture when temperature, CO₂ (± O₂) and confirmed media pH are within range and any alarm is cleared.
- **Reject** a chamber that is out of range, has an active/unresolved alarm, has inadequate gas supply, or has not re-equilibrated after cleaning or interruption.

## 10. Deviations

An out-of-range condition, alarm event, gas-supply failure or power interruption affecting an incubator is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and recorded in the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Where culture material may have been exposed to out-of-spec conditions, escalate as a potential adverse event (OMK-POL-EMB-0004), document the exposure window and the protective action taken.

## 11. Records generated

- Incubator QC / operation log (routine checks, alarms, cleaning, gas changes) — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0061**.
- Calibration status of integral sensors — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0063**.
- Any deviation entry — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Temperature **37.0 °C**, CO₂ **set to achieve media pH 7.2–7.4**, and **reduced O₂** for embryo culture are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015). The **alarm thresholds/tolerances**, dish **equilibration time**, cleaning **interval** and gas reserve/change-over policy are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in **Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A)**). Sensor calibration traceability follows ISO 15189.

## 14. Teaching point

An incubator's job is to be boringly stable, and the embryologist's job is to disturb it as little as possible. Every door opening trades convenience for a recovery period during which conditions are not what the display claims. Plan handling to open seldom and briefly, confirm recovery before trusting a chamber, and remember that CO₂ is judged ultimately by the pH it produces — not by the percentage on the panel.
