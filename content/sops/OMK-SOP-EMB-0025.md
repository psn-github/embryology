#### Purpose

To describe the procedure for identifying and categorising the signs of fertilisation and stages of oocyte maturity. This includes the consumables/equipment required, laboratory witnessing steps, the denudation procedure and the culture of fertilised oocytes following fertilisation check.

#### Scope

Applicable to all laboratory staff deemed competent to perform fertilisation checks.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

#### Definitions

PN Pro Nucleus

PB Polar Body

TL Time Lapse

ES+ EmbryoScope Plus

ICSI Intra cytoplasmic Sperm Injection

#### Procedure

##### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   Pre pulled Pipettes (IVF 134-170µm)
-   Marker Pen
-   Needles for dissection (optional)

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   ES+ GTL dish
-   Oil
-   RI Witness Tags

##### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the fertilisation check procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage and inverted microscopes are switched on and at the correct set point.

##### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B.)

Prior to commencing the fertilisation check determine whether all the oocytes have been inseminated via IVF or ICSI or a combination of both. **If both have been performed, IVF and ICSI inseminated oocytes must be cultured separately and clearly identified.**


Witnessing for fertilisation check can be performed alongside RI witness.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the IVF Insemination dishes match that on the ES+ GTL dish, laboratory notes and RI witness system.

Approve these checks on the RI witness system and sign for these checks.

##### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

For IVF cases, prepare the ES+ GTL dish the afternoon of Day 0 and equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight. Prepare one ES+ dish per 16 oocytes.

For ICSI cases, as the injected oocytes were placed in an ES+ GTL dish post ICSI, no dish preparation is required at this stage.

##### 5.5 Timing of Fertilisation Procedure

Perform fertilisation check 15-18 hours post insemination ± 30 minutes. Where TL has been used post ICSI, the fertilisation check can be deferred but care must be taken to ensure that the injected oocytes are clearly visible i.e. there are no bubbles obscuring the view of the oocyte, the dish is not misaligned or that the oocyte is not at the periphery of the well, meaning it cannot be easily viewed.

##### 5.6 Fertilisation Check Procedure

Consideration must be given to maintaining temperature, pH and osmolality during the procedure; therefore oil covered dishes are used. The procedure should be performed as quickly as practically possible to minimise the exposure of oocytes outside the incubator environment.

For ICSI cases the fertilisation check can be performed whilst the oocytes are within the ES+ incubator. Use the EmbryoViewer to observe the pronuclear status of the oocytes.

**IVF Fertilisation Check**

Remove one 5 well dish from the incubator at a time. Using a pre pulled 134-170µm pipette, gently aspirate each oocyte up and down until all the surrounding cells have been removed. Repeat for all oocytes within the dish.

If it is not possible to remove the surrounding cells by pipetting, needles can be used to dissect away the cells. Care must be taken not to damage the oocyte.

Transfer denuded oocytes into the centre well of the 5 well dish.

Observe the oocytes for the presence of pro-nuclei/polar bodies and their maturity status under high power magnification. Roll each oocyte 360◦ in order to bring any overlapping pro nuclei into view.

Leave those with 2PN’s in the centre well and move all other oocytes into well 2. Mark on the lid above the centre well the number of 2PN’s and mark above well 2 the status of the remaining oocytes. Return the dish to a gassed incubator.

Repeat until all dishes have been checked and all 2PN’s have been identified.

The ES+ dish will require an ES+ barcode label to be attached.

Alongside RI Witness, check the full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the 5 well GIVF Dishes, the ES+ GTL dish(es), laboratory notes and RI Witness system match.

Transfer the 2PN’s to the ES+ GTL dish (any 1PN’s that require further observation may also be transferred but must be clearly marked on the EmbryoViewer and laboratory notes).

Wash the 2PN’s through three wash wells to remove the GIVF+ and any residual sperm.

Place one 2PN in each culture well. **Mark the number of 2PN’s on the side of the dish.**

Record the number of normally/abnormally fertilised oocytes and the number and maturity status of unfertilised oocytes in the laboratory records.

Clear the flowhood of any used consumables. Use Oosafe ± sterile water to clean the flowhood surface.

Complete the laboratory notes & witnessing records.

**ICSI Fertilisation Check**

Use the EmbryoScope Viewer to play the video for each oocyte from time 0 to present time. Additionally, view each oocyte through several planes to check for small/faint/overlapping pro nuclei.

Determine the fertilisation status of each oocyte and annotate the PN status within the EmbryoViewer. Any abnormally fertilised/unfertilised/degenerate oocytes must be marked red denoting that they are not suitable for embryo transfer/freezing. Record the number of 2PN’s and the fertilisation/survival status of the remaining oocytes in the laboratory notes.

In the event that TL monitoring was not available post ICSI, remove the ES+ GTL dish from the incubator and view each injected oocyte at high power magnification on a stereo or inverted microscope. Observe the injected oocytes for the presence of pro-nuclei/polar bodies. Note the fertilisation status of each injected oocyte in the laboratory notes.

Return the ES+ GTL dish back to the incubator. If space is now available in an ES+ incubator, transfer the dish there for onward culture.

##### 5.7 Low/Failed Fertilisation

In the event of failed/low (<25%) fertilisation with IVF, observe the insemination dishes on an inverted microscope and report the % sperm survival, % of sperm with rapid progression and the degree of sperm binding to the zona pellucida. Return the dishes to the incubator and re-check for the signs of fertilisation 2-6 hours later. Any late emerging 2PN’s during this time frame can be considered for embryo transfer/cryopreservation in the absence of any other normally developing 2PN’s.

In the event of failed/low (<25%) fertilisation with ICSI, re-check for the signs of fertilisation 2-6 hours later. Any late emerging 2PN’s during this time frame can be considered for embryo transfer/cryopreservation in the absence of any other normally developing 2PN’s.
