## 1. Purpose

To define how a previously cryopreserved (autologous) sperm sample is identified, retrieved from storage, thawed and prepared for use in IUI, IVF or ICSI, so that the viable motile fraction is recovered with full traceability and the correct patient's straws are used.

## 2. Scope

Applies to thawing and preparation of sperm previously cryopreserved for the **male partner of the patient couple** (autologous), whether ejaculated or surgically retrieved at the time of freezing. Cryopreservation itself is covered in ELB-SOP-023; fresh ejaculated preparation in ELB-SOP-019; surgical sperm in ELB-SOP-021. Donor samples are out of scope and are not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist / andrology technologist** — trained and signed off (ELB-SOP-005); retrieves, thaws, prepares and records.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity and straw/vial identity at retrieval and at every transfer (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — authorises retrieval from storage, reviews post-thaw yield and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Straw / cryovial | The labelled device in which sperm is stored in liquid nitrogen |
| Post-thaw motility | Progressive motility of the thawed sample, compared with the recorded pre-freeze value (🔧 Appendix A) |
| Recovery rate | Proportion of pre-freeze motile sperm recovered after thaw and preparation |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Cryostorage inventory (**ELB-LOG-016**) and the cryopreservation record (**ELB-LOG-004**) for the patient's straws.
- LN₂ handling equipment and PPE (ELB-SOP-037); thawing surface at the temperature/time specified by the freezing-kit IFU.
- Density-gradient and wash/handling media within shelf life; sterile tubes, pipettes, dishes (lots recorded on **ELB-LOG-003**).
- Centrifuge; counting chamber; microscope with heated stage; RI Witness reader.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Thawing temperature and time, and the wash/dilution sequence, follow the cryoprotectant/freezing-kit IFU. Removing cryoprotectant by washing before use is mandatory where the IFU specifies it.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm a current order and consent for use of the stored sample, and confirm the storage period/consent are valid (ELB-POL-009, ELB-LOG-016).
2. Locate the correct straws/vials in the inventory; confirm patient identity and storage position before opening the tank.
3. Confirm the recorded **pre-freeze parameters** from **ELB-LOG-004** so post-thaw recovery can be judged.
4. Confirm the intended use (IUI/IVF/ICSI) and timing; confirm media are at working temperature.

> **CRITICAL — identity at retrieval.** Verify the patient against the straw label, the inventory position and the order, and complete an EW identity match, before removing any device from storage (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-017). Two patients' devices are never open together.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm identity at the tank; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1) and record the retrieval against the chain of custody (ELB-SOP-017).
2. Remove only the identified straw(s)/vial(s); keep all other devices below the LN₂ surface.
3. Thaw per the freezing-kit IFU (temperature and time recorded against Appendix A).
4. Transfer the thawed sample to a labelled tube; if the IFU requires cryoprotectant removal, dilute and wash by centrifugation per IFU.
5. Assess an aliquot for **post-thaw motility** and concentration; compare with the pre-freeze values and calculate recovery.
6. Prepare the sample for the intended use:
   - For IVF/ICSI or where further selection is needed, prepare by density-gradient or swim-up per ELB-SOP-019.
   - For IUI, finish per ELB-SOP-022.
7. Assess the final prepared fraction (concentration, progressive motility, volume; **TMSC**).
8. Label the prepared aliquot to the patient and place under correct conditions; perform an **EW match** before release (checkpoint W2). Record everything on **ELB-LOG-003** and the post-thaw outcome on **ELB-LOG-004**.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at retrieval and at every transfer (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient vs straw/vial label vs inventory position vs order at retrieval | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Prepared aliquot correctly labelled and released to the right cycle | EW match |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts retrieval/processing. Return the device to storage, resolve identity and re-witness before continuing. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- A thawed preparation is **acceptable** when **post-thaw motility** and final **TMSC** meet the locally defined threshold for the intended use (🔧 **Appendix A**).
- A **poor post-thaw recovery** is escalated to the senior embryologist and the clinician: consider thawing an additional straw, an alternative preparation method, or switching the planned use (e.g. ICSI rather than IUI). Record the decision.
- No recoverable motile sperm after thaw is escalated to the clinician before the cycle proceeds; remaining stored straws and consent are reviewed.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure — low post-thaw recovery, device damage, thaw-protocol error, EW downtime, or a discrepancy between inventory and straw label — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Sperm preparation worksheet — **ELB-LOG-003**.
- Sperm cryopreservation & post-thaw record — **ELB-LOG-004** (post-thaw motility and recovery).
- Custody / retrieval entries — **ELB-SOP-017**; inventory update — **ELB-LOG-016**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Semen reference values follow the current **WHO** manual. Preparation methods (density-gradient / swim-up) are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022). Thaw temperature/time follow the freezing-kit IFU. The **post-thaw motility acceptance threshold** is **locally defined** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

A frozen sample carries two risks the fresh one does not: the wrong straw, and a recovery that disappoints. The first is defeated by treating retrieval as an identity event with the same discipline as any gamete transfer — match the patient, the label and the inventory position before the tank is opened. The second is anticipated, not discovered: knowing the pre-freeze numbers before you thaw tells you whether the recovery is acceptable, and whether the planned use must change before the cycle commits.
