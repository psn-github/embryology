## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for performing intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) — the injection of a single spermatozoon into the cytoplasm of a mature (metaphase II) oocyte — so that fertilisation is achieved consistently, safely and with full traceability of every gamete handled.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all ICSI procedures performed in the Embryology Laboratory on oocytes retrieved from, and spermatozoa belonging to, the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only). Covers ejaculated, surgically retrieved and previously cryopreserved sperm used for ICSI. Conventional insemination is covered separately (ELB-SOP-025). Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — must be trained and signed off as competent in micromanipulation (ELB-SOP-005) before performing ICSI unsupervised. Responsible for the procedure, the witnessing checkpoints and the records generated.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and specimen identity at every critical transfer point (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — authorises trainees, reviews fertilisation outcomes and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| MII | Metaphase II — mature oocyte with extruded first polar body; the only stage suitable for ICSI |
| GV | Germinal vesicle — immature oocyte |
| MI | Metaphase I — immature oocyte, no polar body, no GV |
| PVP | Polyvinylpyrrolidone — viscous medium used to slow and immobilise sperm |
| 2PN | Two pronuclei — normal fertilisation marker (assessed under ELB-SOP-028) |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted microscope with Hoffman/relief-contrast optics and heated stage; micromanipulators with holding and injection pipettes.
- Holding pipette and ICSI injection pipette (single-use, sterile).
- Pre-equilibrated ICSI dish: central PVP droplet, peripheral droplets of buffered handling medium, under oil overlay.
- Buffered (HEPES/MOPS) handling medium and culture medium — used within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Hyaluronidase for denudation (procedure in ELB-SOP-026).
- Heated stage/warming blocks maintained to setpoint (**Appendix A**); RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** All media, oil and consumables are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and that denudation (ELB-SOP-026) and oocyte maturity assessment are complete.
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A) before removing dishes.
3. Confirm the injection dish was prepared and equilibrated for the time specified in Appendix A.
4. Verify sperm preparation is complete, labelled and matches the couple (ELB-SOP-019/020/021).
5. Confirm micromanipulator alignment, pipette patency and stage temperature.

> **CRITICAL — identity before you begin.** Do not bring oocytes and sperm to the same workstation until both have passed an electronic-witness identity match (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016). Two patients' gametes are never open at one workstation simultaneously.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm partner sperm and patient oocytes at the workstation; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Place a small aliquot of prepared sperm at the edge of the central PVP droplet.
3. Select a single morphologically normal, progressively motile spermatozoon. Immobilise it by drawing the injection pipette across the tail (mid/lower tail) to break the membrane.
4. Aspirate the immobilised sperm, tail-first, into the injection pipette.
5. Transfer one MII oocyte into a handling droplet; orient so the **polar body is at the 6 or 12 o'clock position** to avoid the meiotic spindle at the injection site (3 o'clock).
6. Hold the oocyte on the holding pipette; bring the injection pipette to the 3 o'clock position at the same focal plane.
7. Penetrate the zona and oolemma; confirm membrane breakage by gentle aspiration of a small amount of cytoplasm.
8. Deposit the spermatozoon with minimal PVP and minimal cytoplasm displacement; withdraw the pipette gently.
9. Release the oocyte. Repeat for each MII oocyte, working in batches small enough to limit time outside the incubator.
10. After the batch, transfer injected oocytes to fresh, labelled, equilibrated culture dish; perform **EW match** before returning to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
11. Record the number of oocytes injected, MII/MI/GV counts, sperm source and any oocyte degeneration on the ICSI record (ELB-LOG-008).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where gametes/embryos are identified, combined or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient oocytes and partner sperm belong to the same couple before injection | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Injected oocytes returned to the correctly labelled dish/position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Inject:** mature MII oocytes only.
- **Do not inject:** GV and MI oocytes (record; may be re-assessed for maturation per local policy — see Appendix A). Degenerated or grossly abnormal oocytes are recorded and not injected.
- A spermatozoon is acceptable for injection if it is the best available by motility and morphology; for severely compromised samples, selection criteria and any adjunct (e.g. viability testing) follow Appendix A.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. EW downtime, pipette failure mid-batch, unexpected oocyte lysis above the local threshold) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG, deviation). Significant clinical events follow the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- ICSI / injection record — **ELB-LOG-008**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**, initiated for the cohort.
- Fertilisation assessment (next day) — **ELB-SOP-028**.
- Any deviation entry — deviation log.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. ICSI-relevant entries include: workstation/stage temperature setpoint, injection-dish equilibration time, maximum oocyte time outside the incubator per batch, PVP use, the local oocyte-degeneration alert threshold, and policy on culturing/re-assessing immature (GV/MI) oocytes. Each is marked as **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / Istanbul Consensus) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

ICSI bypasses the natural barriers to fertilisation, so the embryologist *is* the quality filter: spindle-safe orientation (polar body at 6/12 o'clock), clean membrane breakage confirmed by cytoplasmic aspiration, and minimal PVP carry-over are what separate a clean injection from oocyte trauma. Identity discipline matters most here because two gamete sources meet for the first time — the witness checkpoint is not paperwork, it is the safeguard against the one error that cannot be undone.
