## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised methods for preparing a freshly ejaculated semen sample — recovering a motile, morphologically competent and capacitated population of spermatozoa, separated from seminal plasma, debris, leucocytes and non-motile cells — for use in conventional IVF insemination or ICSI.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the preparation of fresh ejaculated semen produced by the **male partner of the patient couple** (autologous) for IVF/ICSI. Preparation for IUI is covered in ELB-SOP-022; frozen samples in ELB-SOP-020; surgical/testicular sperm in ELB-SOP-021. Donor samples are out of scope and are not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist / andrology technologist** — trained and signed off in sperm preparation (ELB-SOP-005); performs the method, the witnessing checkpoints and the records.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity and label match at each transfer (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — selects the method for difficult samples and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| DGC | Density-gradient centrifugation — isolates motile sperm by sedimentation through density layers |
| Swim-up | Migration of motile sperm upward into an overlaid medium |
| Capacitation | Physiological maturation enabling fertilisation, supported by preparation medium |
| TMSC | Total motile sperm count — the yield metric recorded after preparation |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Class II laminar flow hood (ELB-SOP-044); centrifuge with calibrated speed; heated surfaces at the working temperature setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- Density-gradient medium and sperm-washing/handling medium, used within shelf life with lot numbers recorded (**ELB-LOG-003**).
- Sterile conical tubes, pipettes and culture dishes; counting chamber.
- Inverted/phase-contrast microscope with heated stage; RI Witness reader at the bench.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Gradient and wash media, centrifuge speeds/times and tube types are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Do not substitute media lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the sample has been received, identified and liquefied (ELB-SOP-018) and that a current order and consent exist.
2. Confirm the intended use (IVF or ICSI) and the planned insemination/injection time.
3. Confirm the **post-liquefaction baseline assessment** is recorded (volume, concentration, motility, vitality, morphology — per **WHO** manual) on **ELB-LOG-002**.
4. Select the preparation method by indication: density-gradient as default; swim-up where appropriate to the sample (per ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022; see Appendix A).
5. Confirm media are at working temperature and labelled to the patient.

> **CRITICAL — identity before processing.** Verify the sample label against the patient and worklist and complete an EW identity match before opening the tube (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). Only one patient's sample is open at the workstation.

## 7. Procedure

### 7A. Density-gradient centrifugation (default)

1. Confirm sample identity; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Layer the density gradient in a labelled conical tube per IFU (e.g. upper and lower density layers).
3. Gently load the liquefied semen on top of the gradient without disturbing the interface.
4. Centrifuge at the speed and time specified by the IFU (recorded against Appendix A).
5. Aspirate and discard the seminal plasma and gradient layers; recover the soft pellet.
6. Resuspend the pellet in wash medium and centrifuge again to wash; repeat per IFU.
7. Discard the supernatant, resuspend the final pellet in a defined volume of handling/culture medium.

### 7B. Swim-up (selected samples)

1. Confirm sample identity; perform the **EW identity match** (checkpoint W1).
2. Wash the liquefied sample in medium by centrifugation per IFU; discard supernatant.
3. Gently overlay the pellet with a defined volume of medium, or layer medium beneath, per the local technique.
4. Incubate undisturbed at the working temperature for the migration time (Appendix A / IFU) to allow motile sperm to swim up.
5. Recover the upper motile fraction without disturbing the pellet.

### 7C. Common finishing steps

1. Assess the prepared fraction: concentration, progressive motility and volume; calculate **TMSC**.
2. Record method, media lots, centrifuge settings, volumes and the post-preparation assessment on **ELB-LOG-003**.
3. Label the prepared aliquot to the patient and place under correct culture/handling conditions; perform an **EW match** before release for insemination/ICSI (checkpoint W2).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory wherever the sample is identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Sample identity vs patient vs worklist before processing | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Prepared aliquot correctly labelled and released to the right cycle | EW match |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Resolve identity and re-witness before continuing. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- A preparation is **acceptable** when the recovered fraction provides adequate progressive motility and a **TMSC** sufficient for the intended use; the operative thresholds and the method-selection criteria are in **Appendix A**.
- For a **poor recovery**, escalate to the senior embryologist: consider an alternative method, pooling of fractions, or proceeding to ICSI. Record the decision.
- A sample with no recoverable motile sperm is escalated to the clinician before the cycle proceeds.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure — centrifuge fault, poor recovery requiring a method change, EW downtime, or a media-lot substitution — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Semen analysis worksheet (baseline) — **ELB-LOG-002**.
- Sperm preparation worksheet — **ELB-LOG-003** (method, lots, settings, yield).
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Semen reference values follow the current **WHO** manual. Preparation method defaults (density-gradient / swim-up) are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022). Centrifuge speeds/times follow the media IFU. The minimum acceptable post-preparation **TMSC / motility** for each downstream use is **locally defined** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Sperm preparation is a selection step, not just a washing step: the goal is to hand the next stage the *best* spermatozoa, not merely *clean* ones. Density-gradient and swim-up each exploit a different sperm property — density versus motility — so matching method to sample matters. Gentle handling and disciplined removal of seminal plasma protect the cells you want; the label and witness checks protect the one thing that can never be repaired, the patient's identity.
