## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for removing the cumulus and corona cells from cumulus–oocyte complexes using hyaluronidase and mechanical denudation, so that oocyte maturity can be assessed and oocytes prepared for ICSI, while minimising enzymatic and mechanical injury to the oocyte.

## 2. Scope

Applies to denudation of oocytes from the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only), principally ahead of ICSI (ELB-SOP-027) and where maturity assessment is required. Oocytes for conventional insemination are inseminated cumulus-intact and are not routinely denuded (ELB-SOP-025). Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — performs enzymatic and mechanical denudation, assesses and records maturity, and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent (ELB-SOP-005).
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity at every transfer point (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews maturity yield and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Denudation | Removal of cumulus/corona cells from the oocyte |
| Hyaluronidase | Enzyme that digests the hyaluronan matrix binding cumulus cells |
| MII | Metaphase II — mature oocyte with extruded first polar body |
| MI | Metaphase I — no polar body, no germinal vesicle |
| GV | Germinal vesicle — immature oocyte |
| Denuding pipette | Calibrated stripping pipette of defined inner diameter |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Hyaluronidase solution at the working concentration specified by the IFU/Appendix A; pre-equilibrated where required.
- Pre-equilibrated buffered (HEPES/MOPS) handling medium for washing and mechanical stripping — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Denuding (stripping) pipettes of decreasing inner diameter; warmed dishes and surfaces.
- Stereomicroscope and inverted microscope with heated stage; tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Hyaluronidase concentration, the maximum oocyte exposure time and all media/oil conditions are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and that retrieval and culture (ELB-SOP-024) are complete for the patient.
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); confirm dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Confirm the hyaluronidase working concentration and exposure limit from the IFU/**Appendix A** 🔧.
4. Confirm all dishes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — minimise enzyme exposure.** Hyaluronidase contact must be kept to the minimum effective time per the IFU/Appendix A. Prolonged exposure risks oocyte injury and premature activation; mechanical stripping completes the denudation, not the enzyme.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm patient COCs at the workstation; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Transfer the COCs into the hyaluronidase droplet/dish and aspirate gently in and out of a wide-bore pipette to loosen the outer cumulus, for no longer than the IFU/Appendix A exposure limit 🔧.
3. Move the partially stripped complexes out of enzyme into the first handling-medium wash as soon as the cumulus loosens.
4. Complete denudation mechanically by pipetting through stripping pipettes of progressively smaller inner diameter, removing corona cells without forcing the oocyte through too narrow a bore.
5. Wash the denuded oocytes through fresh handling medium to clear residual enzyme and cells.
6. Assess each oocyte for maturity under the inverted microscope and classify as **MII**, **MI** or **GV**; note any morphological abnormality or degeneration (grading per ELB-SOP-029).
7. Transfer the denuded oocytes to a labelled, equilibrated culture dish; perform an **EW match** before returning to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
8. Record hyaluronidase lot and exposure time, oocyte counts by maturity (MII/MI/GV), any degeneration, dish/incubator position and operator on the retrieval/maturity record (**ELB-LOG-005**) and the culture record (**ELB-LOG-009**).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where gametes are identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of COCs presented for denudation vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Denuded oocytes returned to the correctly labelled dish/position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Proceed to ICSI:** mature MII oocytes (ELB-SOP-027).
- **Record and hold:** GV and MI oocytes are recorded; re-assessment or extended culture follows the local policy on immature oocytes 🔧 (**Appendix A**).
- Degenerated or grossly abnormal oocytes are recorded and not injected.
- Denudation causing oocyte lysis above the local alert threshold 🔧 (**Appendix A**) prompts review of technique and pipette diameter.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. enzyme exposure beyond the IFU limit, EW downtime, oocyte lysis above threshold) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Maturity counts on the oocyte retrieval/maturity worksheet — **ELB-LOG-005**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 14. Teaching point

Denudation is a controlled injury: the goal is a clean, spindle-intact MII oocyte, not the fastest strip. Let the enzyme do only the loosening — kept to the minimum time — and let calibrated mechanical pipetting finish the job. Forcing an oocyte through too narrow a bore, or leaving it in hyaluronidase, are the two avoidable causes of lysis and premature activation; both are matters of patience, not skill alone.
