## Purpose

To outline the procedure for the preparation of culture dishes and tubes used in treatment. To ensure the correct media is used for gametes/embryos at the different stages of development. To prevent microbial contamination of culture dishes. To ensure dishes/tubes are correctly labelled.

## Scope

The SOP is applicable to all embryologists and practitioners deemed competent to perform dish preparation. Including:

-   -   Equipment & Consumables Required
    -   General Guidance
    -   GMOPS\+ Centre Well Oocyte Collection Dishes for TVOR
    -   GIVF+5 Well Oocyte Collection Dishes for TVOR
    -   G1+ Post Denudation dish
    -   GTL Post ICSI GTL dish
    -   Hyase Dishes
    -   GMOPS+ ICSI/Lake Dishes
    -   GTL EmbryoScope dishes
    -   Post Thaw Dishes
    -   G2+ or EmbryoGlue Embryo Transfer Dish
    -   GMOPS+ SSR Dishes
    -   GIVF+ Sperm preparation tubes
    -   Pipetting Best Practice

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

### 5.2 General Guidance

All dishes/tubes containing gametes or embryos must be labelled with the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. as standard (except tubes used for collection of follicular fluid at TVOR).

Dishes already labelled with a patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. cannot be re-labelled or re-allocated to another patient.

An RI tag must be attached to the pot/tube/dish prior to transferring gametes/embryos into it.

The SOP assumes that clinics are 100% time lapse for fresh treatment cycles, in the event that time lapse is not available, continue to use ES+ GTL dishes for culture and use a standard incubator until such a time that time lapse is available.

All dishes/tubes/pots must be ‘vented’ in a flowhood for a minimum of 24hrs prior to use.

When preparing ES dishes, prepare one dish at any one time. When preparing microdrop dishes, prepare a maximum of two dishes at any one time. When preparing dishes with >0.5ml volumes, prepare a maximum of three dishes at any one time.

Media volumes must be measured using a pipette and must not be estimated.

Care must be taken to ensure the correct volume has been decanted into the dish/tube. Insufficient volumes of media can result in significant changes in osmolality/pH which may render gametes/embryos non-viable.

All dishes must be prepared on a room temperature surface with care taken to replace lids as soon as practically possible.

Sterile technique must be observed throughout all dish preparation and only sterile products must be used for dish preparation.

Avoid repeatedly extracting media from the same media bottle by either decanting a set volume into a dish and using that for preparation or using a multi-pipette. Do not pipette unused media back into the media bottle.

With exception of GMOPS, all other culture media are pre-supplemented with HSA.

All bicarbonate buffered media must be equilibrated for a minimum of 6 hours in a gassed incubator prior to use. Where possible dishes/tubes must be prepared on the day prior to use or on the day of use to ensure optimum condition (exception may be at a weekend when a member of the laboratory team would otherwise not be on site i.e., prepare on Saturday for Monday if no laboratory staff on site on a Sunday).

It is useful to have some ‘spare’ equilibrated dishes in the event that more oocytes are collected than expected (spare GIVF+ 5 well), an IVF case needs to be converted to ICSI (spare post denudation and ES+ GTL) or an embryo transfer is difficult (spare G2+ tube).

All preparation must take place in a flowhood. Before using any media, the expiry date on the bottle must be checked. A robust system must be in place to ensure any expired media is not used clinically and it is either removed from the fridge or moved to a clearly labelled area of the fridge marked ‘Expired Media’. Expired media may be used for training and monitoring of equipment.

All media has a standard manufacturer’s expiry date, but this may be shortened following opening/warming. See the table overleaf for guidelines on products which may have modified expiry dates after opening.

Note the date of opening on the media bottle along with any adjuncts that have been added to the media, for example ‘+ Heparin’ if heparin has been added.

### 5.3 G MOPS+ TVOR Centre Well Dishes, prepared p.m. on the day before TVOR

Prepare 1 x G MOPS+ Centre Well Dishes per 6 follicles up to a maximum of three dishes (thereafter the dishes are rotated). Pipette 1ml of G MOPS+ into each dish and quickly overlay with 1ml of oil.

Place in an ungassed incubator to warm overnight along with one empty 60mm culture dish per patient.

GMOPS plus Heparin is used for rinsing the TVOR needle and follicular flushing. Add 200µl of Heparin (1,000 I.U./ml) to each 125ml GMOPS bottle and gently invert. Write the date of opening on the bottle.

Prepare 6mls of GMOPS plus heparin in 14ml test tubes/patient. This is used to flush the oocyte retrieval needle. Warm in an ungassed incubator overnight.

If flushing is required (≤3 follicles of ≥10mm at the point of trigger), prepare a further 20mls of GMOPS plus heparin in 14ml test tubes/flush case. Warm in an ungassed incubator overnight. Prior to the oocyte collection, load the GMOPS plus heparin into a 20ml syringe using a kwill.

1ml Oil

### 5.4 GIVF+ 5 well dishes, prepared p.m. on the day before TVOR.

Figure 4: 40mm GTL Dish

**5.6.2 ES+ GTL dish, prepared day before TVOR or on the morning of the TVOR**

Prepare one ES+ GTL Post ICSI dish per 16 oocytes.

The dishes can be made on the day of the oocyte collection, in which case continuous culture can take place until Day 6 or they can be made the day before the oocyte collection, in which case continuous culture can take place until Day 5 (medium change required on Day 5 for culture until Day 6/7).

Decant 0.5-1.0ml of GTL into a dish. Pipette 30µl into each wash well (A-D). Using a narrow diameter plastic pipette, pipette G-TL into each of the individual wells (1-16) whilst looking down the microscope. Make sure the tip touches the bottom of each microwell during filling and slightly overfill the well to create a convex meniscus to help minimise bubble formation. Using a pipette, fill the two culture compartments each with 180µl of G-TL. Overlay with 2.0ml of oil, ensuring all microwells and wash drops are completely covered.

Equilibrate in a gassed incubator for a minimum of 6 hours.

_Additional Note: The pH of the GTL media reaches a pH of 7.32 when equilibrated in an ES+ incubator for 6 hours. The pH make take longer to reach 7.32 in a conventional incubator, particularly when the door is opened on a regular basis._

### 5.8 GMOPS+ ICSI Dish, prepared a.m. the morning of the TVOR

### 5.11 Post Thaw Dish, prepared p.m. day before the thaw

For oocyte thaws, prepare a 40mm G1+ dish to be used immediately post thaw (as seen in section 5.5). Following injection, move to an ES+ GTL dish (with or without the intermediary 40mm GTL dish). See section 5.6.

For thawing of day 1-3 embryos, prepare an ES+ GTL culture dish so that undisturbed culture can take place until Day 5 or 6 (see section 5.6).

For thawing of day 5/6/7 blastocysts, prepare a G2+ culture dish as seen below.

### 5.12 G2+ or Embryo Glue Centre Well Embryo Transfer Dish, media/dish prepared p.m. day before Embryo Transfer

Prepare 1 x 1ml of Embryo Glue or G2+, depending upon the patient’s request, in a 5ml test tube. Additionally prepare 1 x 1ml G2+ in a 5ml test tube for rinsing the catheter.

Place both test tubes loose capped in a gassed incubator to equilibrate overnight.

In addition, place a labelled Centre well dish in the incubator to warm.

On the morning of embryo transfer (minimum 3 hours before ET), decant 1ml of G2+ or Embryo Glue into the centre well dish ready for the embryo transfer.

Use the additional 1 x 1ml of G2+ to rinse the catheter immediately before loading.

1ml G2+ or EmbryoGlue

### 5.13 GMOPS+ plus Heparin SSR Dishes, Centre well & 60mm Culture dishes, media prepared p.m. on the day before the SSR

Prepare 2 x 12.5mls of GMOPS+ with Heparin in 14mm test tubes per SSR and place into an ungassed incubator to warm overnight (20µl Heparin is added to each 12.5ml tube of GMOPS+).

Warm 4 x centre well dishes for PESA and 4 x 60mm culture dishes for biopsy in an incubator.

Immediately prior to the SSR procedure, place the empty SSR dishes on a heated stage and then pipette 5mls of GMOPS+ plus heparin into each 60mm culture dish and 0.5ml GMOPS\+ with heparin into each centre well dish.

Keep the dishes on the heated stage during the procedure.

### 5.14 GIVF+ Conical Tubes for Sperm Preparation, prepared p.m. of the day before TVOR

Prepare 2 pre-labelled conical tubes with GIVF+, 1 x 2ml and 1 x 4ml. The 2ml is used for the first wash step and the 4ml is for the second 2.0ml wash and a further 1ml to resuspend the final sample ready for insemination (1ml). The final 1ml can be used to dilute the stock solution if required.

Equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight.

Puresperm100 is diluted with GIVF+ to a concentration of 80% & 40% and is used to make 80:40 density gradients. See Oxford Medical Kuwait Sperm Preparation SOP.

### 5.15 Pipetting Best Practice

Do not carry all the patient’s oocytes/embryos in the pipette at any one time (only exception is when there is only one oocyte/embryo).

Prime the pipette with the culture medium from the dish the oocytes/embryos are being moved to (the only exception is thawing of oocytes/embryos/blastocysts).

Minimise carry-over of previous medium by expelling the oocytes/embryos into the new dish but not the reservoir of media behind the oocytes/embryos.

Use a size appropriate pipette to minimise carry-over of culture media.

Ensure oocytes/embryos are counted into the pipette and counted out once in the dish to ensure none remain within the pipette.

Do not carry the oocytes/embryos too close to the tip of the pipette.

When handling fully hatched blastocysts, ensure a large pipette is used and a minimal amount of pressure is applied to the blastocyst. Handling must also be kept to a minimum. If the blastocyst is known to be hatched, alert the patient in advance of the increased risk of damage during handling.
