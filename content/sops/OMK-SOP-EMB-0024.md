#### Purpose

To outline the procedure for ICSI and associated techniques such as Artificial Oocyte Activation (AOA) and Hypo-Osmotic Swelling (HOS) Test. The SOP includes information on the preparation of the micromanipulator, injection technique and additional techniques that may be required.

#### Scope

Applicable to all laboratory staff deemed competent to perform ICSI.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

#### Definitions

ICSI Intra Cytoplasmic Sperm Injection

AOA Artificial Oocyte Activation

HOST Hypo-Osmotic Swelling Test

ES+ Embryoscope Plus

TL Time lapse

#### Procedure

##### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   ICSI Needle
-   Holding Pipette
-   145-200µm Pipette
-   Pipette & Tips
-   Marker Pen
-   Timer (for AOA only)

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   ICSI Dishes (10 & 20µl GMOPS+ drops and 5µl PVP drop)
-   ES+ GTL Post ICSI dish ±40mm GTL Post ICSI dish
-   ICSI Dish for Lake (optional)
-   Sperm Mobil (optional)
-   CultActive (optional)
-   HOS Test (optional)
-   RI Witness Tags

##### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the start of the procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of all used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point

Ensure the ICSI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point.

##### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B)

Before beginning an ICSI procedure check the laboratory notes to ensure that ‘ICSI’ is the agreed insemination method.

Check whether the patent has elected to have a IVF:ICSI split, if so ensure only the designated number of oocytes are inseminated by ICSI.


ICSI insemination requires a second witness to be used alongside RI witness.


Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the G1+ Post Denudation dish matches the ICSI dishes, laboratory notes and RI witness system.

Approve these checks on the RI witness system and both operator and witness must sign for these checks. Each time oocytes or sperm are added to an ICSI or lake dish, a second witness is required.

Once the oocytes have been in injected, alongside RI Witness, check the full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the ICSI dish matches that on the ES+ GTL or 40mm GTL Post ICSI dish, laboratory notes and RI Witness system.

If an intermediary 40mm GTL dish is used post ICSI, alongside RI Witness check the full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the 40mm GTL dish matches that on the ES+ GTL dish, laboratory notes and RI Witness system.

At the end of the procedure the operator must ensure that the correct number of oocytes have been injected and the total number contained with the GTL Post ICSI dish must be noted on the side of the dish.

##### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare ICSI dishes a minimum of 1 hour prior to use and warm in an ungassed incubator. Prepare one ICSI dish per 4-6 oocytes up to a maximum of 3 dishes, thereafter dishes are re-used. Oocytes are injected in batches of 4-6 depending upon the local stability of the heated stage, sperm quality and ICSI practitioners experience. One oocyte is placed in each droplet. If a ‘Lake Dish’ is required, prepare a minimum of 1 hour prior to use and warm in an ungassed incubator.

**Post ICSI Culture Options:**

**1\. Oocytes are transferred into a 40mm GTL Post ICSI dish during the ICSI procedure and then moved to an ES+ GTL dish at the end of the procedure**

**or**

**2\. Oocytes are transferred straight into an ES+ GTL Post ICSI dish following injection.**

The ES+ GTL dish requires a minimum of 6 hours equilibration in a gassed incubator prior to use. Initial transfer of injected oocytes into a 40mm GTL Post ICSI dish during the ICSI procedure may help facilitate achieving the minimum 6 hour equilibration time for the ES+ GTL dish if it is made on Day 0 and also protect the ES+ GTL dish against pH changes that would occur as a result of being removed from the incubator multiple times during an ICSI procedure.

The GTL post ICSI dish requires a minimum of 6 hours equilibration in a gassed incubator prior to use. The GTL dish can be prepared the day prior to the oocyte collection, in which case a medium change is required on Day 5. Or the dish can be prepared on the morning of the oocyte collection and equilibrated for a minimum of 6 hours prior to use, in which case a medium change would be required on day 6 (if culturing to day 7).

One 40mm GTL dish is required per 24 injected oocytes. One ES+ GTL dish is required per 16 injected oocytes.

_Additional Note: Validation shows a pH of 7.32 with an ES+ GTL dish is achieved in an ES+ incubator after 6 hours equilibration. If equilibrating in a conventional style incubator, reaching the optimal pH may take longer. Validate locally if a non-ES+ incubator is used for equilibration._

##### 5.5 Preparation of ICSI Micromanipulator

Check that the microscope is in the correct focal plane for an ICSI dish, this can be done by visualising a cross on the base of the dish. Adjust using course or fine focus controls on the microscope as necessary.

Ensure all controls, joysticks and syringes are operating smoothly and are in mid-range of their full movement potential.

Insert the ICSI and holding pipettes into the pipette holder and ensure a tight seal. Align the ICSI and Holding pipettes by initially observing and adjusting on low magnification before gradually increasing magnification up to X40.

At X40 both ICSI and holding pipettes should be in focus, have a central position and be approximately parallel to the base of the dish. Check the pipettes for any sign of damage and replace if necessary.

Raise both pipettes so that the ICSI dish can be placed on the heated stage with ease.

##### 5.6 Timing of ICSI Procedure

Performed ICSI 4 hours post oocyte collection ± 30 minutes.

##### 5.7 Standard ICSI Procedure

Consideration must be given to maintaining temperature, pH and osmolality of the dishes during the ICSI procedure. As such a maximum of 6 oocytes are injected at any one time and a target time for completion of the 6 oocytes should be <15 minutes.

Perform the witnessing checks as described above.

Depending on the concentration of the sperm stock, add approximately 1-3µl of sperm stock solution to the PVP. Selecting motile sperm can be made easier by adding the sperm to one side of the PVP droplet and then selecting sperm for injection from the opposite side.

Remove the G1+ Post Denudation dish from the incubator and transfer up to 6 oocytes to the wash drop of the ICSI dish. Return the G1+ Post Denudation dish to a gassed incubator. Rinse the oocytes in the wash drop and then place one oocyte in each drop.

Transfer the ICSI dish to the micromanipulator heated stage ensuring the entire surface area of the dish is on the heated stage.

Visualise the drops within the dish and adjust focus as necessary.

Equilibrate the ICSI and holding pipettes in PVP and GMOPS+ respectively.

Visualise the sperm within the PVP drop. Using x40 magnification select the best sperm available for injection considering which sperm are the most motile and the most normally shaped.

Immobilise the sperm and cause a breach in the sperm membrane by swiping the injection pipette over the middle of the sperm tail 2-3 times. A clear ‘break’ in the tail must be visualised before injection. Take care not to damage the head or mid-piece during the process.

Aspirate the sperm into the injection pipette tail first, ensure the position of the sperm is stable within the pipette and is not travelling up or down. Move to the first drop containing an oocyte.

Using x20-x40 magnification, visualise the oocyte and then bring the injection and holding pipettes into clear focus. Rotate the oocyte such that the polar body is at 12 or 6 o’clock and firmly hold the oocyte with the holding pipette. Ensure the oolemma and pipettes are in clear focus then gently bring the sperm to the tip of the injection pipette. Once at the tip, with the injection pipette at 3 or 9 o’clock, gently pass the injection pipette ½ to ¾ way into the ooplasm, apply pressure using the syringe to breach the oolemma (this is often seen with a sudden rush of ooplasm up the pipette). Once the membrane has been breached, gently put the ooplasm and the sperm into the oocyte. The aim is to leave the sperm ½ to ¾ of the way into the ooplasm. Avoid putting excess PVP into the oocyte. Slowly withdraw the needle taking care not to drag the sperm out with the needle. If the sperm does not remain inside the oocyte, the oocyte must be reinjected.

Repeat the procedure for all remaining oocytes in the dish keeping careful track of which oocytes have been injected and which have not.

Once all the oocytes within the first dish have been injected, move the injected oocytes to the GTL Post ICSI dish. If the injected oocytes are to be placed straight into an ES+ dish, print a name/bar code label from the EmbryoViewer.

Alongside RI Witness, check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. matches that on the ICSI dish, GTL Post ICSI dish, laboratory notes and RI witness system.

If moving into a 40mm GTL dish Post ICSI, wash the injected oocytes through 3 drops of GTL and then place in culture drops in groups of up to 4. If moving straight into ES+ GTL dish Post ICSI, wash through three wash wells and then place one injected oocyte in each TL well. Replace the GTL Post ICSI dish in the gassed incubator.

Repeat the procedure above until all the oocytes that are suitable for injection have been injected. Immature oocytes must be checked at the end of the procedure to determine whether they have moved to MII status and are now suitable for injection.

If the oocytes have been transferred into a 40mm GTL Post ICSI dish during the procedure, once the injections are complete, transfer all the injected oocytes into an ES+ GTL culture dish.

At the end of the procedure, mark the total number of oocytes in the ES+ GTL dish on the side of the dish.

Place the ES+ GTL Post ICSI dish into the ES+ incubator overnight (in the event that there is no space within the ES+, overnight culture can take place in a standard incubator but the fertilisation check must then take place at 15-18 hours post insemination ± 30 minutes).

Clear the flowhood/Micromanipulator of any used consumables checking that no mature/injected oocytes remain in the dishes to be discarded.

Use Oosafe spray ± sterile water to clean the surfaces and wipe the lower section of the pipette to prevent any cross contamination of sperm samples.

Complete the embryology & witnessing records.

_Additional Note: In Poland the maximum permitted number of oocytes for injection is six, unless the patients qualifies for exemption.._

##### 5.8 Low Maturity or No Mature Oocytes

If the patient has a low level of maturity, <25%, inject all suitable oocytes at the standard injection time and re-check any MI oocytes towards the end of the working day.

In the event that there are no MII oocytes available for injection on the day of TVOR, consider retaining the oocytes until 8-9am on Day 1. If any oocytes have reached MII they can be injected at this point should the patient wish to do so. Any oocytes not at MII will be discarded at this stage.

##### 5.9 Management of Severe Oligo/Asthenozoospermic Samples

In the event that the sperm sample has a very low concentration or motility it may be preferable to put the sperm sample in GMOPS\+ and find the sperm for injection ahead of the ICSI procedure. This is performed in a separate dish referred to as a ‘Lake Dish’. The ‘Lake dish’ consists of a several strips of GMOPS+ and a droplet of PVP. The ‘Lake dish’ can be used in combination with Sperm Mobil or the HOS test.

Using a pipette place a sample of the sperm solution in the droplets of GMOPS+. Equilibrate the ICSI pipette with PVP and then search within the droplet for the best available sperm.

Once found move the sperm to the PVP drop to a designated area where the sperm can be easily found at a later time point.

Continue until enough sperm have been found for the ICSI procedure.

If the patient has consented to the use of Sperm Mobil and it is clinically appropriate to use it, add 2µl Sperm Mobil to each of the GMOPS+ droplets where the sperm will be added. Identify motile sperm and transfer them to the PVP drop.

If the patient has consented to the use of the HOS Test and it is clinically appropriate to use it, replace one of the GMOPS+ droplets with HOST solution. Transfer sperm from the GMOPS+ droplet and place in the HOST solution. If the sperm tail swells/curls the sperm is deemed viable and suitable for injection.

Once all the sperm for injection have been collected, transfer the sperm from the ‘Lake dish’ to the PVP drop of the ICSI dish.

Perform the ICSI procedure as described above using the pre- selected sperm.

##### 5.10 Artificial Oocyte Activation (AOA)\]

If the patient has consented to AOA and it is clinically appropriate to perform, the procedure is performed immediately after the ICSI procedure.

Shake the CultActive bottle for 30 seconds prior to use. Prepare the AOA dishes a minimum of 6 hours prior to use and place in a gassed incubator for equilibration. CultActive is a bicarbonate buffered culture media.

Transferred injected oocytes into the CultActive droplets immediately following injection. Place 2-3 oocytes in each CultActive droplet and set the timer for 15 minutes. Return the AOA dish to a gassed incubator.

After 15 minutes, remove the oocytes from the CultActive media and rinse the oocytes through the three GTL wash drops and then transfer to the GTL Post ICSI dish. Repeat until all injected oocytes have been exposed and cultured in CultActive.
