## 1. Purpose

To define the safe and correct operation of laminar flow hoods (clean-air workstations) used for handling gametes and embryos, so that a controlled, particle- and contaminant-reduced air environment is maintained over the working field and the integrity of the HEPA-filtered airflow is verified. Correct operation protects the sample; correct verification proves the protection is real.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all horizontal/vertical laminar flow hoods and integrated heated workstations used in the Embryology Laboratory for handling the patient couple's own gametes and embryos and for preparing culture dishes (autologous only). Covers start-up, working technique, cleaning, airflow integrity verification and certification. Sterile technique is detailed in ELB-SOP-009 and air-quality monitoring in ELB-SOP-013. Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Embryologist on duty** — operates the hood per this SOP and ELB-SOP-009, performs start-up and cleaning, and records use/checks.
- **Quality Manager** — schedules HEPA-filter integrity testing, airflow verification and external certification, and maintains records (ELB-LOG-020).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — authorises return to use after maintenance or a failed check.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Laminar flow | Smooth, unidirectional filtered airflow that sweeps particles away from the working field |
| HEPA | High-Efficiency Particulate Air filter |
| Airflow velocity | Speed of air across the working field, verified against the acceptable range 🔧 (Appendix A) |
| Certification | Periodic qualified test of filter integrity and airflow performance |
| Working field | The clean zone in which gametes/embryos and open dishes are handled |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Laminar flow hood with HEPA filtration and (where fitted) integral heated surface and lighting.
- Anemometer / airflow indicator or the hood's own airflow monitor for routine verification.
- Embryo-safe, non-volatile cleaning agents approved for clean-air surfaces (per ELB-SOP-009/014).
- Hood logbook/QC record; calibration and certification records — ELB-LOG-020; air-quality context — ELB-LOG-022.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Operate, clean and certify the hood per its IFU. Use only cleaning agents validated as embryo-safe and free of volatile residues; alcohols and disinfectants must be allowed to flash off before culture material is introduced, as VOC carry-over harms embryos (ELB-SOP-013).

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Switch on the hood and allow it to run for the IFU purge/stabilisation time before use.
2. Confirm airflow is within the acceptable range using the hood monitor or anemometer; confirm no alarm.
3. Confirm the HEPA-filter certification is current (ELB-LOG-020); do not use a hood overdue for certification.
4. Confirm the working surface and integral heater (where fitted) are clean and at the verified temperature.

## 7. Procedure

**Start-up & preparation**

1. Clean the interior surfaces with an approved embryo-safe agent, working from clean to less-clean areas; allow any solvent to evaporate fully before introducing media or material.
2. Arrange only the items needed inside the hood; place them so airflow to the working field is not blocked and dishes are not in another item's wake.

**Working technique**

3. Keep the working field clear and downstream airflow unobstructed; avoid rapid movements that disturb laminar flow.
4. Handle one patient's material at a time in the hood and label per ELB-SOP-016; do not open two patients' material together.
5. Minimise the time open dishes are exposed and keep them within the certified clean zone.

**Shutdown & cleaning**

6. On completion, remove all material, clean surfaces with the approved agent, and leave the hood running or in standby per IFU.
7. Record the session, cleaning and any airflow observations in the hood log.

**Verification & certification**

8. Verify airflow at the routine frequency 🔧 (Appendix A) and record it.
9. Arrange qualified HEPA-integrity and airflow **certification** at the locally defined interval 🔧 (Appendix A) and after any filter change, relocation or repair.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Hood operation does not require a second-person identity witness; material handled in the hood is identified and labelled per ELB-SOP-016, and routine checks are **signed and timed** (ELB-SOP-001).

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Do not handle gametes or embryos in a hood whose airflow is outside range, whose certification is overdue, or in which a cleaning solvent has not fully evaporated. VOC carry-over is invisible and embryotoxic; when in doubt, wait or move to a confirmed clean workstation.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | HEPA certification current and airflow in range before use | ELB-LOG-020 + airflow check |
| **C2** | Cleaning solvent fully evaporated before material introduced | Time/observation per ELB-SOP-009/013 |
| **C3** | Single patient's material handled; labelled | ELB-SOP-016 |

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** the hood for use when airflow is within the acceptable range, certification is current, surfaces are clean and any solvent has evaporated.
- **Reject** the hood when airflow is out of range, an alarm is active, certification is overdue, or filters/seals are suspect.

## 10. Deviations

An out-of-range airflow, a failed or overdue certification, or a contamination concern is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Withdraw the hood from clinical use, arrange certification/repair, and relate the event to air-quality monitoring (ELB-SOP-013, ELB-LOG-022). Escalate per ELB-POL-005 where exposed material may have been compromised.

## 11. Records generated

- Hood operation/QC log entries (use, cleaning, airflow checks).
- HEPA-filter integrity / airflow certification and calibration of airflow instruments — **ELB-LOG-020**.
- Air-quality / TVOC context — **ELB-LOG-022**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The principle of HEPA-filtered, controlled clean-air handling and VOC avoidance is **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 laboratory air-quality guidance). The acceptable **airflow velocity range**, the routine airflow-check **frequency**, and the **certification interval** are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**). Instrument calibration and certification traceability follow ISO 15189.

## 14. Teaching point

A laminar flow hood protects the sample only while its airflow is intact and its air is clean — and both can fail silently. Certification proves the filter still does its job; technique preserves the unidirectional sweep you are relying on. The most common harm at the hood is not particles but chemistry: a disinfectant that smells clean to you can be lethal to an embryo, so let solvents evaporate before the dishes go in.
