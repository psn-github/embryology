## 1. Purpose

To define the laboratory method for receiving, searching, isolating and culturing cumulus–oocyte complexes (COCs) recovered at transvaginal ultrasound-guided oocyte retrieval, so that every oocyte is identified, counted and placed in equilibrated culture with full traceability from the moment of aspiration.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all oocyte retrievals performed for the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only). Covers the laboratory's role: follicular-fluid receipt at the hatch, COC identification and washing, oocyte counting, and transfer to culture pending insemination (ELB-SOP-025) or ICSI (ELB-SOP-027). The clinical aspiration is performed by the treating clinician and is outside this SOP. Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — searches the follicular fluid, isolates and counts COCs, and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent (ELB-SOP-005).
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity at receipt and at every transfer point (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reconciles oocyte count against follicle count with the clinician and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| COC | Cumulus–oocyte complex — the oocyte with its surrounding cumulus and corona |
| Follicular fluid | Aspirate from each follicle, searched for COCs |
| OCCC | Oocyte–corona–cumulus complex (synonym for COC) |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |
| MII / MI / GV | Oocyte maturity stages (assessed after denudation, ELB-SOP-026) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Stereomicroscope with heated stage/surface; warmed search dishes and Petri dishes.
- Pre-equilibrated buffered (HEPES/MOPS) flushing/handling medium and culture medium under oil overlay — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Warmed test-tube rack/heat block for incoming follicular-fluid tubes; sterile transfer pipettes.
- Heated transport block or warmed surface to receive tubes from the procedure room; RI Witness reader at the workstation.
- Tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**) for COC culture.

> **IFU cross-reference.** All media, oil and consumables are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and the expected patient, with consent verified (ELB-POL-009).
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A) and that culture dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time specified in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Confirm search dishes, handling medium and heated surfaces are at setpoint temperature 🔧 before the first tube arrives.
4. Confirm all dishes and tubes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — identity at receipt.** Follicular-fluid tubes are accepted from the procedure room only after a patient-identity match (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016). Only one patient's tubes are open at the workstation at any time.

## 7. Procedure

1. Receive labelled follicular-fluid tubes at the hatch; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1) and confirm tube labels against the worklist.
2. Keep tubes on the warmed rack. Working promptly, pour or pipette each tube's contents into a warmed search dish.
3. Search the fluid under the stereomicroscope; identify each COC by its expanded cumulus mass. Isolate it with a transfer pipette.
4. Wash each COC through fresh handling medium to remove blood and debris.
5. Transfer the washed COCs into a labelled, equilibrated culture dish, minimising time on the heated surface.
6. Count and record the number of COCs recovered. Note appearance (e.g. expanded, compact, blood-stained, atretic) without assigning maturity — maturity is assessed only after denudation (ELB-SOP-026).
7. Reconcile the COC count against the clinician's follicle count; report discrepancies to the Laboratory Lead and the clinician.
8. Perform an **EW match** before returning the culture dish to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
9. Record retrieval details — patient, date/time, COC count, dish/incubator position, media lots and operator — on the oocyte retrieval worksheet (**ELB-LOG-005**).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where gametes are identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of incoming follicular-fluid tubes vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | COC culture dish labelled to the correct patient and incubator position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept and culture:** all recovered COCs, regardless of cumulus appearance — maturity is determined later (ELB-SOP-026).
- **Record but flag:** heavily blood-stained or grossly atretic complexes are noted; final disposition follows maturity assessment.
- A discrepancy between follicle count and oocyte count beyond the local reconciliation threshold 🔧 (**Appendix A**) is escalated before the session is closed.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. EW downtime, temperature excursion of the heated surface, a count discrepancy beyond the local threshold) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Significant clinical events follow the adverse-events policy.

## 11. Records generated

- Oocyte retrieval worksheet — **ELB-LOG-005**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.
- Maturity and downstream handling are recorded under ELB-SOP-026 and the embryo culture record (ELB-LOG-009).

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Retrieval-relevant entries include: search-surface and incubator temperature setpoints, culture-dish equilibration time, maximum oocyte time on the heated surface per batch, and the local follicle-to-oocyte count reconciliation threshold. Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / Istanbul Consensus) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Oocyte retrieval is where the cycle becomes physical material the laboratory is accountable for, so the discipline is speed without compromise: COCs are fragile and temperature-sensitive, yet a rushed search loses oocytes in the fluid. Search thoroughly, keep everything warm, and let the count — reconciled against the follicles — be the first quiet check that nothing was missed. Maturity is not judged here; that waits until the oocyte is denuded.
