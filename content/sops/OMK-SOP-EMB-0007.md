## 1. Purpose

To define how patients, gametes and embryos are uniquely identified and labelled throughout the laboratory, so that every specimen is unambiguously attributable to the correct patient couple at every step. Misidentification is the single error in assisted reproduction that cannot be undone; this SOP is the foundation that witnessing (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002) and chain of custody (OMK-SOP-EMB-0008) build on.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all patient-specific material — semen, prepared sperm, oocytes, zygotes, embryos — and to every vessel, dish, tube, straw, cane and record that holds or refers to it, from receipt/retrieval to transfer, cryostorage or disposition. Applies to the patient couple's own gametes and embryos only (autologous). Donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **All embryologists and andrologists** — apply, read and verify identifiers at every handling step and never proceed on an unclear identifier.
- **Receiving/retrieving staff** — establish the primary identifiers at the point of receipt/retrieval and confirm them against the patient.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness)** — independently verifies identity at every critical point (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).
- **Quality Manager** — audits labelling integrity; **Medical Director** — owns the identifier scheme.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Primary identifiers | The minimum dataset that uniquely identifies the patient/couple |
| Unique cycle/specimen ID | The case identifier linking all material in a treatment cycle |
| RFID / barcode tag | Machine-readable label read by the electronic witnessing system |
| RI Witness | Electronic witnessing system (RFID-based identity matching) |
| EW | Electronic witnessing |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Approved cryo-resistant, alcohol- and LN₂-stable labels and markers.
- RI Witness RFID tags/labels and readers at each workstation and storage point.
- Pre-printed patient/cycle labels generated from the verified record (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).
- Dishes, tubes, straws and canes that accept durable labelling.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Labels, inks and RFID tags are used per their IFU and validated for the temperature/chemical exposure of their use (incubation, alcohol, LN₂). Tags/labels are confirmed legible and adherent after exposure; cryo-labels are applied per the cryostorage IFU.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the patient's identity at first contact using at least two independent **primary identifiers** 🔧 (defined in **Appendix A**) against the record.
2. Confirm the unique cycle/specimen ID is assigned and matches the consent and worklist (OMK-POL-EMB-0006).
3. Confirm labels/tags are available, legible and validated for the intended exposure.

## 7. Procedure

1. At receipt or retrieval, establish the primary identifiers and the unique cycle/specimen ID; confirm against the patient/record (and the partner for partner samples).
2. Label every vessel **before** it receives material, with the full identifier set defined in **Appendix A** 🔧 — minimally two patient identifiers plus the unique cycle/specimen ID.
3. Apply the RI Witness tag and register the vessel in the electronic witnessing system.
4. At every transfer between vessels, label the destination vessel first, read both source and destination identifiers, and perform the EW match (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).
5. Keep only one patient's material open at a workstation at any time; do not place two patients' open vessels in the same field.
6. On re-labelling (e.g. lot change, vessel change), record the reason, re-apply the full identifier set, re-register the tag, and re-witness.
7. For cryostored material, label the straw/device and the cane/position, and record the position in the inventory (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045) under chain of custody (OMK-SOP-EMB-0008).
8. Confirm legibility and tag function after any exposure (alcohol, incubation, LN₂); replace any degraded label and re-witness.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Identity verification is witnessed at every point where material is identified, combined, moved or stored (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual two-person read is the validated fallback when EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity and cycle ID at receipt/retrieval | Two primary identifiers + EW registration |
| **W2** | Source and destination identifiers match at every transfer | EW match / two-person read (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002) |
| **W3** | Label and tag on cryostored material match the inventory position | EW + inventory cross-check (OMK-SOP-EMB-0008) |

> **CRITICAL — stop on any identifier mismatch.** A discrepancy, an unclear label, or a failed/absent witness match halts the step. Do not relabel "to make it match" — resolve the identity to source, document it, and re-witness. Two patients' open vessels are never present at one workstation.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

A specimen is acceptable to handle only when it carries the full, legible identifier set, the cycle ID matches the worklist and consent, and the EW (or manual) witness match passes. Unlabelled, ambiguously labelled, mismatched or non-matching specimens are reject/stop conditions.

## 10. Deviations

Mislabelling, identifier mismatches and witness failures are handled under the deviation/CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and recorded in the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Any event with potential for misattribution is also a reportable adverse event (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).

## 11. Records generated

- Identifier/labelling entries within the cycle record and worklist (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).
- Electronic witnessing match records (RI Witness) and manual witness entries (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).
- Cryostorage inventory entries — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0045** (via OMK-SOP-EMB-0008).
- Any deviation — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The defined set of **primary identifiers** 🔧 and the full **vessel identifier set** 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The requirement for unique identification and independent witnessing of identity is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022) and uses **RI Witness** electronic witnessing; record control aligns with ISO 15189 and confidentiality with OMK-POL-EMB-0005.

## 14. Teaching point

Every other safeguard in the laboratory assumes the label is right. Identification is therefore done once, correctly, at the source, and re-verified at every transfer — never reconstructed later from memory. The discipline is simple and absolute: label the empty vessel before it holds anything, read it at every move, witness it, and stop the instant anything does not match. The label is the patient.
