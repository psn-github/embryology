## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for warming vitrified embryos and oocytes — ultra-rapid rewarming with stepwise cryoprotectant dilution — so that cryopreserved material is returned to culture with maximal survival, assessed against defined survival and re-expansion criteria, and with full traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the warming of vitrified embryos (cleavage and blastocyst stage) and vitrified mature oocytes belonging to the **patient couple** (autologous), retrieved from cryostorage for use in a treatment cycle. Vitrification is covered separately (ELB-SOP-033, ELB-SOP-034). Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope and is not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — must be trained and signed off as competent in warming (ELB-SOP-005) before working unsupervised. Responsible for the procedure, the witnessing checkpoints, the survival assessment and the records generated.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and specimen identity at retrieval from the tank and at every labelling and transfer step (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms consent for warming and intended use is current (ELB-POL-009), reviews survival outcomes and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Warming | Ultra-rapid rewarming of vitrified material with stepwise removal of cryoprotectant |
| TS / DS / WS | Thawing/warming solution · dilution solution · wash solution (kit-defined) |
| Re-expansion | Recovery of blastocoel volume after warming, used as a viability indicator for blastocysts |
| Survival | Intact membrane and structural integrity of the warmed cell(s) per defined criteria |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Validated commercial warming kit matched to the original vitrification kit (TS, DS, WS) used within shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Stereomicroscope with heated stage; calibrated timer; fine handling pipettes.
- Pre-equilibrated culture dish for post-warming recovery (ELB-SOP-043, Appendix A).
- Liquid nitrogen (LN₂), transfer dewar, retrieval tongs/forceps; cryo PPE (face shield, cryo gloves, closed footwear, apron); LN₂ area oxygen monitoring (ELB-SOP-037).
- Cold-chain identifiers and the RI Witness reader.

> **IFU cross-reference.** The warming solution temperature, the immediate immersion of the carrier into pre-warmed thawing solution, and each dilution step's exposure time are defined by the **validated kit IFU** and ESHRE 2015. Use the warming kit matched to the vitrification kit used at freeze. Do not deviate from the IFU timings.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm valid, signed consent for warming and the intended use (e.g. transfer, ELB-SOP-032) is on file (ELB-POL-009).
2. Confirm the specific carrier(s) to warm against the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016): patient, tank, canister, cane and position.
3. Confirm the warming kit is within shelf life and the thawing solution is pre-warmed to the IFU temperature; set timers.
4. Confirm the post-warming recovery dish is prepared, labelled and equilibrated (Appendix A).
5. Confirm LN₂ level in the transport dewar and that cryo PPE is worn before opening the tank (ELB-SOP-037).

> **CRITICAL — identity at retrieval.** Identity is verified twice: once when the carrier is located and removed from the tank against the inventory, and again at the workstation before warming. Only one couple's material is open at a workstation at any time (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).

## 7. Procedure

1. Locate the carrier in the tank against the inventory; confirm identity (Section 8, checkpoint W1) and remove it, keeping it under LN₂/cold until the moment of warming.
2. At the workstation, confirm carrier identity against the recovery dish; perform the **EW identity match** (checkpoint W2).
3. **Immediately** immerse the device tip / unload the specimen directly into the pre-warmed **thawing solution (TS)**; start the timer. Speed at this first step is critical to avoid ice formation on rewarming.
4. Move the specimen sequentially through the **dilution (DS)** and **wash (WS)** solutions for the exposure times specified in the kit IFU, observing osmotic behaviour.
5. Transfer the warmed specimen into the pre-equilibrated culture dish; perform **EW match** (checkpoint W3) before returning to the incubator.
6. Assess survival immediately and, for blastocysts, re-assess re-expansion after the locally defined recovery interval 🔧 (Appendix A).
7. Record the carrier and its inventory position as **emptied**; update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016).
8. Record number warmed, number surviving, re-expansion status, kit lot, times and the assessment against the survival threshold on the warming record (ELB-LOG-015).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at retrieval from the tank and at every identification and transfer step (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Carrier removed from the tank matches the patient and the recorded position | Inventory cross-check + label read |
| **W2** | Carrier and recovery dish belong to the same couple before warming | EW match (RI Witness) |
| **W3** | Warmed specimen placed into the correctly labelled recovery dish | EW match + second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure before warming begins; once a carrier is committed to thawing solution it cannot be returned to storage. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity first. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Survival:** assessed against the locally defined post-warming survival threshold 🔧 and, for blastocysts, the re-expansion criteria 🔧 (Appendix A).
- A specimen meeting the survival criterion proceeds to culture/transfer; one failing it is recorded as non-surviving and the treating clinician is informed.
- If survival in a cohort falls below the local alert level 🔧 (Appendix A), hold and escalate for review of the warming process and the source freeze.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP — exposure outside the IFU window, EW downtime, delayed first immersion, wrong-carrier retrieval, or survival below the alert level — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Significant clinical events follow the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- Warming record (survival and re-expansion assessment) — **ELB-LOG-015**.
- Cryostorage inventory update (position emptied) — **ELB-LOG-016**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The warming solution temperature, the immediate first-immersion step and the dilution exposure times are **guideline-anchored** to the validated kit IFU and ESHRE 2015. Values tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)** include: the post-warming embryo survival threshold and re-expansion criteria 🔧, the post-warming oocyte survival threshold 🔧, the blastocyst recovery/re-expansion assessment interval 🔧, the post-warming recovery dish conditions, and the cohort survival alert level 🔧. Each locally defined value is flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requires Medical Director sign-off.

## 14. Teaching point

Warming is vitrification run backwards, and the first second is the one that counts: the carrier must meet pre-warmed thawing solution without delay, because ice that did not form on cooling can still form on a slow rewarm. After that, the osmotic ladder is gentle but exacting. Survival is not a yes/no judged by eye in haste — it is measured against criteria that the laboratory has validated and the Medical Director has signed, and for a blastocyst the truer signal is re-expansion in the hours that follow.
