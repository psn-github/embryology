## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for conventional in-vitro insemination — co-incubating motile prepared spermatozoa with cumulus-intact oocytes so that fertilisation occurs by natural sperm–oocyte interaction — performed consistently, at the correct sperm concentration, and with full traceability of both gamete sources.

## 2. Scope

Applies to conventional insemination of oocytes retrieved from, and spermatozoa belonging to, the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only). Used where semen parameters and clinical indication are suitable for standard IVF; cases requiring micromanipulation are inseminated by ICSI (ELB-SOP-027). Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — prepares the insemination dishes, calculates and delivers the sperm aliquot, and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent (ELB-SOP-005).
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms that oocytes and sperm belong to the same couple at the point of insemination (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms the insemination decision (conventional vs ICSI), reviews fertilisation outcomes and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| COC | Cumulus–oocyte complex — inseminated cumulus-intact in conventional IVF |
| Insemination concentration | Motile sperm added per oocyte or per droplet/dish |
| 2PN | Two pronuclei — normal fertilisation marker (assessed under ELB-SOP-028) |
| Progressive motility | Forward-moving sperm, the fraction relevant to insemination |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Pre-equilibrated insemination dishes (droplets under oil or organ-culture dishes) of culture medium — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Prepared progressively motile sperm suspension at known concentration (ELB-SOP-019 ejaculated; ELB-SOP-020 frozen as indicated).
- Stereomicroscope and inverted microscope with heated stage/surface; warmed transfer pipettes.
- Haemocytometer or counting chamber for the post-preparation motile count; tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** All media, oil and consumables are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and that the insemination method for the cohort is conventional IVF, authorised by the Laboratory Lead.
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); confirm insemination dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Confirm the prepared sperm sample is labelled to the partner, its motile concentration is known, and it matches the couple (ELB-SOP-019/020).
4. Determine the insemination interval after retrieval and the motile sperm concentration per oocyte from **Appendix A** 🔧 (per ESHRE timing windows).
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — two gamete sources meet here.** Do not bring oocytes and sperm to the same workstation until both have passed an identity match (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016). Two patients' gametes are never open at one workstation simultaneously.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm patient oocytes and partner sperm at the workstation; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Calculate the volume of prepared suspension needed to deliver the locally defined motile sperm concentration 🔧 per oocyte/droplet (**Appendix A**).
3. Gently mix the prepared sperm suspension to ensure an even motile fraction.
4. Add the calculated sperm aliquot to each insemination droplet/dish containing the COCs, dispensing below the oil and avoiding disturbance of the complexes.
5. Confirm sperm are present and motile in each droplet/dish under the microscope.
6. Perform an **EW match** before returning the inseminated dishes to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
7. Record the insemination time, sperm concentration delivered, number of oocytes inseminated, dish/incubator position, media and sperm lots, and operator on the conventional insemination record (**ELB-LOG-006**).
8. Plan the fertilisation check for the window defined in **Appendix A** (16–18 h; per Istanbul/ESHRE) under ELB-SOP-028.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where gametes are identified, combined or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient oocytes and partner sperm belong to the same couple before insemination | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Inseminated dishes returned to the correctly labelled incubator position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Proceed with conventional insemination** where semen parameters meet the local threshold for standard IVF 🔧 (**Appendix A**) and the cohort is so authorised.
- **Convert to ICSI** (ELB-SOP-027) where the recovered motile concentration falls below that threshold, on the Laboratory Lead's decision; record the rationale.
- Inseminated oocytes are not graded for maturity at this stage; cumulus is intact. Maturity and fertilisation are assessed downstream.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. EW downtime, an out-of-range sperm concentration delivered, insemination outside the timing window) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Total or unexpectedly low fertilisation is reviewed and, where appropriate, handled as an adverse event.

## 11. Records generated

- Conventional insemination record — **ELB-LOG-006**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**, initiated for the cohort.
- Fertilisation assessment (next day) — **ELB-SOP-028**, recorded on ELB-LOG-007.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Insemination-relevant entries include: the insemination timing window after retrieval, the motile sperm concentration per oocyte 🔧, the semen-parameter threshold for choosing conventional IVF over ICSI 🔧, dish equilibration time, and the fertilisation-check window (16–18 h). Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / WHO manual) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Conventional insemination succeeds or fails on dose and timing: too few motile sperm and fertilisation suffers, too many and polyspermy and oxidative stress rise. The embryologist's judgement is in the calculation — translating the prepared motile concentration into the right number of sperm per oocyte — and in inseminating within the window. Because two gamete sources are combined for the first time, the witness check is the safeguard against the one error that cannot be undone.
