## 1. Purpose

To define how the laboratory pH meter is calibrated and used to measure the pH of culture and handling media, so that the gas conditions of incubators can be confirmed to deliver the target media pH and any drift is detected before it affects gametes or embryos. Media pH is the physiologically relevant endpoint that CO₂ control exists to achieve.

## 2. Scope

Applies to calibration and use of the pH meter for measuring the pH of culture and buffered handling media supporting autologous gamete and embryo work in the Embryology Laboratory. Covers two-point calibration against standard buffers, temperature-compensated measurement, and the use of pH results to confirm incubator CO₂ adequacy (ELB-SOP-039). Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Embryologist on duty** — calibrates the meter, measures media pH per this SOP, records results on the relevant QC log and signs the entry.
- **Quality Manager** — ensures buffers are in date, electrodes are maintained, and the meter's calibration/service status is current (ELB-LOG-020).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews pH results against the acceptable range and investigates drift.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| pH | Measure of acidity/alkalinity; for culture media the physiologically relevant control endpoint |
| Two-point calibration | Calibration of the meter against two standard buffers that bracket the measurement range |
| Buffer | A solution of known, stable pH used to calibrate the meter (here 4.0 and 7.0) |
| ATC | Automatic temperature compensation — correction of the reading for sample temperature |
| Slope | The electrode's response, expressed against the theoretical ideal; an indicator of electrode health |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- pH meter with electrode suitable for small-volume, temperature-controlled measurement and a current service status.
- Standard calibration buffers **4.0 and 7.0** (add 10.0 only if the working range requires it), within their open-container shelf life.
- Temperature probe / ATC, and a means of measuring at the media use temperature (**37.0 °C**) where the method requires it.
- Rinse (purified water), lint-free wipes; calibration/QC log — ELB-LOG-020; results captured per Appendix A on the relevant QC record.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Calibrate and measure per the meter and electrode IFU, including buffer temperature, electrode conditioning/storage and rinse-between-readings technique. Measure media pH under the conditions the media IFU specifies (temperature and, where applicable, gas-equilibrated state); pH measured outside those conditions is not comparable to the acceptance range.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm buffers **4.0 / 7.0** are in date and not contaminated; pour fresh aliquots, never measure into the stock bottle.
2. Confirm the electrode is hydrated, intact and stored correctly; condition per IFU if it was dry.
3. Confirm the meter's service status is current (ELB-LOG-020) and ATC/probe is connected.
4. Allow buffers and sample to reach the specified measurement temperature.

## 7. Procedure

**Calibration (two-point)**

1. Rinse the electrode with purified water and blot (do not wipe the bulb).
2. Place the electrode in buffer **7.0**; allow the reading to stabilise and accept the point.
3. Rinse and blot; place in buffer **4.0**; allow to stabilise and accept the point.
4. Record the resulting **slope** and confirm it is within the IFU-acceptable range; if not, recondition or replace the electrode and recalibrate.
5. Optionally verify with a third buffer or a fresh aliquot of 7.0 and record the reading.

**Measurement**

6. Rinse and blot the electrode between every sample.
7. Measure the media pH under the defined conditions (temperature, and gas-equilibrated state where required by the media IFU); allow to stabilise and record.
8. Compare against the acceptable media pH range **7.2–7.4** (ESHRE 2015). Record the result, the meter calibration time/slope, the media lot, and sign.
9. Use the pH result to confirm incubator CO₂ adequacy (ELB-SOP-039); if pH is out of range, follow Section 10.
10. Recalibrate at the start of each measurement session and at the locally defined interval 🔧 (Appendix A); store the electrode per IFU after use.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

pH measurement and calibration do not require a second-person identity witness; every record must be **signed and timed** and tie to the meter calibration in force (ELB-SOP-001, ISO 15189).

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Media pH outside **7.2–7.4** is a stop condition. Confirm the meter calibration and electrode slope are valid (to exclude a measurement artefact), then treat an in-range, confirmed out-of-spec media pH as an incubator gas fault: hold the affected chamber (ELB-SOP-039) and do not culture in suspect conditions until pH is reconfirmed in range.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Two-point calibration valid; slope within IFU range | 4.0 / 7.0 buffer calibration on ELB-LOG-020 |
| **C2** | Media pH is within 7.2–7.4 under defined conditions | Recorded pH measurement |
| **C3** | Result reviewed and signed | QC log entry |

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** a measurement when calibration is current, the slope is within range, the reading is stable and taken under the defined conditions.
- **Reject** the measurement when the slope is out of range, buffers are expired/contaminated, or the reading will not stabilise. **Reject** the media/chamber when a validly measured pH falls outside 7.2–7.4.

## 10. Deviations

An out-of-range media pH, an out-of-range electrode slope, or use of expired buffers is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). For confirmed out-of-spec media pH, investigate incubator CO₂ (ELB-SOP-039/040) and escalate per ELB-POL-005 if culture material may have been affected. Recondition or replace the electrode for slope failures and recalibrate before reuse.

## 11. Records generated

- pH meter calibration record (buffers, slope, due date) — **ELB-LOG-020**.
- Media pH results used to confirm incubator gas — **ELB-LOG-018**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The acceptable media pH range **7.2–7.4** and the measurement temperature **37.0 °C** are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015); calibration buffers **4.0 / 7.0** are standard practice. The calibration **frequency/interval**, the acceptable electrode **slope range** beyond the IFU minimum, and the media pH measurement schedule are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**). Calibration traceability is maintained per ISO 15189.

## 14. Teaching point

CO₂ is the knob; pH is the result that matters. A pH meter lets the laboratory measure the thing the embryo actually senses, but only when the electrode is healthy and freshly calibrated against bracketing buffers. A drifting electrode can manufacture a crisis or hide one — so before you trust a media pH, check the slope, and before you blame the incubator, exclude the meter.
