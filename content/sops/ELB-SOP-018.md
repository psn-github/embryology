## 1. Purpose

To define how a semen sample is received, identified, recorded and tracked from the moment it reaches the Embryology Laboratory until it is released for preparation, so that every sample is unambiguously linked to the correct patient, its condition at receipt is documented, and an unbroken chain of custody is maintained.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all semen samples produced by the **male partner of the patient couple** (autologous) for laboratory use — diagnostic semen analysis, sperm preparation for IUI/IVF/ICSI, and cryopreservation. Covers samples produced on site and samples produced off site and transported in. Donor samples are out of scope and are not accepted. Surgical/testicular specimens are received under ELB-SOP-021.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Receiving embryologist / andrology technologist** — confirms identity, records receipt, assesses sample acceptability and starts the tracking record.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity and label match at receipt (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — adjudicates borderline acceptance decisions and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Liquefaction | Post-ejaculation breakdown of seminal coagulum to a homogeneous fluid, required before analysis or preparation |
| Receipt window | Locally defined maximum interval from production to laboratory receipt for an off-site sample (🔧 Appendix A) |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |
| Chain of custody | The documented, unbroken record of who held the sample and when (ELB-SOP-017) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Pre-labelled, sterile, non-toxic, wide-mouth collection container (manufacturer-validated for semen).
- Laboratory-supplied collection labels and the requesting clinician's order.
- Heated surface / incubator maintained at the liquefaction temperature setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- Timer; RI Witness reader at the receipt bench.
- Semen receipt log (**ELB-LOG-001**) and analysis worksheet (**ELB-LOG-002**).

> **IFU cross-reference.** Use only collection containers confirmed non-toxic to spermatozoa per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Do not decant a sample into a non-validated vessel.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm a current laboratory order exists for this patient and the requested test/procedure.
2. Confirm the patient produced the sample after the abstinence interval advised by the clinician, and record it (per **WHO** semen manual guidance).
3. Confirm the container is intact, correctly labelled and uncontaminated externally.
4. For an off-site sample, confirm time of production and transport conditions against the **receipt window** and temperature guidance (🔧 **Appendix A**).

> **CRITICAL — identity at receipt.** Do not accept or open a sample until patient identity on the container, the request and the patient's stated details have been matched and witnessed (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). An unlabelled or mismatched container is not accepted.

## 7. Procedure

1. Greet and positively identify the patient using at least two identifiers (ELB-SOP-016).
2. Confirm the label on the container against the patient and the order; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
3. Record receipt in **ELB-LOG-001**: patient identifiers, date/time of production, date/time of receipt, abstinence interval, production site (on/off site), container condition, and the receiving operator.
4. Record any production difficulty reported by the patient (incomplete sample, spillage, second container) — this affects interpretation.
5. Place the sample on the heated surface and allow liquefaction; start the timer and note the time. Assess liquefaction against the time guidance in **Appendix A** (🔧 if locally adjusted).
6. On full liquefaction, assess gross acceptability (Section 9). Record the assessment.
7. Assign and apply the laboratory tracking identifier; link it to the patient record and to the downstream worksheet (**ELB-LOG-002** for analysis, **ELB-LOG-003** for preparation).
8. Hand over to analysis, preparation or cryopreservation only after a documented custody transfer (ELB-SOP-017).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at receipt and at every custody transfer (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity vs container label vs order at receipt | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Tracking identifier correctly applied and linked to the patient record | EW / second-person |
| **W3** | Custody transfer to analysis / preparation / cryo matches the same patient | EW match (ELB-SOP-017) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts receipt. Do not open, analyse or prepare the sample until identity is resolved and re-witnessed. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** when: identity is matched and witnessed; the container is intact and correctly labelled; the sample is within the **receipt window** (🔧 Appendix A) for off-site production; and liquefaction is achieved within the time guidance (Appendix A).
- **Reject / escalate** when: identity cannot be confirmed; the container is unlabelled, leaking or visibly contaminated; production-to-receipt time exceeds the **receipt window** (🔧 Appendix A); or liquefaction fails within the allowed time.
- A rejected or compromised sample is recorded with the reason; the treating clinician is informed and a repeat sample is arranged. The patient is not penalised for a laboratory-side delay.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP — a late off-site sample, failed liquefaction, partial sample, EW downtime, or a labelling discrepancy — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Identity events are also reviewed under the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- Semen receipt log — **ELB-LOG-001**.
- Semen analysis worksheet — **ELB-LOG-002** (when analysis follows).
- Custody transfer entries — per **ELB-SOP-017**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Semen analysis reference values are anchored to the current **WHO** laboratory manual edition. The **sample liquefaction / receipt window** and the liquefaction surface temperature are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off). Abstinence guidance follows the **WHO** manual.

## 14. Teaching point

Receipt is where the chain of custody begins, and a chain is only as strong as its first link. The two facts that decide everything downstream — *whose* sample this is and *what condition* it arrived in — are both fixed at the bench in the first minutes. Get the identity match and the receipt record right, and every later step inherits a clean provenance; get them wrong, and no amount of careful preparation can recover it.
