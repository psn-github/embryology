## Purpose

To describe the procedure of denudation of oocytes using Hyase. This includes consumables/equipment required, laboratory witnessing steps, the denudation procedure and the culture of oocytes post denudation. To understand the process of oocyte denudation prior to ICSI or Vitrification.

## Scope

Applicable to all laboratory staff deemed competent to perform Oocyte Denudation.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

## Definitions

Hyase Hyaluronidase

MII Metaphase two

MI Metaphase one

GV Germinal Vesicle

## Procedure

### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   Glass Pasteur pipette
-   Bung
-   Pre pulled Pipettes (290-310 µm and 130-145µm)
-   Marker Pen
-   Stop watch
-   Clinical Waste Bin
-   Needles for dissection (optional)

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   40mm Hyase Dishes (200µl Hyase drops plus 3 x 50µl GMOPS+ wash drops)
-   40mm G1+ Post Denudation Dish (40 µl G1+ drops)
-   RI Witness Tags

### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the denudation procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point.

### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B)

Before beginning the denudation process check the laboratory notes to ensure that ‘ICSI’ or ‘Oocyte Vitrification’ is the agreed treatment.

Check whether the patient has elected to have a 50:50 IVF:ICSI split, if so ensure only the designated number of oocytes are denuded.

Check whether the patient has elected to have a 50:50 Oocyte Vitrification:fresh culture split, if so ensure only the designated number of oocytes are denuded.

Witnessing for denudation can be performed alongside RI witness.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the Hyase dishes and the 5 well oocyte collection dishes matches that on the laboratory notes and RI witness system.

Following denudation, check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the Hyase dishes and the G1+ Post Denudation dish matches that on the laboratory notes and RI witness system.

Approve these checks on the RI witness system and sign for these checks.

### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare Hyase dishes a minimum of 1 hour prior to use and warm in an ungassed incubator. Prepare one Hyase dish per 12 oocytes (oocytes are denuded in batches of up to 6).

Prepare G1+ Post Denudation dish the afternoon before the patient’s oocyte collection and equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight. Prepare one dish per patient (if it is anticipated that more than 24 oocytes will be collected, prepare a second G1+ dish).

### 5.5 Timing of Denudation Procedure

Perform denudation prior to oocyte vitrification 2 hours post oocyte collection ± 30 minutes.

Perform denudation prior to ICSI 3 hours post oocyte collection ± 30 minutes.

### 5.6 Denudation Procedure

Consideration must be given to maintaining temperature, pH and osmolality during the denudation procedure, therefore oil covered GMOPS+ dishes are used. The procedure should be performed as quickly as practically possible to minimise the exposure of oocytes outside the incubator environment.

Using a pasteur pipette, place up to 6 oocytes in the Hyase droplet. Gently aspirate up and down to disperse the cumulus cells. Oocytes must spend no longer than 30 seconds in the Hyase solution and this must be measured using a timer to ensure accuracy.

Using a pre-pulled 290-310µm pipette, collect the oocytes from the Hyase and place in the first GMOPS+ wash drop. Aspirate gently up and down using the pre-pulled pipette to remove any remaining cumulus cells. Transfer the oocytes to the second wash drop. Aim to minimise the time spent in the first wash drop where there will be some carryover of hyase.

Using a pre pulled 130-145µm pipette, gently aspirate each oocyte up and down to remove the coronal cells.

If it is not possible to remove the surrounding cells by pipetting, needles can be used to dissect away the cells. Care must be taken not to damage the oocyte.

Once all the coronal cells have been removed as far as practically possible, transfer the oocytes to the third GMOPS+ wash droplet for a final rinse.

Assess the maturity status of the oocytes whilst in the final drop and sort into 3 groups:

-   MII
-   MI
-   GV + any other oocytes not suitable for ICSI/Vitrification

Whilst maintaining the pre-sorted groups, transfer the denuded oocytes to the G1+ Post Denudation culture dish.

Prime the pipette with G1+ from the first wash drop. Wash within the first drop. Then prime with G1+ from the second wash drop and wash within. Repeat for the third wash drop. Once washed, separate the oocytes into droplets based on maturity.

Culture a maximum of 4 oocytes in each drop. Label the drops with the stage of maturity of the oocytes within them. Return the G1+ Post Denudation dish to a gassed incubator.

Repeat the procedure above until all the oocytes have been denuded and transferred to the G1+ Post Denudation dish. Mark the wash drops by crossing through with a line under the dish to prevent further use. Note on the lid of the dish the total number of oocytes contained within.

Return the G1\+ Post denudation culture dish to a gassed incubator until ICSI/Vitrification.

Clear the flowhood of any used consumables. Use Oosafe spray ± sterile water to clean the flowhood.

Complete the laboratory records.

## Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

Maximum time oocytes are in Hyase 30 seconds

Damage to oocytes <5%

Maturity Rate 75-90%
