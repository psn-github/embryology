## 1. Purpose

To define the laboratory method for trophectoderm biopsy of blastocysts for preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) — the removal of a small number of trophectoderm cells, their tubing and labelling for the genetics laboratory, and the maintenance of unambiguous traceability between each biopsy specimen and its embryo of origin.

## 2. Scope

Covers the laboratory handling of biopsy for PGT on blastocysts derived from the **patient couple** (autologous embryos only): zona breaching, trophectoderm removal, specimen tubing, labelling and chain of custody to the genetics laboratory. Cleavage-stage and polar-body biopsy are not performed as default. Genetic analysis and result interpretation are performed by the genetics laboratory and are outside this SOP. Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — performs the biopsy, tubes and labels each specimen, and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent in micromanipulation/laser biopsy (ELB-SOP-005) before performing unsupervised.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms embryo identity and, critically, the embryo-to-tube linkage at tubing (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms indication and consent, oversees chain of custody to the genetics laboratory (ELB-SOP-017) and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| PGT | Preimplantation genetic testing |
| TE biopsy | Removal of a small number of trophectoderm cells from a blastocyst |
| Tubing | Transfer of the biopsied cells into a labelled PCR tube |
| Chain of custody | Documented, witnessed transfer of the specimen to the genetics laboratory |
| Blank / negative control | Empty wash drop tubed to detect contamination, where required by the genetics protocol |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted microscope with heated stage, calibrated diode laser, and micromanipulators with holding and biopsy pipettes.
- Pre-equilibrated buffered (HEPES/MOPS) handling medium under oil; biopsy performed in buffered medium per protocol — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Labelled PCR tubes and the wash/loading buffer specified by the genetics-laboratory protocol; fresh pipette per specimen to prevent cross-contamination.
- Tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**); RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Laser settings, the wash/loading buffer, tube type and volume follow the genetics-laboratory protocol and the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots or buffers mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist, the PGT indication and that consent covers biopsy and genetic testing (ELB-POL-009).
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); confirm dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Confirm the genetics-laboratory tubing protocol, buffer and tube labelling convention; pre-label one tube per embryo with the embryo identifier and patient identifiers (ELB-SOP-016).
4. Confirm laser calibration and that fresh single-use pipettes are available — one per embryo.
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — one embryo, one tube, one identity.** The single irreducible requirement of biopsy is that each tubed specimen is unambiguously linked to one embryo. Tube one embryo's cells at a time; change pipette between embryos; do not open a second embryo's tube before the first is sealed and witnessed.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm patient embryos at the workstation; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Select a blastocyst meeting the biopsy criteria (expansion and grade per ELB-SOP-029 and local protocol 🔧).
3. Breach the zona by laser over cell-free space (technique per ELB-SOP-030 where prior hatching applies), creating an aperture sized for trophectoderm herniation.
4. Immobilise the blastocyst on the holding pipette; aspirate a small number of herniating trophectoderm cells into the biopsy pipette.
5. Excise the cells by the validated method (laser-assisted and/or mechanical), removing only the agreed cell number 🔧 and avoiding the inner cell mass.
6. Wash the biopsied cells through the genetics-protocol buffer to clear carry-over, then **tube** them into the pre-labelled PCR tube in the specified volume.
7. Perform the **tubing witness** — confirm embryo identity, tube label and the embryo→tube linkage (checkpoint W2) — then seal the tube. Tube any required blank/negative control per the genetics protocol.
8. Change to a fresh pipette and repeat for the next embryo; never reuse a pipette between embryos.
9. Return the biopsied blastocyst to a fresh, labelled, equilibrated dish (typically for immediate vitrification under the cryopreservation SOP pending result); perform an **EW match** before returning to the incubator/cryo workflow (checkpoint W3).
10. Reconcile the tube set against the embryo list; package and hand over to the genetics laboratory under chain of custody (ELB-SOP-017), with a witnessed transfer (checkpoint W4).
11. Record the embryos biopsied, cell numbers, tube identifiers, buffer/lot, controls, operator and the chain-of-custody handover on the embryo biopsy record (**ELB-LOG-011**) and the culture record (**ELB-LOG-009**).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where embryos/specimens are identified, separated or moved (ELB-SOP-002). The tubing checkpoint is the most critical in the suite: it fixes the link between a genetic result and an embryo. RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of embryos presented for biopsy vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Biopsied cells tubed into the correct embryo's pre-labelled tube (embryo→tube linkage) | EW match + second-person read, per specimen |
| **W3** | Biopsied embryo returned to the correctly labelled dish/cryo position | EW match |
| **W4** | Tube set vs embryo list reconciled; witnessed handover to genetics (chain of custody) | EW / second-person, ELB-SOP-017 |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. A mis-tubed specimen mislinks a genetic result to the wrong embryo and is irrecoverable — do not proceed on assumption. Resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Biopsy** blastocysts meeting the expansion/grade criteria 🔧 (**Appendix A**); remove only the agreed trophectoderm cell number 🔧, avoiding the ICM.
- Embryos not meeting criteria are recorded and managed per local policy; embryos failing to herniate adequately are reviewed before any repeat attempt.
- A tubing specimen that cannot be unambiguously linked to one embryo is rejected and the biopsy reviewed; it is not sent to genetics.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. EW downtime, suspected cross-contamination, an unlinkable tube, ICM contact, chain-of-custody break) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). A mis-link or specimen mix-up is a reportable adverse event.

## 11. Records generated

- Embryo biopsy record (embryos, cells, tube IDs, controls, custody) — **ELB-LOG-011**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**.
- Chain-of-custody handover to genetics — under **ELB-SOP-017**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Biopsy-relevant entries include: the blastocyst expansion/grade biopsy criteria 🔧, the trophectoderm cell number removed 🔧, laser settings (per IFU/genetics protocol), dish equilibration time, and the maximum embryo time outside the incubator per batch 🔧. Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / Istanbul Consensus) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Biopsy is, above all, a traceability procedure that happens to involve micromanipulation. The cells matter — take few, spare the inner cell mass, breach the zona over empty space — but the irreducible discipline is that one embryo's cells go into one labelled tube, witnessed, before the next embryo is opened, and a fresh pipette is used every time. A clean biopsy with a broken embryo-to-tube link is worse than no biopsy: it returns a confident genetic answer about the wrong embryo.
