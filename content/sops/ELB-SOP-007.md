## 1. Purpose

To define how media, oil, plasticware, reagents and other consumables are specified, received, quarantined, released, stored, issued, tracked and withdrawn, so that only suitable, in-date, lot-traceable materials contact a patient's gametes and embryos. Consumables are a direct determinant of embryo health; this SOP ensures every item used is fit for purpose and fully traceable to its lot.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all consumables and reagents used in procedures on the **patient couple's own gametes and embryos** (autologous only) — culture and handling media, oil overlay, cryoprotectants, dishes, tubes, pipettes, needles and tags. Covers ordering, receipt and goods-in checks, quarantine and release, storage, stock rotation, lot traceability to cycles, expiry control, and recall handling (ELB-POL-004). Donor and surrogacy content is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Inventory custodian / Laboratory Lead** — maintains the inventory, manages stock levels, and controls quarantine and release.
- **Receiving staff** — perform goods-in checks against the order and the IFU, and record receipt.
- **Performing embryologist** — uses only released, in-date stock and records the lot/expiry of each consumable against the cycle (ELB-SOP-001).
- **Quality Manager** — audits lot traceability, expiry control and recall actions.
- **Medical Director** — approves the local minimum stock levels, quarantine criteria and storage conditions (**Appendix A**).

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Lot / batch | A defined production run identified by a lot number, with its own expiry |
| Goods-in check | Verification at receipt that the item, lot, quantity, condition and cold-chain match the order and IFU |
| Quarantine | Holding received stock as unavailable until released for use |
| Release | Authorisation that a lot meets requirements and may be used |
| FEFO | First-Expiry-First-Out stock rotation |
| Traceability | The link from each lot to the cycles in which it was used |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Controlled storage: refrigerated, ambient and any controlled-condition storage with monitored temperature (ELB-SOP-006/038).
- The inventory system or stock register, and the cycle records that capture lot/expiry.
- Manufacturer IFUs, certificates of analysis where supplied, and the recall/product-alert log (**ELB-LOG-024**).

> **IFU cross-reference.** Storage temperature, cold-chain on receipt, equilibration and shelf life follow each item's manufacturer IFU and Appendix A. Do not use an item stored or transported outside its IFU conditions.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the item is **released** stock, not quarantined.
2. Confirm the item is **in date** and physically intact (seal, packaging, clarity of media, no visible contamination).
3. Confirm storage conditions have been maintained (cold-chain/temperature record where applicable).
4. Confirm the lot is not subject to an open recall or product alert (ELB-LOG-024, ELB-POL-004).

> **CRITICAL — released and in date only.** Never use quarantined, expired, recalled or compromised consumables on patient material. If in any doubt about an item's history or condition, quarantine it and escalate.

## 7. Procedure

1. **Order** against defined specifications and minimum stock levels (**Appendix A** 🔧).
2. **Receive and check.** On delivery, verify item, lot, quantity, expiry, condition and cold-chain against the order and IFU. Record receipt; for non-conforming deliveries, quarantine and raise a deviation.
3. **Quarantine** newly received stock until release criteria are met (**Appendix A** 🔧), e.g. documentation present, cold-chain intact, certificate of analysis where required.
4. **Release** the lot for use; mark it available in the inventory.
5. **Store** under the IFU/Appendix A conditions in monitored storage; protect light-sensitive media as specified.
6. **Rotate** stock by **FEFO** so the earliest expiry is used first.
7. **Issue and record use.** At the bench, record the lot number and expiry of each consumable against the cycle/record (ELB-SOP-001) so every item is traceable to the patient it was used for.
8. **Control expiry.** Periodically check stock for approaching expiry; withdraw and dispose of expired items so they cannot be used.
9. **Handle recalls.** On a manufacturer recall/product alert, quarantine the affected lots immediately, record action in the recall log (ELB-LOG-024), and follow ELB-POL-004.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Inventory handling does not itself combine patient material, so it has no gamete-identity witness checkpoint. The control point is lot integrity and traceability.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Item is released, in date, intact and not recalled before use | Goods-in / pre-use check (this SOP) |
| **C2** | Lot/expiry of each consumable is recorded against the cycle | Cycle record entry (ELB-SOP-001) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Lot traceability is not optional. If a lot is later recalled or implicated in an event, the laboratory must be able to identify every cycle it touched — which is only possible if the lot was recorded at the time of use.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

A consumable is **accepted for use** when it is released, in date, intact, stored within specification, and not under recall. It is **rejected** when expired, quarantined, recalled, damaged, or of uncertain storage history; rejected stock is segregated and disposed of so it cannot re-enter use.

## 10. Deviations

A non-conforming delivery, a cold-chain breach, use of expired/quarantined stock, a missing lot record, or a recall is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and logged in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). A recall affecting material already used is handled per ELB-POL-004 and, where patient material may be affected, as an adverse event (ELB-SOP-004).

## 11. Records generated

- Inventory / stock register with lot, expiry, quarantine and release status.
- Goods-in receipt records.
- Lot/expiry captured against each cycle record (ELB-SOP-001).
- Recall / product-alert log — **ELB-LOG-024**.
- Any related deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The **requirement** for fit-for-purpose, lot-traceable, in-date consumables with controlled storage is **guideline-anchored** (ISO 15189; ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022). The local **minimum stock levels**, **quarantine/release criteria**, **storage conditions and monitoring**, and **expiry-review frequency** are **locally defined** and recorded in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off). Item-specific storage and shelf life follow each manufacturer IFU.

## 14. Teaching point

The consumable that harms an embryo rarely looks wrong — it is expired, mis-stored, or from a lot that will be recalled next month. The defences are simple and unglamorous: use only released, in-date stock; rotate by expiry; and record the lot every single time. That last habit feels like overhead until the day a recall lands, when it is the only thing standing between you and not knowing which patients were exposed.
