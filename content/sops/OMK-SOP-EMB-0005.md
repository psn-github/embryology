## 1. Purpose

To define how deviations from approved procedures, specifications and setpoints are detected, recorded, assessed, contained and closed out through corrective and preventive action (CAPA). This SOP operationalises the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and is a core element of the ISO 15189 quality management system: it turns a single departure from procedure into a documented, analysed and prevented event.

## 2. Scope

Applies to any departure from an approved SOP, a specification, an Appendix A setpoint, a manufacturer IFU, or the planned course of a cycle, in work on the **patient couple's own gametes and embryos** (autologous only). Covers nonconformities found in process, in QC, in equipment monitoring, or on audit. Events that harm or risk harm to a patient or their material are also handled as adverse events (OMK-POL-EMB-0004, OMK-POL-EMB-0004). Donor and surrogacy content is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Any staff member** — reports a deviation as soon as it is identified, and takes immediate safe containment action.
- **Performing embryologist** — records the deviation, the material affected and the immediate action taken.
- **Quality Manager** — triages severity, assigns CAPA owners, tracks actions to closure, and verifies effectiveness.
- **Laboratory Lead** — authorises containment decisions and root-cause analysis where indicated (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).
- **Medical Director** — reviews significant deviations and approves the local severity grading and timelines (**Appendix A**).

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Deviation / nonconformity | A departure from an approved procedure, specification or setpoint |
| Containment | Immediate action to limit impact and protect affected material |
| Correction | The immediate fix to the specific problem |
| Corrective action | Action to eliminate the cause of an existing nonconformity and prevent recurrence |
| Preventive action | Action to eliminate the cause of a potential nonconformity before it occurs |
| CAPA | The combined corrective/preventive action process |
| RCA | Root-cause analysis (OMK-POL-EMB-0004) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

Not applicable as a bench procedure. The tools of this SOP are the deviation log (**OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**), the CAPA tracker, and the relevant procedural records (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the affected material and its identity are secured before further handling (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).
2. Confirm whether the event is in progress (needs containment now) or already occurred (needs recording and assessment).
3. Confirm whether patient material is, or may be, harmed — if so, also open the adverse-event pathway (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).

> **CRITICAL — contain first.** Protect the patient's gametes and embryos before completing paperwork. Stabilise, re-store or quarantine affected material, then record.

## 7. Procedure

1. **Detect and contain.** On identifying a deviation, take immediate safe action to limit impact (e.g. return material to a compliant incubator, quarantine a suspect media lot, isolate failed equipment).
2. **Record.** Open a deviation entry in the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005): what happened, when, who, the material/cycle affected, the setpoint or step departed from, and the immediate correction taken.
3. **Notify.** Inform the Laboratory Lead and Quality Manager. For any patient-impact event, notify per the adverse-events policy without delay (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).
4. **Assess severity.** The Quality Manager grades the deviation against the local severity scale (**Appendix A** 🔧), which sets the response and closure timeline.
5. **Investigate cause.** For minor, single-cause events, record the immediate cause. For significant, recurrent or patient-impact events, conduct root-cause analysis (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).
6. **Define CAPA.** Specify corrective action (eliminate the cause of this event) and, where applicable, preventive action (eliminate the cause of similar potential events). Assign an owner and a due date.
7. **Implement.** Carry out the actions; update documents, training or setpoints as required and route changes through document control (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).
8. **Verify effectiveness.** The Quality Manager confirms the action worked — the nonconformity has not recurred within the defined verification window (**Appendix A** 🔧) — before closing.
9. **Close out.** Record closure, effectiveness evidence, and any residual risk in the deviation log.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Where containment involves moving or re-storing patient material, the identity witness checkpoints apply (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Identity of any material moved or quarantined during containment | EW match (RI Witness) or second-person check |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A deviation must never be closed by simply correcting the immediate symptom. Closure requires that the cause has been addressed and effectiveness verified, or the same event will recur.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

A CAPA is **acceptable for closure** when the action is implemented, documented, and verified effective with no recurrence in the defined window. It is **not closed** while actions are outstanding, effectiveness is unverified, or the root cause remains open for a significant event.

## 10. Deviations

This SOP *is* the deviation pathway. Failure to record a known deviation is itself a nonconformity and is escalated to the Quality Manager and Medical Director. Patient-impact events follow OMK-POL-EMB-0004 and OMK-POL-EMB-0004 in parallel.

## 11. Records generated

- Deviation log entry, including severity grade, CAPA and closure — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.
- CAPA tracker entries and effectiveness-verification evidence.
- Updated controlled documents arising from CAPA (via OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).
- Adverse-event report where applicable — **OMK-POL-EMB-0004**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The **requirement** to identify, control, analyse and prevent nonconformities is **guideline-anchored** (ISO 15189; ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022). The local **severity grading scale**, the **response and closure timelines**, and the **effectiveness-verification window** are **locally defined** and recorded in **Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A)** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

A deviation is information. Handled well, one near miss prevents many failures; handled as paperwork, it teaches the laboratory nothing and guarantees a repeat. The discipline is to separate the three responses — correct the immediate problem, address the cause, and verify it stayed fixed — and never to confuse the first with the last. The deviation that no one wrote down is the one that comes back.
