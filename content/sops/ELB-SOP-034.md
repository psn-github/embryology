## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for cryopreserving mature oocytes by vitrification, so that a couple's own oocytes are stored — for fertility preservation or cycle management — with maximal post-warming survival and full traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the vitrification of mature (metaphase II) oocytes belonging to the **patient** (autologous). Immature oocytes are not routinely vitrified. Embryo vitrification is covered separately (ELB-SOP-033) and warming is covered separately (ELB-SOP-035). Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope and is not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — must be trained and signed off as competent in oocyte vitrification (ELB-SOP-005) before working unsupervised. Responsible for the procedure, the witnessing checkpoints, labelling, placement and the records generated.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and specimen identity at every labelling, transfer and placement step (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms consent and storage authority is current (ELB-POL-009), reviews survival outcomes and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| MII | Metaphase II — mature oocyte with extruded first polar body; the stage vitrified |
| Vitrification | Solidification into a glass-like state by ultra-rapid cooling, avoiding ice crystals |
| CPA | Cryoprotectant agent (permeating and non-permeating) |
| ES / VS | Equilibration solution / vitrification solution |
| Carrier / device | The labelled support onto which the oocyte is loaded for plunging |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Validated commercial oocyte-vitrification kit (ES, VS, wash/dilution solutions) used within shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Vitrification carrier devices and protective storage sleeves/goblets, single-use and sterile.
- Stereomicroscope with heated stage; calibrated timer; fine handling pipettes sized for denuded oocytes.
- Liquid nitrogen (LN₂), filling vessel and the designated storage tank (ELB-SOP-036, ELB-SOP-037).
- Cryo personal protective equipment (face shield, cryo gloves, closed footwear, apron); LN₂ area oxygen monitoring (ELB-SOP-037).
- Cold-chain identifiers: cryo-resistant labels, canes/goblets and the RI Witness reader.

> **IFU cross-reference.** The oocyte is uniquely sensitive to osmotic and thermal stress; the CPA concentrations, step sequence and exposure times are defined by the **validated kit IFU** and ESHRE 2015 and must be followed exactly. Do not mix lots or kits within a single run.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm valid, signed cryopreservation and storage consent is on file for the patient (ELB-POL-009, ELB-FORM-001).
2. Confirm denudation is complete (ELB-SOP-026) and maturity is assessed; only MII oocytes are vitrified.
3. Confirm the kit is within shelf life, brought to the working temperature stated in the IFU, and that timers are set.
4. Confirm carrier devices, storage goblets/canes and labels are pre-labelled with the patient's identifiers and verified (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm tank space, the assigned position in the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and adequate LN₂ level (ELB-SOP-037).

> **CRITICAL — identity before you begin.** Only one patient's oocytes are open at a workstation at any time. Both the oocyte dish and the carrier device pass an electronic-witness identity match before loading (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm the oocyte dish and the pre-labelled carrier at the workstation; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Move each MII oocyte through the **equilibration solution (ES)** for the exposure time specified in the kit IFU, observing the expected shrink-and-re-expansion behaviour.
3. Transfer the oocyte into the **vitrification solution (VS)**; start the timer. Keep total VS exposure strictly within the IFU window — oocytes tolerate over-exposure poorly.
4. Load the oocyte onto the labelled carrier in the minimal volume specified by the IFU; limit the number of oocytes per device to the agreed maximum 🔧 (Appendix A).
5. **Plunge** the loaded carrier directly into LN₂ (open device) or seal and cool per the IFU (closed device) without delay.
6. Confirm the carrier identity again, then place it into its labelled goblet/cane and record the exact tank, canister, cane and position; perform **EW match** (checkpoint W2).
7. Transfer to the assigned storage position in the designated tank under LN₂; confirm immersion/phase per tank type (ELB-SOP-036).
8. Record device type, number of oocytes per device, kit lot, times and storage location on the oocyte vitrification record (ELB-LOG-014) and update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where oocytes are identified, loaded, labelled or placed into storage (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Oocyte dish and carrier device belong to the same patient before loading | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Loaded carrier matches its goblet/cane and the recorded position | EW match + second-person |
| **W3** | Storage location entered in inventory matches the physical placement | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. The VS exposure clock does not justify proceeding on assumption — if identity cannot be confirmed, complete cooling to protect the oocyte, isolate it under quarantine labelling, and resolve identity before any inventory entry. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Vitrify:** MII oocytes only, with current consent.
- **Do not vitrify:** immature (GV/MI) or degenerated oocytes (record; handle per Appendix A 🔧), or where consent is absent, expired or non-matching — escalate (ELB-POL-009).
- A carrier is rejected if labelling is illegible, damaged or unverifiable; re-label and re-witness before use.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP — CPA exposure outside the IFU window, EW downtime, carrier failure, mis-placement, or a tank/LN₂ issue during the run — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Significant clinical events follow the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- Oocyte vitrification record — **ELB-LOG-014**.
- Cryostorage inventory update (tank/canister/cane/position) — **ELB-LOG-016**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The CPA sequence and exposure times are **guideline-anchored** to the validated kit IFU and ESHRE 2015. Values tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)** include: number of oocytes per device 🔧, policy on immature/degenerated oocytes 🔧, the post-warming oocyte survival threshold 🔧 (applied at warming, ELB-SOP-035), and the maximum storage period 🔧. Each is flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requires Medical Director sign-off.

## 14. Teaching point

The mature oocyte is the most fragile cell the laboratory vitrifies: a single large cell with a meiotic spindle that is exquisitely sensitive to temperature and to osmotic shock. The kit IFU timings are not guidance, they are the limits of what the cell tolerates — over-exposure to vitrification solution is as damaging as ice. Handle gently, keep to the clock, and treat the spindle's invisibility as a reason for discipline, not complacency.
