## 1. Purpose

To define how an unbroken, documented chain of custody is maintained for every patient specimen — gametes and embryos — from receipt or retrieval through every handling step to transfer, cryostorage or witnessed disposition. Chain of custody is the time-ordered record that, at any moment, the laboratory can say who holds (or held) which specimen, where it is, and that it has never been misattributed.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all autologous patient material — semen, prepared sperm, oocytes, zygotes, embryos — and to its movement between people, vessels, workstations, incubators, cryostorage and (where applicable) clinical handover. Builds directly on identification and labelling (ELB-SOP-016) and witnessing (ELB-SOP-002). Donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Every person who handles a specimen** — verifies identity on receiving custody, records the transfer, and is accountable for the specimen while it is in their care.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness)** — independently confirms identity at each custody transfer (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Laboratory Lead** — owns the inventory and reconciles it against the chain-of-custody records.
- **Quality Manager** — audits the chain for completeness; **Medical Director** — owns the policy.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Chain of custody | The continuous, documented record of every transfer of a specimen |
| Custody transfer | Any point where responsibility for a specimen passes (person→person, vessel→vessel, location→location) |
| Reconciliation | Confirming recorded specimens match physical inventory and position |
| RI Witness | Electronic witnessing system used to log identity matches at transfers |
| EW | Electronic witnessing |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- RI Witness electronic witnessing system (readers logging each match by specimen, operator, time and location).
- Cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and disposition/transfer record (ELB-LOG-017).
- Durable identifiers and tags per ELB-SOP-016.
- The cycle record / worklist (ELB-SOP-001).

> **IFU cross-reference.** RI Witness readers and tags are used per their IFU and validated for each location's exposure (workstation, incubator, LN₂). Inventory positions are recorded in the units and scheme defined in **Appendix A**.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the specimen carries the full identifier set and is registered in RI Witness (ELB-SOP-016).
2. Confirm the cycle record/worklist is current and matches consent (ELB-POL-009).
3. Confirm the destination (vessel, incubator slot, cryostorage position) is identified and ready.

## 7. Procedure

1. **On taking custody**, read the specimen identifiers, perform the EW match, and confirm the specimen against the worklist before acting on it.
2. **At every transfer** (person, vessel, workstation, incubator, storage), label the destination first (ELB-SOP-016), perform the EW match, and the system records operator, specimen, time and location.
3. Maintain single-patient working — never hold two patients' open specimens in one custody event/field.
4. **On placing into cryostorage**, record the device, straw, cane and exact position in the inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and confirm by EW match (checkpoint W3).
5. **On retrieving from storage**, confirm the inventory position and identifiers by EW match before removal, and update custody to the operator.
6. **On clinical handover** (e.g. specimen released for transfer), perform the EW match at handover and record the transfer in ELB-LOG-017.
7. **On disposition**, act only on a recorded disposition election (ELB-POL-009); perform the EW match, two-person witness, and record in ELB-LOG-017.
8. **Reconcile** the inventory against the chain-of-custody records at the locally defined interval 🔧 (**Appendix A**); investigate any discrepancy immediately.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Every custody transfer is a witnessed identity event under ELB-SOP-002. RI Witness performs and logs the electronic match; a manual two-person read with written record is the validated fallback during EW downtime.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Identity confirmed on taking custody | EW match (RI Witness) / two-person read |
| **W2** | Source and destination match at each transfer; custody logged | EW match + transfer record |
| **W3** | Cryostorage position recorded and matched on store/retrieve | EW match + inventory cross-check (ELB-LOG-016) |
| **W4** | Identity confirmed at clinical handover / witnessed disposition | EW match + ELB-LOG-017 |

> **CRITICAL — no untracked movement.** A specimen is never moved, stored, retrieved, handed over or disposed of without a logged custody transfer and a passed witness match. Any gap, mismatch or unreconciled position is a stop condition: secure the specimen, do not proceed, and escalate.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

Custody is intact when every movement is logged with an operator, time, location and a passed witness match, and the inventory reconciles. A missing transfer record, an unreconciled position, or a failed/absent witness match is a reject/stop condition until resolved and documented.

## 10. Deviations

Custody gaps, reconciliation discrepancies and witness failures are handled under the deviation/CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Any event with potential for loss or misattribution is a reportable adverse event (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- RI Witness electronic custody/match log (per specimen and transfer).
- Cryostorage inventory — **ELB-LOG-016**.
- Disposition / transfer record — **ELB-LOG-017**.
- Manual witness records during EW downtime (ELB-SOP-002).
- Any deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The inventory reconciliation interval 🔧 and the cryostorage position-coding scheme 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The requirement for continuous, witnessed, documented custody is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022), implemented with **RI Witness** electronic witnessing (ELB-SOP-002), and aligned with ISO 15189 traceability.

## 14. Teaching point

Identification (ELB-SOP-016) says *which patient*; chain of custody says *and we have never once let go of that certainty*. The two together mean that at any instant — bench, incubator, dewar, theatre door — the laboratory can answer where a specimen is and prove it has stayed itself the whole way. Every handover is a witnessed event, every storage position is recorded, and reconciliation is the routine that catches the rare gap before it becomes a loss.
