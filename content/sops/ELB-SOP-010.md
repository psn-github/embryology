## 1. Purpose

To define how solutions are sterilised by membrane filtration when terminal sterilisation by heat is unsuitable, so that heat-labile, embryo-contacting fluids are rendered free of micro-organisms without loss of function or introduction of toxic residues.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the filter sterilisation of heat-labile solutions prepared or finished in the laboratory where a sterile, embryo-safe fluid is required and autoclaving would degrade the product. Commercial media supplied sterile are used as received and are not re-filtered. Applies to autologous workflows only; donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

> **Default position.** Most embryo-contacting media and oil are purchased ready-sterilised and embryo-tested, and are used per IFU. Filtration in-house is the exception, used only where a validated, embryo-safe filter and solution are available and the need is documented.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — selects the correct filter, performs filtration aseptically, and records lot/filter details.
- **Laboratory Lead** — approves any in-house filtered solution for embryo contact and confirms it is validated/embryo-safe.
- **Quality Manager** — audits records and reviews any integrity failure.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Sterilising-grade filter | Membrane rated to retain micro-organisms at the validated challenge (typically 0.22 µm) |
| Integrity test | Bubble-point or equivalent test confirming filter integrity |
| Embryo-safe / MEA-tested | Validated as non-toxic to embryos by mouse embryo assay or equivalent |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Sterile, single-use sterilising-grade filter unit (rated per **Appendix A** 🔧), syringe or vacuum receiver.
- Sterile collection vessel, embryo-tested.
- Laminar flow hood (ELB-SOP-044); sterile technique throughout (ELB-SOP-009).
- The solution to be filtered, with recorded composition and lot of constituents.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Filter pore rating, maximum pressure and compatibility follow the filter IFU and **Appendix A**. Use only filters validated for the fluid and confirmed embryo-safe; flush/discard the initial volume if the IFU requires it.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the filter is in date, the correct rating, and compatible with the solution.
2. Confirm the receiving vessel is sterile and labelled (ELB-SOP-016).
3. Confirm work is in a certified, warmed-up hood with elapsed disinfectant contact time.

## 7. Procedure

1. Assemble the filter and receiver aseptically without touching sterile connections.
2. Pre-wet/flush the membrane if the IFU requires, discarding the flush volume.
3. Filter the solution at or below the maximum rated pressure; do not force a blocked filter.
4. Collect into the sterile vessel; cap immediately.
5. Where required by **Appendix A** 🔧, perform a post-use integrity (bubble-point) test and record the result.
6. Label the filtered solution with contents, lot, date/time, expiry and operator (ELB-SOP-016).
7. Discard the filter and any filtrate that failed integrity.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Filtered solution correctly labelled and attributed before storage/use | Second-person / RI Witness (ELB-SOP-002) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A filter that fails integrity invalidates the batch — discard the filtrate, do not use it for any patient. A blocked filter must never be force-pressurised; it can shed membrane or fibres.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept:** correct in-date filter used within rated pressure, integrity test passed (where required), product labelled and within its assigned expiry.
- **Reject:** integrity failure, expired/incompatible filter, unlabelled product, or any sign of leakage or particulate carry-over.

## 10. Deviations

Integrity failures, wrong-filter use and contamination are handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Filtration record (filter lot, rating, integrity result, solution lot) — laboratory record / deviation log where applicable.
- Labelling and attribution entry — per ELB-SOP-016.
- Any deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Filter pore rating 🔧, maximum filtration pressure 🔧, integrity-test requirement 🔧 and assigned expiry of in-house filtered solutions 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The requirement that embryo-contacting fluids be sterile and non-toxic is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015); process control aligns with ISO 15189.

## 13. References

- ESHRE. *Revised guidelines for good practice in IVF laboratories (2015).*
- ISO 15189 — requirements for quality and competence in medical laboratories.

## 14. Teaching point

Filtration removes organisms but not toxins, and a passed integrity test is the only objective evidence the membrane was intact during use. Prefer validated, embryo-tested commercial media; reserve in-house filtration for the rare, documented case and treat the integrity result, not the operator's confidence, as the release criterion.
