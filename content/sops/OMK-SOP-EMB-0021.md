## Purpose

This SOP covers the process of oocyte collection and culture of oocytes on Day 0. To outline the laboratory procedure for oocyte collection, this includes consumables/equipment required, laboratory witnessing steps, the oocyte collection procedure and the culture of oocytes post collection.

## Scope

This SOP covers the procedure of egg collection by laboratory staff and is applicable to all laboratory staff working in any Oxford Medical Kuwait clinic who are deemed competent to perform egg collection.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

## Procedure

• Gloves

• Marker pen

• 1 Discard Pot

• 1 Gel Pac

• Cloth

• Stop watch/timer

• Temperature probe

• G85 incubator or similar ungassed incubator

-   Clinical Waste Bin

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   14ml Test Tubes
-   Syringes for flushing (if applicable)
-   GMOPS + Heparin for flushing follicles/rinsing TVOR needle (200µl Heparin/125ml bottle GMOPS)
-   1 x 60mm Culture dish
-   1-3 x Centre Well Dishes (1ml GMOPS+, 1ml Ovoil)
-   5 Well Dishes (0.7ml GIVF+, 0.3-0.5ml Ovoil)
-   RI Witness Tags

### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the start of the theatre list, the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The test tube warmers and G85 (or equivalent) must be subject to an independent temperature check prior to use. The temperature should be 37◦C ±0.2◦C. If it is outside this range, the set point must be adjusted and the temperature then re-measured until 37◦C ±0.2◦C is achieved. Only proceed to egg collection once the temperature is within range.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of all used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point. If a surface probe is available, check the surface temperature is at the correct temperature.

### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, D.O.B and Clinic Number)

The patients RI Witness card must be assigned prior to the start of the procedure. Assigning of the patient RI Witness card requires an operator and a second person to act as the witness. The full name, D.O.B. and clinic number on the RI card must be cross checked to the laboratory notes and RI Witness.

A member of the theatre team and a member of the laboratory team are required for the patient ID check as this is an entry point into the system. When the patient is brought into theatre ask the patient to state their full name and D.O.B. Check that this information along with their clinic number matches the patient’s laboratory notes and patient RI Witness card. Operator and witness must then sign for the above witnessing checks.

Immediately following the patients ID check, place the patients RI Witness card in the RI reader.

Remove the pre-labelled GMOPS+ dishes from the un-gassed incubator and alongside RI Witness assign the GMOPS+ dishes to the patient checking the full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the dishes matches the laboratory notes and RI Witness. Approve these checks on the RI witness system.

Place the GMOPS\+ dishes in the G85 incubator (or equivalent).

### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare GMOPS+ centre well dishes the afternoon before the patient’s oocyte collection and equilibrate in an ungassed incubator overnight. Prepare one GMOPS+ dish per 6 follicles up to a maximum of three dishes (thereafter the dishes are re-used).

Prepare GIVF+ 5 well dishes the afternoon before the patient’s oocyte collection and equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight.

For IVF patients prepare one GIVF+ dish per 6 follicles. For ICSI patients prepare one GIVF+ dish per 12 follicles. It may also be useful to have one or two spare unlabelled GIVF+ 5 well dishes in the event a patient has more oocytes collected than were anticipated.

### 5.5 Oocyte Collection Procedure

Place 6mls of GMOPS with Heparin in a 14ml test tube and place in the theatre test tube warmer (used to flush the needle).

If follicular flushing is required, prepare flushing syringes with GMOPS with Heparin and place in a warming device/ungassed incubator until used.

Place a prewarmed 60mm dish on the RI heated stage.

The GMOPS+ dishes should remain within the G85 (or equivalent) until the first oocyte is found. Each GMOPS+ dish should be used for approximately 5 minutes before been returned to the G85 (or equivalent) and a fresh dish removed. If all three dishes have been used, return back to the first dish and continue rotating. The 5 minutes must be timed using a timer.

Following the aspiration of each follicle the test tube must be transferred to the laboratory test tube warmer ready for examination as quickly as possible. Pour the follicular fluid into the 60mm dish, if blood is present in the aspirate it may be preferable to check half of the tube at a time. Systematically check the follicular fluid under the microscope to search for oocytes. Check under bubbles, around the edge of the dish and swirl the fluid to bring oocytes into view.

When the first oocyte(s) is found, remove the first GMOPS\+ dish from the G85 (or equivalent) and start the timer for 5 minutes. Transfer the oocyte in a minimal volume of follicular fluid to the GMOPS+ dish using a sterile pasteur pipette. Visualise the oocyte in the dish before continuing to ensure it has left the pipette. Ensure the GMOPS+ dish remains on the heated stage with its lid on as much as practically possible during the procedure.

Once all the oocytes have been removed from the follicular fluid, discard the follicular fluid in the discard pot. The 60mm dish can then be re-used to examine further aspirates.

Inform the clinician when an oocyte(s) is found and give the clinician the running total for the patient so far. If no oocytes are found in a tube communicate back to the clinician whether any granulosa or cumulus cells were present.

Process all further aspirates as described above; in order of arrival into the laboratory. The embryologist should aim to keep pace with the clinician, however if test tubes are collecting in the laboratory test tube warmer, inform the clinician to pause briefly. It is useful to mark on the lid of the GMOPS\+ Centre well dish how many oocytes are contained within each dish.

Once the last test tube has been checked, the timer can be stopped, and the oocytes can then be transferred into their GIVF+ 5 well dishes.

### 5.6 Cultures of Oocytes Post Collection

If the oocytes are for the patient’s own use the oocytes can be transferred to the patients GIVF+ 5 well dishes alongside RI witness alone. The patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. must be cross checked on the GMOPS+ dishes, 5 well GIVF+ dishes, laboratory notes and RI witness system. Approve the checks on the RI witness system and sign for the witness check in the laboratory notes.

Consideration must be given to maintaining temperature, pH and osmolality during the transition from GMOPS+ to GIVF+. Keep GMOPS+ dishes in the G85 (or equivalent) until the oocytes within are to be moved and only remove one or two GIVF+ dishes from the gassed incubator at a time.

For ICSI patients, up to 12 oocytes can be placed in each dish. For IVF, up to 6 oocytes can be placed in each dish.

Remove one or two GIVF+ 5 well dishes from the gassed incubator. Prime the pasteur pipette with GIVF+ from well 1. Collect the number of oocytes to be placed in one dish and rinse in well 1. Prime the pipette with GIVF+ from well 2 and rinse oocytes in well 2. Finally, equally share the oocytes between wells 3 and 4 and note the number of oocytes in each well on the lid of the dish.

Quickly return the GIVF+ 5 well dishes to a gassed incubator.

Repeat as necessary until all the oocytes have been transferred to the GIVF+ 5 well dishes and all dishes have been placed into gassed incubators.

## Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

Egg:Follicle Ratio for follicles >10mm = >80%

## Monitoring

A Oxford Medical Kuwait laboratory manager is responsible for auditing this SOP annually.

The local laboratory manager is responsible for monitoring each embryology operators Egg:Follicle ratio. Any deviation will be discussed at team meetings or supervision meetings.

The local clinical lead is responsible for monitoring each clinicians/nurses Egg:Follicle ratio. Any deviation will be discussed at team meetings or supervision meetings.

The Egg:Follicle ratio and mean egg yield are reviewed at the Oxford Medical Kuwait KPI meeting every 4 months.
