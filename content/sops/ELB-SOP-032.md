## 1. Purpose

To define the laboratory's role in embryo transfer — selecting and confirming the embryo(s) for transfer, loading the transfer catheter, handing it to the clinician at the bedside, and confirming an empty catheter after transfer — so that the correct embryo reaches the correct patient with full, witnessed traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the laboratory handling of fresh and frozen-warmed embryo transfers for the **patient couple** (autologous embryos only). Covers embryo selection (ELB-SOP-029), catheter loading, bedside identity confirmation, transfer to the clinician and post-transfer catheter check. The clinical placement of the catheter is performed by the treating clinician. Warming of cryopreserved embryos is covered under ELB-SOP-035. Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — confirms the embryo(s) for transfer, loads the catheter, attends the bedside identity confirmation, and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent (ELB-SOP-005).
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and embryo identity at selection, loading and the bedside (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Treating clinician** — confirms patient identity at the bedside with the embryologist, performs the transfer, and agrees the number of embryos transferred.
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms the number to transfer against consent and policy, and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Transfer catheter | Soft catheter used to deliver embryo(s) to the uterus |
| Bedside check | Joint patient-identity confirmation by embryologist and clinician at transfer |
| eSET | Elective single embryo transfer |
| Empty-catheter check | Microscopic confirmation that the embryo(s) left the catheter |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted/stereomicroscope with heated stage; warmed handling surface and transfer surfaces near the transfer room.
- Pre-equilibrated transfer medium per IFU (and, where used, a transfer medium with adherence compound) — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Soft transfer catheter (per clinician preference/IFU); warmed dish for catheter loading.
- Tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**); RI Witness reader at the laboratory workstation and, where available, at the bedside.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Transfer medium, any adherence compound and the catheter are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist, the patient, and that consent covers transfer and the number to be transferred (ELB-POL-009).
2. Confirm the number of embryos to transfer against the agreed plan and policy 🔧 (**Appendix A**), favouring elective single transfer where appropriate.
3. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); confirm transfer medium/dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time in **Appendix A** 🔧.
4. For frozen-warmed transfers, confirm warming and post-warming survival/re-expansion are recorded (ELB-SOP-035).
5. Confirm dishes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016); confirm the RI Witness workstation is active and record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — right embryo, right patient, at the bedside.** Embryo transfer is the moment the embryo leaves the laboratory's control. The bedside identity confirmation, performed jointly by embryologist and clinician with the patient present, is the final and non-skippable safeguard.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm the selected embryo(s) at the workstation; perform the **identity and selection match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1), verifying grade and number against the plan (ELB-SOP-029).
2. Transfer the selected embryo(s) into the equilibrated transfer dish/medium.
3. At the announced time, perform the **bedside patient-identity confirmation** jointly with the clinician and the patient (checkpoint W2): patient identifiers, embryo identifiers and number to transfer all agree.
4. Load the embryo(s) into the catheter under the microscope, in the minimum medium volume per the loading technique, confirming the embryo(s) are drawn into the catheter.
5. Pass the loaded catheter to the clinician promptly, minimising time and temperature change; the clinician performs the transfer.
6. Receive the catheter back and immediately perform the **empty-catheter check** under the microscope (checkpoint W3) — confirm no embryo retained. If an embryo is retained, reload and repeat with the clinician.
7. Record the embryos selected and transferred, their grades, the number, the bedside confirmation, the empty-catheter result, catheter/medium lots, the clinician and embryologist, and the date/time on the embryo transfer record (**ELB-LOG-012**) and the culture record (**ELB-LOG-009**).
8. Account for any non-transferred embryos (e.g. cryopreservation, continued culture, disposition) per consent.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at selection, loading, the bedside and the empty-catheter check (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Correct embryo(s) selected for the correct patient; grade/number vs plan | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Bedside: patient identity and embryo identity agree with patient present | EW / joint embryologist + clinician + patient |
| **W3** | Catheter empty after transfer (no embryo retained) | Microscopic check, recorded |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the transfer. The bedside check (W2) is performed with the patient present and is never delegated or assumed. A retained embryo on the empty-catheter check is reloaded and re-transferred, witnessed. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Transfer** the selected embryo(s) of the agreed grade and number, consistent with consent and the local transfer-number policy 🔧 (**Appendix A**).
- An embryo that degenerates or is compromised before loading is recorded and not transferred; an alternative is selected with the witness and clinician.
- Transfer does not proceed unless W1 and the bedside W2 both pass.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. EW downtime, a retained embryo, a number-to-transfer discrepancy against consent, a temperature excursion of the loaded catheter) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Any identity discrepancy at the bedside is a stop condition and a reportable adverse event.

## 11. Records generated

- Embryo transfer record — **ELB-LOG-012**.
- Embryo culture & development record (final disposition of the cohort) — **ELB-LOG-009**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Transfer-relevant entries include: the local embryo-number-to-transfer policy (favouring eSET) 🔧, transfer-medium equilibration time, the maximum loaded-catheter time outside the incubator 🔧, and selection grade thresholds (per ELB-SOP-029). Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / Istanbul Consensus) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Transfer is the one step where the embryo leaves the laboratory, and the safeguards are correspondingly layered: select and witness in the lab, confirm identity jointly at the bedside with the patient present, and prove the catheter is empty afterward. The bedside check is the irreplaceable one — it is the last point at which the wrong embryo reaching the wrong patient can still be caught. Load gently, hand over quickly, and verify the empty catheter every time.
