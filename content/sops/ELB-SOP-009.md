## 1. Purpose

To define aseptic working practice in the Embryology Laboratory so that gametes, embryos, media and culture surfaces are protected from microbial and particulate contamination. Embryo culture is open culture in a sensitive medium; technique, not just equipment, keeps it clean.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all handling of gametes, embryos, media, dishes and instruments at the bench, in laminar flow hoods and at micromanipulation workstations. Covers the patient couple's own gametes and embryos only (autologous). Donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **All embryologists and andrologists** — apply sterile technique at every step and stop work if asepsis is broken.
- **Laboratory Lead** — ensures staff are trained (ELB-SOP-005), monitors compliance, and maintains the clean-work environment.
- **Quality Manager** — audits technique and reviews any contamination event.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Aseptic technique | Practice that prevents introduction of micro-organisms to a sterile field |
| Critical zone | The first air / unobstructed laminar flow over the open work in a hood |
| VOC | Volatile organic compound |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Laminar flow hood / workstation (ELB-SOP-044), cleaned and certified.
- Sterile, single-use, embryo-tested dishes, pipettes and tips; sterilised reusable items (ELB-SOP-011, ELB-SOP-012).
- 70% alcohol or an embryo-safe surface disinfectant; lint-free wipes.
- Powder-free, non-latex gloves (ELB-POL-003).
- Sterile-filtered media and oil where applicable (ELB-SOP-010), used within IFU shelf life.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Use only embryo-tested, sterile consumables within their stated shelf life. Disinfectants and their contact times follow the IFU and **Appendix A**; never introduce a product not validated as embryo-safe into the culture area.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the hood has run for the warm-up/purge time in **Appendix A** 🔧 and the work surface is clean.
2. Confirm air quality / TVOC is within range (ELB-SOP-013); avoid VOC-generating activities near open culture.
3. Assemble all sterile items needed before opening any dish, to minimise reaching across the field.

## 7. Procedure

1. Perform hand hygiene; don powder-free gloves. Re-disinfect gloves with 70% alcohol before working at the field.
2. Wipe the work surface and tray with disinfectant and allow the IFU contact time to elapse.
3. Work within the critical zone; keep open dishes inside the first air and do not place objects upstream of the open work.
4. Open sterile packaging only at the point of use; do not touch the working surfaces of pipettes, tips or dish interiors.
5. Use one sterile pipette/tip per patient per fluid; never re-enter a stock with a used tip.
6. Keep only one patient's material open at a workstation at any time (ELB-SOP-016).
7. Minimise talking, movement and traffic across the field; keep the sash at the certified working height.
8. On any suspected breach (dropped lid, glove contact, spill), stop, discard the affected items, re-disinfect and restart that step.
9. On completion, clear the field, disinfect surfaces and dispose of waste (ELB-SOP-015).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Only one patient's material is open at the workstation | RI Witness / second-person (ELB-SOP-002) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A broken sterile field is not "probably fine". Discard affected consumables and media, re-disinfect, and document the breach. Cross-contamination of identity is prevented by single-patient working — never short-cut it.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

A field is acceptable for use when surfaces are disinfected with elapsed contact time, the hood is certified and within warm-up, and only validated sterile consumables are in use. Compromised consumables, expired media, or an uncertified hood are reject conditions.

## 10. Deviations

Contamination, sterility breaches and uncertified-equipment use are handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Contamination/breach entries — deviation log **ELB-LOG-023**.
- Air quality / TVOC supporting record — **ELB-LOG-022**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Hood warm-up/purge time 🔧, disinfectant contact times 🔧 and the air-quality / TVOC range 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The principle of aseptic open culture is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015); facility/airflow requirements align with ISO 15189.

## 13. References

- ESHRE. *Revised guidelines for good practice in IVF laboratories (2015).*
- ISO 15189 — requirements for quality and competence in medical laboratories.

## 14. Teaching point

In an IVF laboratory, sterility and identity are the same discipline practised two ways: keep one patient's material open at a time and keep the field clean, and most catastrophic errors become impossible. Technique fails quietly — there is no alarm for a contaminated dish — so the habit must be exact every time, not just when you are being watched.
