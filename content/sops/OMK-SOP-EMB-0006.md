## 1. Purpose

To define how laboratory staff are trained, assessed as competent, signed off to work unsupervised, and have their competency maintained and re-verified over time. Competency assurance is a core ISO 15189 requirement: it ensures that every person who handles a patient's gametes and embryos has demonstrated the skill and judgement to do so safely before working alone.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all embryology and andrology staff and trainees performing or witnessing laboratory procedures on the **patient couple's own gametes and embryos** (autologous only). Covers initial training, supervised practice, competency assessment, unsupervised sign-off, ongoing competency, re-assessment after a break or a procedure change, and witnessing competency (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002). Donor and surrogacy content is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Trainee / staff member** — completes assigned training, works only within their signed-off scope, and declares any uncertainty.
- **Trainer / assessor** — a competent senior embryologist who delivers training, conducts assessment against defined criteria, and recommends sign-off.
- **Laboratory Lead** — assigns trainers, approves the scope of practice for each person, and authorises unsupervised work.
- **Quality Manager** — maintains the training and competency records (OMK-SOP-EMB-0006) and audits that scope of practice matches signed competencies.
- **Medical Director** — owns the competency framework and the local re-assessment intervals (**Appendix A**).

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Competency | Demonstrated ability to perform a defined procedure safely, correctly and independently |
| Scope of practice | The specific procedures an individual is signed off to perform unsupervised |
| Direct observation | Assessment by a competent assessor watching the procedure performed in full |
| Re-assessment | Repeat competency confirmation at a defined interval or trigger |
| Sign-off | Formal authorisation to perform a procedure unsupervised |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

Not applicable as a bench procedure. The tools are the training plan, the procedure SOPs being trained against, competency assessment checklists, and the training & competency acknowledgment log (**OMK-SOP-EMB-0006**).

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the trainee has read and acknowledged the relevant SOP(s) before practical training begins (OMK-SOP-EMB-0006).
2. Confirm a competent assessor is assigned for the procedure being trained.
3. Confirm trainee practice on patient material occurs only under direct supervision until sign-off.

> **CRITICAL — supervision until signed off.** A person who is not signed off for a procedure does not perform it on patient gametes or embryos without a competent person directly supervising. Witnessing competency is assessed before a person witnesses unsupervised (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002).

## 7. Procedure

1. **Plan.** Define the training plan and the scope of practice the person is working toward.
2. **Knowledge.** The trainee studies the relevant SOPs and any underpinning theory, and records acknowledgment (OMK-SOP-EMB-0006).
3. **Supervised practice.** The trainee performs the procedure under direct supervision, progressing as the assessor judges safe.
4. **Assess competency.** The assessor evaluates by direct observation against the SOP's defined steps, critical control points and witnessing checkpoints (OMK-SOP-EMB-0002), over the number of supervised cases / observations defined locally (**Appendix A** 🔧).
5. **Sign off.** When the assessor judges the person consistently competent, the Laboratory Lead authorises unsupervised practice for that defined scope and records it (OMK-SOP-EMB-0006).
6. **Maintain competency.** Ongoing performance is monitored against quality indicators; re-assess at the defined interval (**Appendix A** 🔧).
7. **Re-assess on trigger.** Re-assess after an extended absence, a significant procedure or equipment change, or a deviation/adverse event implicating competency (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005, OMK-POL-EMB-0004).
8. **Withdraw scope** where competency is not maintained; return the person to supervised practice and document the plan to regain sign-off.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing competency is itself assessed and signed off before a person witnesses unsupervised.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | The person can correctly perform the witness checks for the procedure (EW and manual fallback) | Direct observation against OMK-SOP-EMB-0002 |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** Sign-off is procedure-specific. Competence in one procedure does not authorise another. The scope of practice on record must match what the person actually does at the bench.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

A person is **signed off** when assessed consistently competent by direct observation against the SOP criteria over the defined number of cases, with witnessing competency confirmed. Sign-off is **withheld or withdrawn** where performance is inconsistent, a critical control point is missed, or re-assessment is overdue.

## 10. Deviations

Working outside a signed scope of practice, an overdue re-assessment, or a competency gap revealed by an event is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and logged (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). A competency failure that affected patient material is also handled as an adverse event (OMK-POL-EMB-0004).

## 11. Records generated

- Training & competency acknowledgment and sign-off — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0006**.
- Competency assessment checklists and re-assessment records.
- Scope-of-practice record per individual.
- Any related deviation — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The **requirement** for documented training, competency assessment and ongoing re-assessment is **guideline-anchored** (ISO 15189; ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022). The local **minimum number of supervised cases per procedure**, the **re-assessment interval**, and the **absence threshold triggering re-assessment** are **locally defined** and recorded in **Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A)** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Competency is not a certificate earned once; it is a current state that can lapse with absence, drift with habit, or be outpaced by a new technique or instrument. The honest question is not "was this person trained?" but "can this person do this safely today?". Procedure-specific sign-off and timely re-assessment keep the answer truthful, and the bench safe for the patient whose only embryos are in your hands.
