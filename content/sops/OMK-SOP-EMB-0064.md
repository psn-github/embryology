## 1. Purpose

To define how thermometers and temperature probes used in the Embryology Laboratory are calibrated and verified against a traceable reference, so that every temperature measurement on which gamete and embryo handling depends is accurate and defensible. Calibration establishes the measurement traceability required by ISO 15189 and underpins the 37.0 °C culture environment.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all reference and working thermometers and temperature probes used to set or verify incubators, heated stages, warming blocks, water baths and refrigerators/freezers supporting autologous gamete and embryo work. Covers calibration against a traceable standard, single-point verification between calibrations, and the recording of correction factors. Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Quality Manager** — schedules calibration, arranges external traceable calibration where required, maintains the calibration log (OMK-LOG-EMB-0063) and approves correction factors for use.
- **Embryologist / technician** — performs in-house verification checks where delegated, applies recorded correction factors, and reports any drift.
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews calibration outcomes and authorises continued use, restriction or withdrawal of an instrument.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Calibration | Comparison of an instrument against a traceable reference to determine its error/correction |
| Verification | A simpler check (often single-point) that the instrument still reads within tolerance between calibrations |
| Reference thermometer | A higher-accuracy thermometer with a current, traceable certificate, used as the standard |
| Correction factor | The recorded offset applied to a working instrument's reading to obtain the true value |
| Traceability | An unbroken chain of comparisons to a recognised (e.g. national/international) standard |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Traceable reference thermometer with a current calibration certificate (the standard).
- Stable temperature source spanning the points of use (e.g. dry-block / calibrated bath) capable of holding **37.0 °C** and any other relevant points.
- Thermometer(s)/probe(s) under test.
- Calibration log — OMK-LOG-EMB-0063; calibration labels/tags.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Calibrate at the points and using the method specified by the instrument and reference IFU. Allow each device its full immersion depth and stabilisation time; a reading taken before thermal equilibrium is not valid.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the reference thermometer's certificate is current (OMK-LOG-EMB-0063); a reference out of certification cannot be used to calibrate.
2. Confirm the temperature source is stable at the chosen point before comparison.
3. Identify each instrument under test by unique ID and confirm its prior correction factor and due date.

## 7. Procedure

1. Place the reference thermometer and the instrument under test in the same stable temperature source at the same depth, away from walls/heaters.
2. Allow both to reach thermal equilibrium for the stabilisation time in the IFU.
3. At each calibration point — including **37.0 °C** for culture-related devices — record the reference reading and the instrument-under-test reading simultaneously.
4. Calculate the error (instrument minus reference) at each point and record the **correction factor**.
5. Repeat at the additional points relevant to the instrument's use (e.g. fridge/freezer ranges) per Appendix A 🔧.
6. Compare the error against the acceptable tolerance 🔧 (Appendix A). If within tolerance, apply and label the correction factor; if outside, follow Section 10.
7. Record the calibration on **OMK-LOG-EMB-0063**: instrument ID, reference ID and certificate, points, readings, correction factor, result, date, next-due date, and signature.
8. Affix a calibration label showing instrument ID, date and next-due date.
9. Recalibrate at the locally defined interval 🔧 (Appendix A), and additionally after any drop, repair, battery change or suspect reading.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Calibration does not require a second-person identity witness; every calibration record must be **signed and timed** and the reference's traceability captured (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004, ISO 15189).

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** An instrument outside tolerance must not stay in clinical use. Quarantine it, and assess whether any equipment it was used to verify (e.g. an incubator at 37.0 °C) may have been mis-set during the period since its last good calibration; escalate if culture material may have been affected.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Reference thermometer certificate is current | OMK-LOG-EMB-0063 / certificate |
| **C2** | Instrument error at 37.0 °C is within tolerance | Simultaneous comparison recorded |
| **C3** | Calibration reviewed, labelled and signed | OMK-LOG-EMB-0063 entry + label |

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** (return to use) when error at every required point is within the acceptable tolerance and the correction factor is recorded and labelled.
- **Reject** (quarantine) when error exceeds tolerance at any point, when no current traceable reference is available, or when the instrument shows physical/electronic fault.

## 10. Deviations

An out-of-tolerance instrument, a missed calibration due date, or a lost-traceability situation is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and recorded in the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Perform an impact assessment on equipment verified with the affected thermometer (e.g. incubators, OMK-SOP-EMB-0062); escalate to OMK-POL-EMB-0004 where culture conditions may have been compromised.

## 11. Records generated

- Calibration log (instrument ID, reference, points, correction factors, due dates) — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0063**.
- Resulting correction applied during incubator/stage checks — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0061**.
- Any deviation entry — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

The culture temperature reference point **37.0 °C** is **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015), and calibration traceability is required by **ISO 15189**. The calibration **interval**, the acceptable **tolerance** (error limit), and the additional verification points per instrument type are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in **Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A)**).

## 14. Teaching point

A thermometer is the laboratory's witness to temperature, and an uncalibrated witness is no witness at all. The point of calibration is not the certificate but the traceable chain it represents: if you can show that the reference traces to a recognised standard and the instrument reads within tolerance against it, then — and only then — does 37.0 °C on the display mean 37.0 °C in the drop.
