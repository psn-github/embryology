## Purpose

To describe the information provided to patients at different points in their treatment journey. The laboratory team communicate with patients at various stages of their treatment cycle. It is essential that accurate information is provided to patients and that this is given in a clear and concise manner that patients can understand.

## Scope

Applicable to all laboratory staff deemed competent to provide laboratory information to patients.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

## Definitions

eSET Elective Single Embryo Transfer

TL Time lapse

AH Assisted Hatching

GP General Practitioner

FER Frozen Embryo Replacement

PGT Pre Implantation Genetic Testing

All Staff

All staff are responsible for following the process laid out in this document, ensure they receive up-to-date training to enable safe working and to request further training if they feel it is required. Staff are also responsible for reporting any non-compliance or incidents occurring on the controlled register and following up the outcome of any reported incidents/non-conformities.

## Procedure

### 5.1 Patient Communication Overview

Prior to providing any information to a patient(s) regarding their treatment, the patient’s identity must be ‘positively’ confirmed using three identifiers. Request their full name and date of birth plus the first line of their address/telephone number/postcode or partner’s full name. Cross reference this to the patient’s laboratory record/Ideas file to ensure it is correct before providing any information.

Laboratory information should be delivered clearly, accurately and with empathy.

In the event that there is ‘bad news’, take care to deliver the information sensitively and allow the patient to ask questions. Remind the patient that we provide a counselling service and offer a review appointment if the patient wishes. Alternatively, the patient may prefer to call back at a later point when they feel ready to speak.

Details of the information provided to patients must be recorded in the patient’s laboratory notes or on the patients IDEAs file as a progress note.

If a patient’s treatment plan is changed, for example from IVF to ICSI, the rationale for this decision must be clearly documented in a progress note within the patients IDEAs record.

Each patient having a fresh IVF/ICSI cycle should be provided with the **Oxford Medical Kuwait Patient Guide to Embryology (TFP1-INF-EMB-0001)** either just before TVOR or on the day of TVOR.


At embryo transfer (fresh and frozen) the patient must sign for the actual number of embryos that are being transferred. This must be captured using the **Oxford Medical Kuwait Unified Embryo Transfer Consent (TFP1-CON-EMB-0003)**.

Verbal information provided to patients can also be followed up with a portal message to ensure the patient has understood the information correctly.
