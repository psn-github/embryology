## 1. Purpose

To define the routine independent checks of incubator temperature and CO₂ concentration so that every incubator used for gamete and embryo culture is confirmed to be holding its setpoints before clinical material is exposed to it. These checks verify the incubator's own displays against traceable reference instruments and create the QC record that demonstrates a stable, in-specification culture environment.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all CO₂ and tri-gas incubators in the Embryology Laboratory used to culture or hold the patient couple's own gametes and embryos (autologous only). Covers the daily and shift checks of temperature and CO₂; full incubator operation, cleaning and qualification are covered in OMK-SOP-EMB-0066, and overarching equipment QC in OMK-SOP-EMB-0060. Donor and surrogacy material is out of scope and not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Embryologist on duty** — performs the scheduled checks, compares readings against the acceptable range, records them on OMK-LOG-EMB-0061 and signs the entry. Acts immediately on any out-of-range result.
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews the QC log, investigates trends and authorises return to use after a deviation.
- **Quality Manager** — confirms reference instruments are within calibration (OMK-SOP-EMB-0064, OMK-SOP-EMB-0063) and audits the log for completeness.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Setpoint | The target value programmed into the incubator (temperature, CO₂) |
| Reference instrument | An independent, calibrated device used to verify the incubator display (calibrated thermometer; digital gas analyser) |
| Alert tolerance | The permitted deviation from setpoint before the result is flagged 🔧 (Appendix A) |
| QC | Quality control |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Calibrated reference thermometer/probe traceable to a recognised standard (calibration per OMK-SOP-EMB-0064).
- Digital gas analyser for independent CO₂ verification (use per OMK-SOP-EMB-0063), within its verification interval.
- Incubator QC log — OMK-LOG-EMB-0061.
- Where fitted, the incubator's continuous monitoring/alarm system and its display.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Operate the reference thermometer and gas analyser strictly per their manufacturer Instructions For Use, including warm-up and sampling time. A reading taken before the instrument has stabilised is not a valid QC result.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the reference thermometer and gas analyser are within their calibration/verification interval (OMK-LOG-EMB-0063); do not use an instrument that is overdue.
2. Confirm the incubator has been at setpoint undisturbed for the stabilisation period specified by its IFU before sampling.
3. Confirm the QC log (OMK-LOG-EMB-0061) is to hand and the previous entry shows the incubator was in range.

## 7. Procedure

1. Read and record the incubator's **displayed** temperature and CO₂ for the chamber to be checked.
2. Measure the **actual** chamber temperature with the calibrated reference thermometer per its IFU; allow the reading to stabilise, then record it.
3. Measure the **actual** CO₂ with the digital gas analyser (OMK-SOP-EMB-0063); allow the displayed value to stabilise, then record it.
4. Compare displayed vs measured values, and compare measured values against the acceptable range for temperature (target **37.0 °C**, ESHRE 2015) and CO₂ (set to achieve target media pH **7.2–7.4**, ESHRE 2015) with the locally defined alert tolerances 🔧 (Appendix A).
5. If all values are within range, record the result, initial and time the entry on OMK-LOG-EMB-0061.
6. If any value is outside range, follow Section 10 before continuing to use the incubator.
7. Perform the checks at the locally defined frequency 🔧 (Appendix A) — typically each working day and at shift change — and additionally after any door-open event longer than routine, after a gas-cylinder change, or after a power interruption.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Routine equipment QC does not require a second-person identity witness, but every QC entry must be **signed and timed** by the embryologist who performed it (OMK-SOP-EMB-0004).

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** An incubator reading outside the acceptable range is a stop condition for that chamber. Do not place fresh dishes into, and where clinically possible relocate culture material from, an out-of-range chamber until the cause is found and the chamber is reconfirmed in range. Confirm CO₂ adequacy against media pH, not the display alone (OMK-SOP-EMB-0065).

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Displayed temperature agrees with calibrated reference at 37.0 °C | Reference thermometer reading recorded |
| **C2** | Measured CO₂ is sufficient to hold media pH 7.2–7.4 | Gas analyser + periodic pH confirmation |
| **C3** | Result reviewed and signed | Log entry on OMK-LOG-EMB-0061 |

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept** the incubator for use when measured temperature and CO₂ are within the acceptable range and tolerances defined in Appendix A and agree with the displays.
- **Reject** (hold the chamber) when any measured value is outside tolerance, when display and reference disagree beyond tolerance, or when a reference instrument is out of calibration. Record, escalate and manage as a deviation.

## 10. Deviations

An out-of-range result, or a display that disagrees with the reference beyond tolerance, is handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and recorded in the deviation log (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Where culture material may have been affected, escalate as a potential adverse event (OMK-POL-EMB-0004). Adjust the incubator or move it to maintenance, recheck after stabilisation, and document the corrective action before returning the chamber to clinical use.

## 11. Records generated

- Incubator QC log (daily/shift temperature and CO₂ checks) — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0061**.
- Calibration status of reference instruments — **OMK-LOG-EMB-0063**.
- Any deviation entry — **OMK-SOP-EMB-0005**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Culture temperature **37.0 °C** and the principle of setting CO₂ to achieve media pH **7.2–7.4** are **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015). The check **frequency**, the **alert tolerances** for temperature and CO₂, and the acceptable CO₂ percentage for the site's media and altitude are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in **Appendix A (OMK-APP-EMB-A)**, requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

An incubator display tells you what the incubator *believes*, not what the embryo experiences. The whole value of this check is the independent, calibrated measurement that confirms — or contradicts — that belief. Because CO₂ acts on the culture environment only through pH, the gas reading is a means to an end: trust the pH of the medium, verified periodically, over any number on the front panel.
