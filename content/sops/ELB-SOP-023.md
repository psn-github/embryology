## 1. Purpose

To define how an (autologous) sperm sample is assessed, mixed with cryoprotectant, frozen, labelled and placed into liquid-nitrogen storage so that viable spermatozoa are preserved for future use with full identification, traceability and a documented post-thaw expectation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to cryopreservation of ejaculated or surgically retrieved sperm from the **male partner of the patient couple** (autologous) — for fertility preservation, for banking before treatment, or to store surplus from a fresh or surgical sample. Thawing and preparation of stored samples is covered in ELB-SOP-020. Donor banking is out of scope and is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist / andrology technologist** — trained and signed off in cryopreservation (ELB-SOP-005); assesses, freezes, labels, stores and records.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity and device labelling at filling and at storage placement (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews low pre-freeze quality decisions and any deviation.
- **Cryostorage custodian** — maintains the inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and storage conditions (ELB-SOP-037).

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Cryoprotectant | Medium that protects sperm from freezing injury, added in defined ratio per IFU |
| Straw / cryovial | The labelled storage device loaded with the cryoprotected sample |
| Pre-freeze parameters | Concentration, motility and volume recorded before freezing, the baseline for post-thaw recovery |
| LN₂ | Liquid nitrogen |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Class II laminar flow hood (ELB-SOP-044); microscope with heated stage; counting chamber.
- Validated sperm cryoprotectant and storage devices (straws/cryovials) with patient-specific labelling; lots recorded on **ELB-LOG-004**.
- Controlled-rate freezer or validated manual LN₂-vapour freezing method per IFU; LN₂ handling equipment and PPE (ELB-SOP-037).
- Cryostorage tank with mapped positions; inventory (**ELB-LOG-016**); RI Witness reader.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Cryoprotectant type and ratio, equilibration time, the freezing/cooling profile and the device fill follow the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Use only devices rated for LN₂ storage and labelled to survive cryogenic temperatures.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm a current order and **consent to cryopreserve and store**, including the agreed storage period and disposition (ELB-POL-009, ELB-FORM-001).
2. Confirm the sample is received, identified and liquefied (ELB-SOP-018) or freshly processed (ELB-SOP-021).
3. Record the **pre-freeze parameters** (concentration, motility, volume — per **WHO** manual) so post-thaw recovery can later be judged.
4. Confirm cryoprotectant, devices and labels are ready and patient-specific; confirm a mapped storage position and inventory entry (**ELB-LOG-016**).

> **CRITICAL — identity before freezing.** Verify the sample against the patient and the device labels, and complete an EW identity match, before adding cryoprotectant or filling any device (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). Only one patient's sample is open at the workstation.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm sample identity; perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Add cryoprotectant to the sample in the defined ratio, gently, and allow equilibration for the time specified by the IFU (recorded against Appendix A).
3. Fill the labelled straws/cryovials, leaving the headspace and seal specified by the IFU; record the number of devices.
4. Freeze using the validated cooling profile — controlled-rate freezer programme or the validated LN₂-vapour method per IFU (recorded against Appendix A).
5. Plunge/transfer the frozen devices into LN₂ storage at the mapped position; perform an **EW match** at placement (checkpoint W2).
6. Record device count, position, cryoprotectant lot, freezing method and pre-freeze parameters on **ELB-LOG-004**, and update the inventory (**ELB-LOG-016**).
7. Where local policy requires, thaw one test device to verify **post-thaw motility** against the local acceptance threshold and record the result (Appendix A).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at device filling and at storage placement (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Sample identity vs patient vs device labels before cryoprotectant/fill | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Frozen devices placed in the correct mapped position and inventory entry | EW match + inventory cross-check (ELB-LOG-016) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts filling or placement. Devices placed in storage must match the inventory exactly — a mis-stored straw is a chain-of-custody failure. Resolve identity and re-witness before continuing; log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- Cryopreservation **proceeds** when consent and storage period are valid, the device labelling is patient-specific, and the pre-freeze sample is recorded.
- A **low pre-freeze quality** sample may still be frozen on clinical request; the limited expectation is recorded and the patient/clinician informed.
- A **test-thaw** below the locally defined **post-thaw motility** threshold (🔧 Appendix A) is recorded and escalated; the storage may be retained with a documented expectation or the freezing method reviewed.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure — freezer/profile fault, device damage, low post-thaw on test, EW downtime, or an inventory/label discrepancy — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Storage and access events also fall under the cryostorage security policy (ELB-POL-007).

## 11. Records generated

- Sperm cryopreservation & post-thaw record — **ELB-LOG-004** (pre-freeze, method, devices, test-thaw).
- Cryostorage inventory entry — **ELB-LOG-016** (position, count).
- Custody / placement entry — **ELB-SOP-017**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Pre-freeze assessment follows the current **WHO** manual; the cryopreservation approach is **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015; ASRM 2022) with cryoprotectant ratio, equilibration and cooling profile per the kit **IFU**. The **post-thaw motility acceptance threshold** and the **maximum storage period** are **locally defined** (flagged 🔧, requiring Medical Director and, for storage limits, licensing-authority sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Freezing is a promise to the patient that what goes into the tank can come back out and be used — so two records made *before* the cells are frozen carry the whole procedure: the patient-specific label, and the pre-freeze parameters. The label is the only thing that distinguishes one straw from thousands; the pre-freeze numbers are the only yardstick by which a future thaw can be judged adequate. Identity at filling and an exact inventory at placement are the difference between a recoverable sample and a lost one.
