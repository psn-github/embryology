## 1. Purpose

To set the laboratory's general guidelines for selecting, performing and verifying sterilisation, so that the correct method is matched to each item and every sterilised item is released only on objective evidence that the process worked. This SOP governs sterilisation method choice and verification; item-specific routines are in ELB-SOP-010 (filtration) and ELB-SOP-011 (glassware).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all in-house sterilisation in the Embryology and Andrology Laboratory: moist heat (autoclave), dry heat, and membrane filtration. Single-use embryo-contacting consumables are purchased sterile and embryo-tested and are not reprocessed. Applies to autologous workflows only; donor and surrogacy work is not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing staff** — select the validated method, run the cycle and verify the result before release.
- **Laboratory Lead** — owns the validated cycle list and approves any new item/method.
- **Quality Manager** — maintains the verification schedule, reviews indicator/calibration records and any failure.
- **Medical Director** — signs off the locally defined cycle parameters in Appendix A.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Moist heat | Saturated steam under pressure (autoclave) |
| Dry heat | Sterilisation by hot air |
| Chemical indicator (CI) | Indicator changing on exposure to sterilising conditions |
| Biological indicator (BI) | Standardised spore challenge used to verify lethality |
| Load | A defined set of items sterilised together in one cycle |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Autoclave and/or dry-heat oven with calibrated temperature/time controls (ELB-LOG-020).
- Chemical indicators (per load) and biological indicators (per the verification schedule).
- Sterilising-grade filters for heat-labile fluids (ELB-SOP-010).
- Sterilisation packaging and labels (ELB-SOP-016).

> **IFU cross-reference.** Sterilisers, indicators and filters are used per their IFU and the parameters in **Appendix A**. Do not exceed rated loads or deviate from validated cycles.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the steriliser is in calibration (ELB-LOG-020) and not under a fault hold.
2. Select the method matched to the item: heat-stable solids → heat; heat-labile fluids → filtration (ELB-SOP-010).
3. Confirm indicators are in date and the correct type for the cycle.

## 7. Procedure

1. Clean and package items appropriately before sterilisation (ELB-SOP-011 for glassware).
2. Place a chemical indicator with each load and a biological indicator per the verification schedule in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Run the validated cycle for the item type and load (temperature/time per **Appendix A** 🔧).
4. On completion, confirm cycle data (temperature/time/pressure) met the setpoint and the chemical indicator changed correctly.
5. Where a biological indicator was used, incubate and read it per its IFU; do not release BI-controlled loads until the result is confirmed where required.
6. Label sterilised items with contents and cycle/expiry date; store sealed.
7. Record the load contents, cycle parameters and indicator results.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Cycle and indicator conform before the load is released for use | Operator + record review (ELB-SOP-002 for labelled outputs) |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A cycle is not "successful" because it ran. Release only on conforming cycle data and a passed indicator. A failed or ambiguous indicator quarantines the entire load.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Accept:** validated method used, cycle parameters met, chemical indicator conforming, biological indicator passed where required, item labelled and within assigned expiry.
- **Reject / quarantine:** parameter excursion, failed/ambiguous indicator, wrong method for the item, or steriliser out of calibration.

## 10. Deviations

Cycle failures, indicator failures and out-of-calibration use are handled under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). A failed biological indicator triggers recall/quarantine of affected loads.

## 11. Records generated

- Sterilisation cycle/load records (parameters, CI/BI results).
- Calibration log — **ELB-LOG-020**.
- Any deviation — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Validated cycle parameters (autoclave and dry-heat temperature/time), the biological-indicator verification frequency 🔧, and assigned shelf life of sterilised items 🔧 are recorded in **Appendix A**. The requirement to verify sterilisation objectively is guideline-anchored (ESHRE 2015) and aligns with ISO 15189 process control.

## 13. References

- ESHRE. *Revised guidelines for good practice in IVF laboratories (2015).*
- ISO 15189 — requirements for quality and competence in medical laboratories.

## 14. Teaching point

Sterilisation is a process you cannot inspect by eye, so it is governed by indicators and recorded parameters rather than appearance. Match the method to the material (never autoclave what only filtration can save), and treat the indicator result as the release decision — the cycle running is not the same as the cycle working.
