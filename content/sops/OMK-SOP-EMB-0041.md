## Purpose


## Scope

Applicable to all Embryologists and practitioners deemed competent to carry out vitrification of oocytes:

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

## Procedure

### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   Serological pipettes
-   Rapid I devices
-   Heat sealer
-   Rapid I Liquid Nitrogen Box
-   Liquid Nitrogen
-   Pre pulled Pipettes (145-190µm)
-   Timers
-   Pipette and tips
-   Cane/Cryocassette/Visi

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   5 well dishes
-   ICSI dishes
-   Rapid Vit Oocyte Media
-   Oil
-   RI Witness Tags

### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the vitrification procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point. Vitrification is performed at 37◦C. Maintenance of a temperature of 37◦C is critical to the success of the procedure.

Fill the Rapid I Cryobox and a dilvac with liquid nitrogen. Ensure appropriate PPE is used when handling liquid nitrogen.

Ensure the heat sealer is closely situated and switched on.

### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B)


Check the required Kuwait MoH forms have (or country specific equivalent) been completed providing consent to oocyte freezing.

Oocyte vitrification requires a second witness to be used alongside RI witness.

For vitrification of the patients own oocytes, check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the G1+ Post Denudation dish and vitrification dishes matches that on the laboratory notes and RI witness system. Check the patient’s full name, clinic number, D.O.B. and date of vitrification on the Rapid I straws and visi/cassette match that on the laboratory notes and RI witness system. Each straw must be labelled with the number of oocytes contained within. Approve these checks on the RI witness system and both operator and witness must sign for these checks.


### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare the oocyte vitrification dishes 30-60 minutes prior to use (validate time period required locally) and allow to warm in an ungassed incubator to 37◦C. Prepare one oocyte vitrification dish per 3 Rapid I’s to be used. One ICSI dish is also required for every oocyte vitrification dish.

Gently invert the Rapid Vit Oocyte solutions 3 times prior to making the dish. Place 0.8ml of Vitri1, 0.8ml of Vitri2 and 0.8ml of Vitri3 in separate wells of a 5 well dish. Overlay Vitri 1 and 2 with 0.3ml of oil.

### 5.4 Timing of Vitrification Procedure

Perform oocyte vitrification 3 hours post oocyte collection ± 30 minutes.

### 5.5 Cryoprotectant Exposure Times

Vitrolife Rapid Vit Oocyte

**Vitri1:** Exposure time 5-20 minutes

**Vitri2:** Exposure time 2-5 minutes (until oocyte has re-expanded back to its original size, usually around 4 minutes)

**Vitri3:** Exposure time 35 seconds

### 5.6 Preparation for the Procedure

Label sufficient Rapid I’s for the number of oocytes to be vitrified.

1-3 oocytes can be placed on each Rapid I.


Enter the storage location(s) in IDEAs. Prepare a storage card if required.

### 5.7 Procedure

Maintenance of a temperature of 37◦C is crucial during oocyte vitrification. Temperature can be maintained using either a heated stage or an ungassed G85/incubator.

Using a 145-190µm pipette transfer the number of oocytes to be placed on up to 3 Rapid I’s into Vitri1. Immediately start the timer for 5 minutes.

Wash the oocytes within Vitri1, replace the lid on the dish and culture at 37◦C for 5 minutes.

Place the Rapid I’s for this round into the Rapid I cryobox to cool.

After 5 minutes, prime the pipette with solution from Vitri2. Collect the number of oocytes to be placed on one Rapid I and transfer into Vitri2. Immediately start the timer for 5 minutes. Wash the oocytes in Vitri2.

Oocytes must remain in Vitri2 for 2-5 minutes during which time they should re-expand. Once re-expanded they can be transferred into Vitri3.

Immediately before transferring the oocytes into Vitri3 make 2 x 50µl drops of Vitri3 within the ICSI dish. Remove the metal rod from within the Rapid I.

Prime the pipette with solution from Vitri3.

Transfer the oocytes into the drops of Vitri3 in the ICSI dish. Empty the pipette and wash thoroughly in both wash drops.

Place the oocytes on the Rapid I. Immediately plunge within the Rapid I straw. The total duration in Vitri3 must not exceed 35 seconds.

Seal the Rapid I straw with the heat sealer.

Repeat for the remaining oocytes in the dish.

Once all oocytes have been vitrified within the dish, repeat the whole process until all the oocytes have been frozen.

Place the oocytes into a storage dewar alongside a second witness.

Clear the flowhood of any used consumables. Use Oosafe spray to clean the flowhood surface.

Complete the laboratory and witnessing records.

## Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

Oocyte Survival ≥90%

2PN Rate ≥55%

Blastocyst Formation Rate (A-E on day 5) ≥45%

Good Blastocyst Formation Rate (A/B/C Day 5 or 6) ≥25%

KPI’s with  oocytes are expected to meet the lower limits above. KPI’s with own oocytes may be lower depending on patient age, patient history.
