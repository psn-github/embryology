## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method and timing for assessing fertilisation after insemination or ICSI — by counting pronuclei and polar bodies — so that normal (2PN) fertilisation is distinguished reliably from failed, abnormal and degenerate outcomes, and recorded with full traceability.

## 2. Scope

Applies to the fertilisation assessment of all inseminated and injected oocytes from the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only), following conventional insemination (ELB-SOP-025) or ICSI (ELB-SOP-027). Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — assesses pronuclear status within the defined window, classifies each oocyte and is responsible for the records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent (ELB-SOP-005).
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity at each transfer point (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — reviews abnormal fertilisation patterns and total fertilisation failure, and any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| 2PN | Two pronuclei — normal fertilisation (two polar bodies expected) |
| 1PN | One pronucleus — abnormal/uncertain fertilisation |
| 3PN (≥3PN) | Three or more pronuclei — abnormal (often polyspermic) fertilisation |
| 0PN | No pronuclei seen — unfertilised or asynchronous |
| PB | Polar body |
| TFF | Total fertilisation failure |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted microscope with high-magnification optics (Hoffman/relief-contrast) and heated stage for pronuclear visualisation.
- Pre-equilibrated culture dishes for return to incubation — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Warmed transfer pipettes and handling surface; tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**).
- RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** All media, oil and consumables are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist and that insemination/ICSI (ELB-SOP-025 / ELB-SOP-027) was performed at the recorded time for the cohort.
2. Confirm the fertilisation check falls within the defined window — **16–18 h post-insemination/injection** (Istanbul/ESHRE; **Appendix A**).
3. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A) before removing dishes.
4. Confirm all dishes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — assess within the window.** Pronuclei are transient. Assess at 16–18 h (**Appendix A**); late assessment risks pronuclear breakdown and misclassification, and may miss abnormal (e.g. 3PN) zygotes that must be excluded from transfer and cryopreservation.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm patient oocytes/zygotes at the workstation; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. For each oocyte, examine under the inverted microscope; for conventional insemination, residual cumulus/corona may require brief denudation to visualise pronuclei (ELB-SOP-026).
3. Count the **pronuclei** and the **polar bodies** for each oocyte. Classify as:
   - **2PN** — normal fertilisation (juxtaposed pronuclei, typically two polar bodies);
   - **1PN** — single pronucleus (abnormal/uncertain);
   - **≥3PN** — abnormal, typically polyspermic;
   - **0PN** — no pronuclei (unfertilised or asynchronous — note for possible re-check).
4. Note pronuclear morphology and alignment where relevant to later grading (ELB-SOP-029).
5. Return normally fertilising (2PN) zygotes to fresh, labelled, equilibrated culture; segregate abnormally fertilised oocytes so they cannot enter the transfer/cryopreservation pathway.
6. Perform an **EW match** before returning dishes to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
7. Record pronuclear counts per oocyte, the 2PN total, abnormal counts, the assessment time, dish/incubator position and operator on the fertilisation assessment record (**ELB-LOG-007**) and the culture record (**ELB-LOG-009**).
8. Where 0PN dominates or there is total fertilisation failure, escalate to the Laboratory Lead and document any agreed re-check or rescue decision.

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where oocytes/zygotes are identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of dishes presented for assessment vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Zygotes returned to the correctly labelled dish/position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Abnormally fertilised oocytes (1PN, ≥3PN) must be physically and recordably segregated so they cannot be transferred or frozen. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Normal fertilisation:** 2PN — continue in culture toward grading and use.
- **Abnormal fertilisation:** 1PN and ≥3PN — recorded and excluded from transfer and cryopreservation; disposition follows local policy and consent.
- **0PN:** recorded; managed per local policy 🔧 (**Appendix A**), which defines whether and when a re-check is permitted.
- Total or low fertilisation against the cohort is reviewed against the local alert thresholds 🔧 (**Appendix A**).

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. assessment outside the 16–18 h window, EW downtime, mis-segregation of abnormal zygotes) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). Total fertilisation failure is reviewed and, where appropriate, handled as an adverse event.

## 11. Records generated

- Fertilisation assessment record — **ELB-LOG-007**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**, updated with the 2PN cohort.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Fertilisation-relevant entries include: the fertilisation-check window (16–18 h; Istanbul/ESHRE), the policy and timing for re-checking 0PN oocytes 🔧, and local fertilisation-rate alert thresholds 🔧. ## 14. Teaching point

Fertilisation is read in a narrow window: two pronuclei and two polar bodies at 16–18 h is the signature of normal fertilisation, and the only one that may proceed. The discipline here is timing and segregation — assess before the pronuclei fade, and make certain that 1PN and 3PN zygotes are removed from every downstream pathway. An abnormally fertilised embryo that reaches transfer is a failure of this step, not a later one.
