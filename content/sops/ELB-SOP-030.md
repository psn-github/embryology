## 1. Purpose

To define the standardised method for assisted hatching — controlled thinning or breaching of the zona pellucida — performed only on clinical indication and with the agreed technique, so that the procedure is consistent, minimally traumatic to the embryo and fully traceable.

## 2. Scope

Applies to assisted hatching of embryos derived from the **patient couple** (autologous gametes only), where clinically indicated and consented (ELB-POL-009). Covers laser, mechanical and chemical methods as adopted locally; the laser method is the default unless otherwise specified. Zona breaching performed as part of embryo biopsy is covered under ELB-SOP-031. Donor and surrogacy workflows are out of scope and are not performed.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — performs assisted hatching and is responsible for the procedure, witnessing checkpoints and records generated. Must be trained and signed off as competent in micromanipulation/laser use (ELB-SOP-005) before performing unsupervised.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient and embryo identity at every transfer point (ELB-SOP-002, ELB-SOP-016).
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms the clinical indication and consent, and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Zona pellucida | Glycoprotein shell surrounding the oocyte/embryo |
| Assisted hatching (AH) | Thinning or breaching of the zona to aid hatching |
| Laser AH | Non-contact diode-laser ablation of the zona |
| Hatching | Emergence of the blastocyst from the zona |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Inverted microscope with heated stage and an objective-mounted, calibrated diode laser system (for laser AH), or micromanipulators (for mechanical AH).
- Pre-equilibrated buffered (HEPES/MOPS) handling medium and culture medium under oil — within manufacturer shelf life; lot numbers recorded.
- Holding pipette and, for mechanical methods, the appropriate microtools; warmed dishes and surfaces.
- Tri-gas/benchtop incubator at setpoint (**Appendix A**); RI Witness reader at the workstation.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Laser pulse settings, the resulting aperture/thinning dimensions and all media/oil conditions are used strictly per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Equilibration time, temperature and gas conditions follow Appendix A and the relevant IFU; do not substitute lots mid-procedure without re-labelling and re-witnessing.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the day's worklist, the clinical indication for assisted hatching and that consent covers it (ELB-POL-009).
2. Confirm incubator temperature, CO₂/O₂ and pH are within range (ELB-SOP-039, Appendix A); confirm dishes were prepared and equilibrated for the time in **Appendix A** 🔧.
3. Confirm the laser is calibrated and the pulse settings match the IFU/local protocol 🔧 (**Appendix A**); confirm the stage temperature.
4. Confirm dishes carry the patient's identifiers and match the worklist (ELB-SOP-016).
5. Confirm the RI Witness workstation is active; record the manual fallback if EW is unavailable.

> **CRITICAL — protect the blastomeres.** The laser target is the zona only. Direct laser energy through cell-free perivitelline space, away from blastomeres/ICM, using the minimum pulses needed to achieve the agreed thinning/aperture. Excess energy or proximity to cells causes injury.

## 7. Procedure

1. Confirm patient embryos at the workstation; perform the **identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1).
2. Transfer the embryo to a handling droplet and immobilise it on the holding pipette, orienting so the target zona region overlies cell-free perivitelline space.
3. **Laser AH:** focus the laser on the zona at the chosen site; deliver the minimum pulses to thin or breach the zona to the dimension specified by the IFU/Appendix A 🔧, keeping clear of blastomeres/ICM.
   **Mechanical AH:** breach/thin the zona with the microtool per the validated technique.
4. Confirm the intended thinning or aperture and that no cell has been contacted or damaged.
5. Work in batches small enough to limit time outside the incubator 🔧 (**Appendix A**).
6. Transfer the treated embryo to a fresh, labelled, equilibrated culture dish; perform an **EW match** before returning to the incubator (checkpoint W2).
7. Record the method, laser settings/pulses or mechanical technique, the site and dimension, the embryos treated, dish/incubator position and operator on the assisted-hatching record (**ELB-LOG-010**) and the culture record (**ELB-LOG-009**).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at every point where embryos are identified or moved between vessels (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity of embryos presented for hatching vs worklist | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Treated embryos returned to the correctly labelled dish/position | EW match |
| **W3** | Dish labelling vs worklist at end of session | EW / second-person |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Do not proceed on assumption — resolve identity, then re-witness. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- **Perform** only on the recorded clinical indication and with consent; technique and target dimensions per the local protocol 🔧 (**Appendix A**).
- Embryos showing zona/blastomere injury attributable to the procedure are recorded and reviewed.
- Do not perform on embryos already designated for biopsy by the same aperture without the combined-procedure protocol (ELB-SOP-031).

## 10. Deviations

Any departure from this SOP (e.g. laser miscalibration, an aperture outside the protocol dimension, EW downtime, embryo injury) is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## 11. Records generated

- Assisted-hatching record — **ELB-LOG-010**.
- Embryo culture & development record — **ELB-LOG-009**.
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 12. Guideline-linked & locally-defined values

Values for this SOP are tabulated in **Appendix A (ELB-APP-A)**. Hatching-relevant entries include: the laser pulse settings and target thinning/aperture dimension (per IFU/local protocol 🔧), the clinical-indication criteria 🔧, dish equilibration time, and the maximum embryo time outside the incubator per batch 🔧. Each is marked **guideline-anchored** (ESHRE 2015 / ASRM 2022 / Istanbul Consensus) or **locally defined** (flagged 🔧 in Appendix A and requiring Medical Director sign-off).

## 14. Teaching point

Assisted hatching is a small, deliberate breach with a large margin for harm: the zona is the target, never the cells. Orient the embryo so the laser fires through cell-free space, use the fewest pulses for the agreed dimension, and keep the embryo out of the incubator no longer than necessary. The procedure is justified only by indication and consent — it is not a default, and doing it well means doing as little to the embryo as the result allows.
