## Purpose

To describe the procedure for warming vitrified blastocysts using the Rapid I device with Vitrolife warming kits. Blastocysts may be cryopreserved as part of a fertility preservation programme or following treatment. When the patient wishes to use the cryopreserved blastocysts they must be warmed and returned to culture.

## Scope

Applicable to all embryologists and practitioners deemed competent to perform warming of vitrified of blastocysts:

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

## Procedure

### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

-   Serological pipettes
-   Rapid I Liquid Nitrogen Box
-   Liquid Nitrogen
-   Pre pulled Pipette (290-310µm)
-   Timers
-   Straw Cutter/Forceps

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   5 well dishes
-   Rapid Warm Blast Media
-   G2+ Day 3-6 Culture Dish
-   Oil
-   RI Witness Tags

### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to performing the warming procedure the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage is switched on and at the correct set point. Warming is performed at 37◦C.

Fill the Rapid I Cryobox and a dilvac with liquid nitrogen. Ensure appropriate PPE is used when handling liquid nitrogen.

### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, Clinic Number and D.O.B)

24-48 hours prior to the scheduled thaw date, check the patients thaw has been scheduled on the correct date in their cycle (post LH surge or start of progesterone) and call the patient to check/confirm the following:

-   Confirm number of embryos in storage
-   Confirm the day of the thaw and planned day of embryo transfer
-   Ask the patient to positively confirm how many embryos they wish to thaw and how many embryos they wish to transfer. Cross reference this to the information in the Oxford Medical Kuwait Consent to Frozen Embryo Transfer form
-   If the patient wishes to have DET but meets eSET, explain the local eSET policy
-   Confirm the stage/grade/ploidy status of the embryos to be thawed
-   If patient is using HRT/progesterone, check the medication has been taken in line with the patient’s protocol
-   Confirm which add-ons will be used i.e Glue/AH/PGT
-   Check the patient is fit and well and is able to proceed with the embryo transfer

Record these checks in a progress note or in the laboratory notes.

On the day of warming, perform local checks to ensure that the warming is being conducted on the correct date, the pre thaw call has been conducted/recorded and the planned thaw is going ahead, and that no messages via progress/portal have been received from the patient/clinical team informing of a cancellation.

Check the patients consent form to determine the number of blastocysts to warm. If more than one batch of blastocysts have been frozen, check the correct batch is being warmed.

Blastocyst warming requires a second witness to be used alongside RI witness.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the Visi/Cassette, Rapid I straws and warming dishes matches that in the laboratory notes and RI witness system. Also check the freeze date on the Rapid I straws and the number of blastocysts contained within each Rapid I straw matches that in the laboratory records. Check the grade/grade noted on the straw corresponds to the one selected for warming. Approve these checks on the RI witness system and both operator and witness must sign for these checks.

Post thaw the warmed blastocysts are moved into a 40mm G2+ culture dish alongside RI witness alone.

### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare the 40mm G2+ dish the afternoon before the patient’s blastocyst warm and equilibrate in a gassed incubator overnight. Prepare one dish per patient.

Prepare the blastocyst warming dishes 30-60 minutes prior to use (validate time period required locally) and allow to warm in an ungassed incubator to 37◦C. Prepare one set of blastocyst warming dishes per 3 Rapid I’s to be warmed.

Gently invert the Rapid Warm Blast solutions 3 times prior to making the dishes. Place 0.5ml of Warm1, 0.5ml of Warm2 and 0.5ml of Warm3 in separate wells of a 5 well dish. Overlay Warm 2 and 3 with 0.3ml of oil.

### 5.5 Timing of Warming Procedure

Perform warming on Day 5 of the patient’s cycle such that the blastocysts are in synchrony with the endometrium (Day 5 and 6 embryos are both synchronised with a Day 5 endometrium).

Warm a minimum of 2 hours prior to the embryo transfer time.

Assess for signs of re-expansion 30-90 minutes post warming. If signs of re-expansion are not seen, consider warming a further blastocyst(s).

### 5.6 Warming Exposure Times

Vitrolife Rapid Warm Blast

Warm1: Exposure time 2 minutes

Warm2: Exposure time 3 minutes

Warm3: Exposure time 5 minutes

### 5.7 Warming Procedure

Maintenance of a temperature of 37◦C is crucial during blastocyst warming. Temperature can be maintained either using a heated stage or an ungassed G85/incubator.

Perform the witnessing checks to ensure the correct sample has been selected for warming. Transfer the Rapid I’s to the nitrogen filled cryobox. Move the cryobox close to the microscope. The speed of warming is crucial to blastocyst survival.

Using the straw cutter, cut the top of the Rapid I straw. Place the warming dish under the microscope with its lid off. Working as quickly as possible, remove the Rapid I from the straw and plunge directly into Warm1. Immediately start the timer for 2 minutes.

Priming the pipette with the solution that the blastocysts are currently in, expose the blastocysts to the following warming regime:

Warm1 for 2 minutes

Warm2 for 3 minutes.

Warm3 for 5 minutes.

Following warming, transfer the blastocysts to the 40mm G2+ culture dish alongside RI witness.

Wash the blastocysts through 3 droplets of G2+before then separating into droplets for culture.

Culture a maximum of 4 blastocysts in each drop. Return the culture dish to a gassed incubator.

If assisted hatching is to be performed, hatch in either Warm3 or the 40mm G2+ culture dish.

Repeat the procedure above until all the blastocysts for thaw have been warmed. Once the first blastocyst is in Warm3, a further blastocyst can be warmed without them entering the same well simultaneously.

Mark the wash drops of the culture dish by crossing through with a line under the dish to prevent further use. Note on the lid of the dish the total number of blastocysts contained within.

Complete the laboratory and witnessing records.

Return any remaining frozen blastocysts to their designated nitrogen storage vessel alongside a second witness. Both operator and witness must sign for these checks.
