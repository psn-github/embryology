#### Purpose

To outline the laboratory procedure for embryo transfer. This includes consumables/equipment required, laboratory witnessing steps, embryo selection and the embryo transfer procedure.

#### Scope

Applicable to all laboratory staff deemed competent to perform embryo transfer.

Gamete/embryo donation and surrogacy are out of scope (prohibited in Kuwait).

#### Definitions

ET Embryo Transfer

eSET Elective Single Embryo Transfer

DET Transfer of Two Embryos

TET Transfer of Three Embryos

General Manager

The General Manager (GM) is accountable for the implementation of this clinic-wide process.

#### Procedure

##### 5.1 Equipment & Consumables Required

**Equipment/Consumables Required**

• Test Tube Warmer (optional)

• Pre-Pulled Pipette (290-310µm)

• Embryo Transfer Catheter

• 1ml syringe

-   Stopwatch
-   Clinical Waste bin

**Dishes/Media Required**

-   1 x 5ml test tube with 1ml G2\+ (Catheter rinse)
-   1 x 5ml test tube with 1ml G2+ or Embryo Glue (ET dish)
-   1 x Centre Well Dish
-   RI Witness Tags
-   Oil (Optional, see additional note in 5.4)

##### 5.2 Equipment Checks

Prior to the start of the theatre list, the equipment to be used must be checked to ensure it is operating optimally.

The flowhood must be checked to ensure the flow is on and the hood is clear of all used dishes/pipettes and tubes.

Ensure the RI heated stage and inverted microscope are switched on and at the correct set point.

Any test tube warmers to be used must be subject to an independent temperature check prior to use. The temperature should be 37◦C ±0.2◦C. If it is outside this range, the set point must be adjusted and the temperature then re-measured. Only proceed to embryo transfer once the temperature is within range.

##### 5.3 Witnessing Checks (Full Name, D.O.B and Clinic Number)

All patients must complete and sign the Oxford Medical Kuwait Unified Embryo Transfer Consent (TFP1-CON-EMB-0003). This captures in writing how many embryos the patient is having transferred.

1\. Movement of the Embryo(s) from the Culture Dish to the Embryo Transfer Dish

This step can be performed by a single operator alongside RI Witness.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the culture dish containing the embryo(s) matches that on the embryo transfer dish, laboratory notes, RI card and RI Witness system. Ensure only the designated number of embryos for transfer are moved to the embryo transfer dish. Ensure the embryo(s) identified as having the best potential for implantation and therefore chosen for embryo transfer are the one(s) moved to the embryo transfer dish.

The operator must sign for the above witnessing checks.

2\. Positive Identification of the Patient in the Embryo Transfer Procedure Room

This step must be performed alongside a second witness (clinician/nurse performing the transfer procedure).

The embryologist performing the embryo transfer alongside the clinician/nurse performing the embryo transfer must perform a positive identity check with the patient. The patient must be asked to confirm their full name and D.O.B. Check this information along with their clinic number matches the patient’s laboratory notes and patient RI Witness card. Immediately place the RI Witness card in the RI reader.

The operator and witness must sign for the above witnessing checks.

3\. Removal of the ET Dish Ready for Loading the Embryo(s)

This step must be performed alongside a second witness in order to confirm that the verbal information provided by the patient matches that on the embryo transfer dish. The second witness may be a member of the clinical team performing/assisting with the embryo transfer or a member of the laboratory team.

Check the patient’s full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the dish containing the embryo(s) for transfer match the verbal information provided by the patient, the laboratory notes and the RI witness system.

The operator and witness must sign for the above witnessing checks.

_Note: If it is not possible to have a second witness to check the identity of the ET dish at the time of loading due to the laboratory/theatre layout, instead a second witness must be used during Step 1: Movement of the Embryo(s) from the Culture Dish to the Embryo Transfer Dish. A second witness must verify the ET dish in either Step 1 or Step 3._

##### 5.4 Preparation of Dishes

Prepare 1ml of G2+ and an additional 1ml of G2+ or EmbryoGlue in separate 5ml test tubes. Prepare the afternoon before the embryo transfer.

Also prepare a spare 1-2ml of G2\+ per day in a separate 5ml test tube that can be used in the event of a difficult transfer. Equilibrate loose capped in a gassed humidified incubator overnight.

In addition, warm one pre-labelled centre well dish per patient.

A minimum of 3 hours prior to embryo transfer, place 1ml of G2+ or Embryo Glue (depending on the patient’s wishes), into the centre well dish. Return the dish to a gassed incubator until the embryo transfer. It takes approximately three hours for the pH of the media to return back to optimal level after pouring.

_Additional Note: An embryo transfer dish may be prepared with 1ml of G2+/EmbryoGlue overlaid with 1ml of oil if the above method is not practical due to the timings required/equipment available within the laboratory. The oil covered embryo transfer dish must be made the day before the embryo transfer and equilibrated in gassed incubator overnight._

##### 5.5 Timing of the Embryo Transfer Procedure

The aim is to perform all embryo transfers on Day 5 of development.

A day 2/3 or Day 6 transfer may be performed if clinically applicable.

##### 5.6 Number of Embryos for Transfer

The number of embryos for transfer depends on a variety of factors, including female age, number of previous attempts at treatment, embryo quality, NHS funding policies and whether PGT-A has been performed.

If the cycle is NHS funded, the number of embryos for transfer must not exceed that set out in the CCG contract.

If the patient is privately funded, the number of embryos for transfer should be directed by the local clinics eSET policy and any other relevant clinical factors.

As a guide the following patient groups should be considered for eSET:

-   Oocyte recipients
-   Patient’s with known euploid embryo
-   Patients who are <38 with a good quality blastocyst
-   Patients having frozen embryo transfer

When managing the patient groups above, consideration must also be given to the number of attempts at treatment the patient has had, the quality of the embryos and whether there are any remaining embryos suitable for cryopreservation.

Any patient who meets eSET criteria but subsequently elects to have a DET must have a clear justification for the transfer of two embryos noted in the patents clinical file.

Three embryo transfer (TET) can only take place in patients who are 40 or over at the point of embryo transfer and who are using their own oocytes. Clear justification for the transfer of three embryos must be noted in the patient’s clinical notes.

All patients electing to have two or three embryos transferred must be made aware of the risk of multiple pregnancy.

All clinics must keep an auditable record/log of all patients who have a TET or who meet eSET and elect to have a DET.

At embryo transfer the patient must sign for the actual number of embryos that are being replaced. This is captured on the Oxford Medical Kuwait Unified Embryo Transfer Consent form (TFP1-CON-EMB-0003).

In the event that the patient wishes to transfer multiple embryos when they originally consented to one in the original Oxford Medical Kuwait IVF/ICSI or FET treatment consent form, this is possible providing the patient and their partner (if applicable) both agree and the patient signs for this, as described above.

If the patient’s partner does not attend for the embryo transfer appointment and the patient wishes to transfer multiple embryos having previously consented to one, providing the partner has ticked ‘Yes’ to the question below, the patient can proceed with a multiple embryo transfer. If, however the patient’s partner does not attend for the embryo transfer and they did not tick ‘Yes’ to the question below, verbal consent must be sought. Record that verbal consent has been taken in the patients Ideas file or laboratory notes. In the event that verbal consent cannot be obtained, transfer of multiple embryos cannot be performed.

##### 5.7 Mixed Transfers

Mixed IVF:ICSI embryo transfers can only take place in up to 2% of a clinics embryo transfers.

Mixed transfers consisting of embryos created with frozen eggs and embryos created with fresh eggs can only take place in up to 2% of a clinics embryo transfers.



It is prohibited to perform transfer of biopsied and non-biopsied embryos in the same cycle.

Mixed transfers of frozen and fresh embryos can take place providing the conditions above are not breached.

##### 5.8 Pre-ET Patient Discussion (Embryologist and/or Clinician)

-   Inform the patient of the number and quality of the embryos.
-   Confirm the number of embryos for transfer.
-   Confirm whether any remaining embryos are suitable for cryopreservation and if so, how many
-   Confirm whether any remaining embryos will be cultured until day 6
-   Complete the Oxford Medical Kuwait Unified Embryo Transfer Consent

##### 5.9 Embryo Transfer Procedure

Place the catheter and syringe on the RI heated stage to warm (ensure the catheter remains sterile).

Prepare a 290-310µm pre-pulled pipette.

Determine the number of embryos for transfer (see above).

Grade the embryos and select the embryo(s) with the highest potential for implantation for the transfer procedure.

Alongside RI witness, transfer the embryo(s) for embryo transfer into the Centre Well Embryo Transfer dish.

If Embryo Glue is to be used, the embryo(s) should spend a minimum of 10 minutes and a maximum of 4 hours in the Embryo Glue solution before embryo transfer.

Replace the embryo transfer dish and culture dish containing any remaining embryos back into a gassed incubator until the clinician/nurse instructs that they are ready for the catheter to be loaded.

Perform the patient ID check as described in 5.3.

**Catheter Loading Options:**

**Option 1:**

While the clinician/nurse is positioning the speculum, remove the 5ml test tube containing G2+ from the gassed incubator. Rinse the catheter/syringe with the G2+.

Leave a residual 0.2ml of G2+ in the catheter/syringe and place the catheter on the heated stage ready for loading.

When the clinician/nurse instructs they are ready, remove the Embryo Transfer dish from the incubator. Alongside a second witness check that the full name, clinic number and D.O.B. on the Embryo Transfer dish, RI witness card and laboratory records all match.

Load the embryo(s) into the catheter using the following technique:

AIR – EMBRYO-AIR

Each air bubble should be approximately 3-5mm and the media column containing the embryo(s) approximately 3-5mm.

Replace the lid on the embryo transfer dish and keep it on the RI heated stage/inside a gassed G85 or equivalent.

Pass the catheter to the clinician/nurse (if a malleable or outer catheter has been used you may be required to assist feeding the inner catheter through the outer).

Once the clinician/nurse has notified that the catheter is in the correct position, plunge the syringe down to the 0.18-0.19ml marker on the syringe. Due to the residual volume in the catheter it is recommended that the embryologist ‘plunge’ the syringe to prevent excess media being inseminated.

**Option 2:**

The catheter can be rinsed with G2+ or left ‘dry’.

If rinsing, while the clinician/nurse is positioning the speculum, remove the 5ml test tube containing G2+ from the gassed incubator. Rinse the catheter/syringe with the G2+ finishing with the syringe at the 0.0ml mark.

When the clinician instructs they are ready, remove the Embryo Transfer dish from the incubator. Alongside a second witness check that the full name, clinic number and D.O.B on the Embryo Transfer dish, RI witness card and laboratory records all match.

Load the embryo(s) into the catheter using the following technique:

AIR – EMBRYO – AIR - MEDIA

Each air bubble should be approximately 3-5mm and the embryo column containing the embryo(s) approximately 3-5mm. The media column without the embryos should again be 3-5mm.

Replace the lid on the embryo transfer dish and keep it on the RI heated stage/inside a gassed G85 or equivalent.

Pass the catheter to the clinician/nurse (if a malleable or outer catheter has been used you may be required to assist feeding the inner catheter through the outer).

Once the catheter is in the correct position, plunge the syringe down to the 0.0ml marker on the syringe (embryologist or clinician can plunge the syringe).

**Catheter Rinsing Following the Procedure**

Once the clinician/nurse has removed the catheter, return it to the laboratory.

Rinse the catheter, and outer if used, into the embryo transfer dish whilst checking for any retained embryos.

If a retained embryo(s) is found, notify the clinician/nurse and immediately reload the catheter and repeat the procedure.

If no embryos are found, notify the clinician/nurse that the procedure is complete.

Clear the flowhood of any used consumables. Use Oosafe spray ± sterile water to clean the flowhood.

Complete the laboratory and witnessing records.

##### 5.10 Difficult Embryo Transfer

In the event that the embryos have been loaded into the catheter and the clinician cannot pass the catheter, return the catheter to the laboratory and expel the embryos back into the embryo transfer dish. Replace the embryo transfer dish back into a gassed incubator until the clinician is ready to attempt the procedure again.

If an alternative catheter is required, prepare the catheter as described above.

A spare 1-2ml of G2+ is available if a further catheter needs to be flushed (see section 5.4).

#### Standards/Key Performance/Quality Indicators

Implantation Rate Day 5 ≥35%

% Patients having Day 5 Transfer ≥90%

#### Monitoring

A Oxford Medical Kuwait laboratory manager is responsible for auditing this SOP at least once annually.

The local laboratory manager is responsible for monitoring each embryology operator’s clinical pregnancy rate per embryo transfer. Any deviation from KPI will be discussed at team meetings or supervision meetings

The percentage of patients having a day 5 ET, clinical pregnancy rates and implantation rates are reviewed every 4 months at the Oxford Medical Kuwait KPI meeting.
