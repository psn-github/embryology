## 1. Purpose

To define how a surgically retrieved (autologous) testicular or epididymal specimen is received in the laboratory, processed to recover spermatozoa, and prepared for ICSI or cryopreservation, recognising that these samples are scarce, often non-motile or immotile at recovery, and must be handled to maximise the yield of viable cells.

## 2. Scope

Applies to specimens obtained by surgical sperm retrieval from the **male partner of the patient couple** (autologous) — for example testicular tissue/aspirate (TESE/TESA) and epididymal aspirate (PESA/MESA). Covers laboratory receipt, processing, search, and preparation for same-day ICSI or for cryopreservation (ELB-SOP-023). Surgery is performed by the treating clinician and is out of laboratory scope. Donor samples are out of scope and are not handled.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Performing embryologist** — trained and signed off in surgical-sperm processing and microscopy (ELB-SOP-005); receives, processes, searches and records.
- **Witness (second embryologist or RI Witness electronic system)** — confirms patient identity at handover and at every transfer (ELB-SOP-002).
- **Treating clinician** — performs the retrieval, labels the specimen and confirms identity at handover.
- **Senior embryologist / Laboratory Lead** — confirms presence/absence of sperm before the surgeon closes (where the workflow requires) and reviews any deviation.

## 4. Definitions & abbreviations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| TESE / TESA | Testicular sperm extraction (tissue) / aspiration |
| PESA / MESA | Percutaneous / microsurgical epididymal sperm aspiration |
| Mincing | Mechanical disaggregation of testicular tissue to release sperm |
| Twitching | Minimal tail movement used as a viability sign in otherwise immotile testicular sperm |
| EW | Electronic witnessing (RI Witness) |

## 5. Equipment, materials & media

- Class II laminar flow hood (ELB-SOP-044); stereomicroscope and inverted microscope with heated stage.
- Buffered handling medium and culture medium; sterile dishes; fine needles/scalpel/slides for mincing; centrifuge tubes (lots recorded on **ELB-LOG-003**).
- Erythrocyte-lysing buffer where required by local protocol; cryoprotectant and devices if freezing (ELB-SOP-023).
- RI Witness reader at the bench; timer.

> **IFU cross-reference.** Media, lysing buffer and cryoprotectant are used per the manufacturer Instructions For Use. Mechanical disaggregation and any enzymatic step follow the local validated technique referenced in Appendix A.

## 6. Pre-procedure checks

1. Confirm the scheduled retrieval, the matched oocyte cohort (for same-day ICSI), and that order and consent (including consent to freeze surplus) are in place.
2. Prepare labelled dishes and warmed media before the specimen arrives; confirm working temperature.
3. Confirm the planned route: immediate processing for ICSI, cryopreservation, or both.

> **CRITICAL — identity at handover.** Take the specimen only with the clinician's positive patient identification and a labelled container, and complete an EW identity match before processing (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). Only one patient's specimen is open at the workstation.

## 7. Procedure

1. Receive the specimen with the clinician; confirm identity and perform the **EW identity match** (Section 8, checkpoint W1). Record receipt.
2. Transfer the tissue/aspirate into labelled warmed medium.
3. **Testicular tissue:** mince mechanically in medium to release sperm; where the local protocol uses an enzymatic step, follow the validated method (Appendix A). Apply erythrocyte-lysing buffer if required to clear red cells.
4. **Epididymal aspirate:** dilute in medium and assess directly.
5. Search systematically under magnification for spermatozoa; assess for motility or, where immotile, for viability signs (e.g. twitching). Record the search outcome.
6. Concentrate the recovered sperm by gentle centrifugation per IFU; resuspend in a small defined volume of handling medium.
7. Route the recovered sperm:
   - For ICSI, hold under correct conditions and release to ELB-SOP-027 after an **EW match** (checkpoint W2).
   - For cryopreservation of surplus, proceed to ELB-SOP-023 after an **EW match**.
8. Record specimen type, processing method, search time, motility/viability findings, recovered volume and routing on **ELB-LOG-003** (and **ELB-LOG-004** if frozen).

## 8. Critical control points & witnessing

Witnessing is mandatory at handover and at every transfer (ELB-SOP-002). RI Witness performs the electronic match; a manual second-person check is the fallback if EW is unavailable.

| Checkpoint | What is verified | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **W1** | Patient identity vs specimen label vs order at clinician handover | EW match (RI Witness) + label read |
| **W2** | Recovered sperm correctly labelled and routed to ICSI / cryo for the right cycle | EW match |

> **CRITICAL CONTROL POINT.** A failed or absent witness match halts processing. These specimens are irreplaceable in the moment — resolve identity and re-witness before continuing, but never discard tissue to expedite a check. Log any EW downtime and the manual fallback used.

## 9. Acceptance / rejection criteria

- Processing is **successful** when viable spermatozoa are recovered sufficient for the intended ICSI cohort or for cryopreservation; sufficiency criteria and viability-selection guidance are in **Appendix A**.
- Where **no sperm** is found after a thorough, documented search, escalate to the senior embryologist and clinician before concluding; extend the search and re-examine residual tissue per local protocol.
- A confirmed absence of recoverable sperm is communicated to the clinician; the oocyte cohort plan is reviewed under the cycle decision.

## 10. Deviations

Any departure — failed recovery, contamination, processing error, EW downtime, or an identity discrepancy at handover — is managed under the deviation and CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006) and recorded in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023). A failed retrieval affecting a committed oocyte cohort is reviewed under the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005).

## 11. Records generated

- Sperm preparation worksheet — **ELB-LOG-003** (specimen type, search, findings, routing).
- Sperm cryopreservation & post-thaw record — **ELB-LOG-004** (if surplus is frozen).
- Any deviation entry — **ELB-LOG-023**.

## 14. Teaching point

Surgical sperm are scarce and fragile, and almost always require ICSI — so the laboratory's job is patient recovery, not selection on motility you will not find. An immotile testicular spermatozoon can still be alive; recognising a viability sign and protecting every cell recovered is what turns a difficult retrieval into a usable cohort. Because the specimen is irreplaceable in the moment, identity discipline and gentle, systematic processing matter more here than anywhere else in andrology.
