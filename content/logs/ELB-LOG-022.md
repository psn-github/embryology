## Purpose

To record monitoring of laboratory air quality — total volatile organic compounds (TVOC) and the related environmental parameters that protect gamete and embryo culture — so that the working environment is shown to remain within the acceptable range defined in Appendix A, and so that any excursion or alert triggers a documented response.

## Scope

Applies to TVOC and air-quality monitoring of the embryology laboratory and clean-air workspaces under ELB-SOP-013, whether by continuous monitor or spot measurement. Covers routine readings, filter/scavenger changes and alert events. Incubator gas readings are recorded separately (ELB-LOG-018).

> **Acceptance is by Appendix A.** The acceptable TVOC range, the alert threshold and the monitoring frequency are locally defined (🔧 Appendix A, ELB-APP-A). On reaching the alert threshold, identify and remove the source, increase air handling per ELB-SOP-013, and assess any impact on culture before continuing.

## Completion instructions

1. Complete one row at each scheduled reading and whenever the monitor alerts.
2. Record the TVOC value and mark whether it is within range and whether the alert threshold was reached.
3. Record temperature, humidity and, where monitored, differential pressure.
4. Note filter / VOC-scavenger status and any change made.
5. On an alert, document the action taken and raise a deviation (ELB-POL-006 / ELB-LOG-023) where the excursion is outside the acceptable range or affects culture conditions.
