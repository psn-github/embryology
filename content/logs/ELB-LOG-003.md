## Purpose

To record the preparation of a sperm sample for insemination or ICSI, including the method used, media and consumable lots, and the pre- and post-preparation parameters. Completed under the relevant preparation SOP (ELB-SOP-019 ejaculated, ELB-SOP-020 frozen, ELB-SOP-021 surgical/testicular).

## Scope

Preparation of autologous partner sperm for treatment. Identity is confirmed per ELB-SOP-016 and the prepared sample is carried forward to insemination (ELB-LOG-006) or ICSI (ELB-LOG-008).

## Completion instructions

Confirm two identifiers against the sample and request per ELB-SOP-016 before starting. Record all media and consumable lot numbers. Enter pre-prep parameters from the analysis worksheet where available; measure post-prep parameters on the final preparation. Label the prepared tube with two identifiers immediately.

## Witnessing & sign-off

Labelling of the prepared sample and its hand-off to insemination/ICSI are witnessed checkpoints (W) per ELB-SOP-002 (RI Witness or manual second-person check).
