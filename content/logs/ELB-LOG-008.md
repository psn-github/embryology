## Purpose

To record an ICSI procedure: oocyte maturity counts, the sperm source used, the number of oocytes injected, and any degeneration, with the witnessing of the gamete combination. This record pairs with the ICSI SOP (ELB-SOP-027).

## Scope

ICSI on autologous oocytes with autologous sperm (ejaculated, frozen-thawed or surgical). Denudation is performed under ELB-SOP-026; the cohort proceeds to fertilisation assessment (ELB-LOG-007) and culture (ELB-LOG-009).

> The oocyte-degeneration alert threshold, maximum time outside the incubator per batch, injection-dish equilibration time and stage setpoint are locally defined and held in Appendix A (flagged 🔧). A post-injection degeneration rate above the local alert threshold is managed under ELB-POL-006 with a deviation log entry (ELB-LOG-023).

## Completion instructions

Confirm by witnessing that the oocytes and sperm belong to the same couple before injection (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). Record maturity counts before injection; the totals must reconcile (MII + MI + GV + degenerate = total denuded). Record the sperm source and any degeneration. Inject MII oocytes only.
