## Purpose

To record the laboratory side of oocyte retrieval: the identification of the patient, the collection and counting of cumulus-oocyte complexes (COCs), media and consumable lots, and the maturity assessment that determines the fertilisation route. Completed under the oocyte retrieval SOP (ELB-SOP-024).

## Scope

Oocyte retrieval for autologous treatment. The oocyte count and maturity carried here feed insemination (ELB-LOG-006), ICSI (ELB-LOG-008) and culture (ELB-LOG-009).

## Completion instructions

Confirm patient identity per ELB-SOP-016 before any tube is opened. Count COCs as tubes arrive and reconcile against the follicle count. Record all media and consumable lots. Enter maturity counts only after denudation where ICSI is planned; for conventional insemination, count COCs and assess maturity as the SOP directs.
