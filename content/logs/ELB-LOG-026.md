## Purpose

To record that staff have read and understood each issued or revised controlled document, and to record the competency sign-off that authorises an embryologist to perform a procedure unsupervised. The log links document control to individual competence, demonstrating that only trained, current staff perform clinical work.

## Scope

Applies to all Embryology Laboratory staff and to every controlled document issued or revised under ELB-SOP-001, and to competency assessments performed under the training & competency policy (ELB-SOP-005). It covers initial training, re-acknowledgment after a document revision, and periodic competency re-assessment.

> **Competence precedes practice.** No embryologist performs a procedure unsupervised until signed off as competent for it (ELB-SOP-005), and no revised document takes effect for an individual until they have acknowledged it. The re-assessment interval is locally defined (🔧 Appendix A, ELB-APP-A).

## Completion instructions

1. On issue or revision of a controlled document, record it in Part 1 and obtain each relevant staff member's acknowledgment before the document is relied upon.
2. Record competency assessments in Part 2, naming the method, assessor and outcome.
3. Mark clearly whether the outcome authorises unsupervised practice; set the next re-assessment date.
4. Where an assessment outcome is "not yet competent", restrict the individual to supervised practice and arrange further training (ELB-SOP-005).
5. Keep entries current; an out-of-date competency is treated as a deviation (ELB-LOG-023).
