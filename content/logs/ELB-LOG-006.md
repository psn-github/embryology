## Purpose

To record conventional (standard) insemination — the co-incubation of prepared partner sperm with the patient's oocytes — including the insemination concentration, timing relative to retrieval, and the witnessing of the gamete combination. Completed under the conventional insemination SOP (ELB-SOP-025).

## Scope

Conventional insemination of autologous oocytes with autologous prepared sperm. ICSI is recorded separately (ELB-LOG-008). The cohort is carried forward to fertilisation assessment (ELB-LOG-007) and culture (ELB-LOG-009).

> The interval from retrieval to insemination and the motile-sperm insemination concentration are locally defined and held in Appendix A (flagged 🔧). Standard insemination requires an adequate progressively motile count; if the prepared sample is marginal, the decision to proceed or convert to ICSI follows the SOP and is recorded.

## Completion instructions

Confirm that the oocytes and the prepared sperm belong to the same couple, by witnessing, before combining them (ELB-SOP-016, ELB-SOP-002). Record the retrieval and insemination times to calculate the interval. Label the insemination dish with two identifiers and the incubator position.

## Witnessing & sign-off

Combining oocytes and sperm is the critical witnessed checkpoint (W) per ELB-SOP-002 — two gamete sources meet, so identity must be confirmed by RI Witness electronic match (or a manual second-person check if EW is unavailable) immediately before insemination.
