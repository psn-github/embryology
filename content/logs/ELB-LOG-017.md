## Purpose

To record the final disposition or external transfer of cryopreserved material under ELB-SOP-037: discard, transfer to another licensed centre, or other action elected by the patient couple. This record pairs with the patient's disposition election (ELB-FORM-003) and is governed by the informed-consent policy (ELB-POL-009). Disposition is irreversible; this record is the auditable proof that it was authorised, witnessed and executed correctly.

## Scope

One record per disposition event. Applies to embryos, oocytes and sperm belonging to the patient couple (autologous) only. A valid, signed disposition election (ELB-FORM-003) must be on file before any action. For embryos, the election of both partners is required.

> A failed or absent witness match halts the action. Two-person verification of identity and authorisation is mandatory because disposition cannot be undone.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm a valid, signed disposition election (ELB-FORM-003) is on file and, for embryos, that both partners have consented.
2. Obtain and record authorisation before any action.
3. List each device, reconciled against the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016).
4. Complete the transfer-out or discard block as applicable.
5. Complete both witnessing checkpoints with operator/witness identifiers and times.
6. Update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) to mark the material as disposed/transferred.
7. Note any deviation under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023); sign and date below.

## Records & retention

This is a permanent controlled record, retained indefinitely as the evidence of authorised disposition, per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001), the cryostorage security policy (ELB-POL-007) and the informed-consent policy (ELB-POL-009). It is filed with the patient's disposition election (ELB-FORM-003).
