## Purpose

To record every deviation from a controlled procedure, specification or expected outcome, together with its investigation, corrective and preventive action (CAPA) and closure. The log is the central register that gives the laboratory oversight of what went wrong, what was done about it, and whether the action worked.

## Scope

Applies to all deviations identified in the Embryology Laboratory under ELB-SOP-003 and the deviation & CAPA policy (ELB-POL-006): procedural departures, equipment and environmental excursions, identity or witnessing failures, and out-of-specification results. Deviations that meet the adverse-event definition are additionally managed under ELB-POL-005 and recorded on the adverse-event report (ELB-LOG-025).

> **No deviation is closed without an effectiveness check.** Containment is not closure. A deviation is closed only when the root cause is identified, the corrective and preventive actions are complete, and their effectiveness has been confirmed (ELB-POL-006). Severity grading and escalation thresholds are defined locally (🔧 Appendix A, ELB-APP-A).

## Completion instructions

1. Open a numbered entry as soon as a deviation is identified; record the immediate containment.
2. Notify the senior embryologist / Quality Manager per the grading in ELB-POL-006; escalate to an adverse-event report (ELB-LOG-025) where the criteria are met.
3. Investigate and record the root cause; do not record a symptom as a cause.
4. Define corrective and preventive actions with an owner and a target date.
5. Verify and record the effectiveness of the CAPA before closing the entry.
6. Have the closure approved and dated by the senior embryologist or Quality Manager.
