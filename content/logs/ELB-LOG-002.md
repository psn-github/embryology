## Purpose

To record the macroscopic and microscopic analysis of a semen sample. Method, terminology and reference values follow the current WHO laboratory manual for the examination and processing of human semen.

## Scope

Diagnostic and pre-treatment semen analyses on the male partner of an autologous couple. Links to the receipt log (ELB-LOG-001) and, where the sample proceeds, the preparation worksheet (ELB-LOG-003).

## Completion instructions

Confirm identity per ELB-SOP-016 before analysis. Assess only after liquefaction is complete. Perform replicate counts and accept only when replicates agree per the WHO manual; otherwise re-prepare and recount. Record the measured value, not a normal/abnormal label.
