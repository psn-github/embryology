## Purpose

To record the quality-control checks of the digital gas analyser used to verify incubator CO₂ (and where applicable O₂) concentrations. The log demonstrates that the analyser itself is functioning correctly and is verified at the frequency defined in Appendix A before its readings are relied upon to accept an incubator.

## Scope

Applies to every digital gas analyser held in the Embryology Laboratory and to each verification, self-check or reference-gas comparison performed under OMK-SOP-EMB-0063. It does not record the incubator CO₂/O₂ readings themselves — those are entered on the incubator QC log (OMK-LOG-EMB-0061).

> **Acceptance is by Appendix A.** The verification frequency and the acceptable deviation of the analyser from the reference value are locally defined (🔧 Appendix A, OMK-APP-EMB-A). Do not use an analyser that is overdue for verification or that reads outside the acceptance limit.

## Completion instructions

1. Complete one row at each scheduled verification and after any battery or sensor change.
2. Allow the analyser to warm up and stabilise per OMK-SOP-EMB-0063 before recording a reading.
3. Where a certified reference gas is used, record its lot, expiry and certificate value.
4. Calculate the deviation and mark whether it is within the Appendix A acceptance limit.
5. If the analyser fails verification, quarantine it, record the failure, raise a deviation (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005 / OMK-SOP-EMB-0005) and do not use it for incubator QC until re-verified.
