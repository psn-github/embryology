## Purpose

To record the freezing of an autologous sperm sample and any subsequent thaw, including cryoprotectant and consumable lots, the number and location of devices stored, and the post-thaw assessment. Completed under the sperm cryopreservation SOP (ELB-SOP-023).

## Scope

Cryopreservation and warming of the male partner's own sperm. Consent is recorded on ELB-FORM-001; inventory is maintained on ELB-LOG-016. Donor sperm is out of scope and not handled.

> Freezing protocol, cooling profile and the expected minimum post-thaw motility recovery are locally defined and held in Appendix A (flagged 🔧). A recovery below the local expectation is recorded and, where it affects the treatment plan, managed under ELB-POL-006 with an entry in the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## Completion instructions

Confirm identity and storage consent (ELB-FORM-001) before freezing per ELB-SOP-016. Label every device with two identifiers. Record the exact tank/canister/position and update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) at freeze and on any thaw or removal.

## Witnessing & sign-off

Labelling, placement into storage and removal/thaw are witnessed checkpoints (W) per ELB-SOP-002 (RI Witness or manual second-person check).
