## Purpose

To record manufacturer recalls, field-safety notices and product alerts affecting media, consumables, devices or reagents used in the Embryology Laboratory, together with the action taken. The log demonstrates that affected lots are identified, removed from use and dispositioned, and that any clinical impact is assessed.

## Scope

Applies to every recall or product alert received under the manufacturer recalls & product alerts policy (ELB-POL-004), for any lot of media, oil, consumable, reagent or device. Inventory control and quarantine are performed under ELB-SOP-007. Any resulting clinical impact is managed as a deviation (ELB-LOG-023) or adverse event as applicable.

> **Quarantine first.** On receipt of a recall, immediately identify and quarantine any affected lot held in inventory (ELB-SOP-007) before assessing wider impact. Where affected product has already been used, assess clinical impact and raise a deviation (ELB-POL-006 / ELB-LOG-023) or adverse-event report as required.

## Completion instructions

1. Open a numbered entry when a recall or alert is received and record its source and reason.
2. Cross-check affected lot numbers against current and recent inventory (ELB-SOP-007).
3. Quarantine, return, destroy or monitor as the alert directs; record the action and any substitute lot.
4. Determine whether affected product reached clinical use and assess impact accordingly.
5. Close the entry once the action is complete and approved by the senior embryologist or Quality Manager.
