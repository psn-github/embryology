## Purpose

To record an adverse event — an incident that caused, or had the potential to cause, harm to a patient or to the gametes/embryos in the laboratory's care — and to link it to its root-cause analysis and onward reporting. The report ensures serious events are investigated rigorously, learned from and reported to the appropriate parties.

## Scope

Applies to adverse events and near misses in the Embryology Laboratory managed under the adverse-events policy (ELB-POL-005) and ELB-SOP-004: loss of or damage to gametes/embryos, identity or witnessing failures reaching the patient, equipment failures with clinical impact, and any event meeting the local reporting criteria. Lower-grade departures without adverse-event criteria are managed solely on the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

> **CRITICAL — escalate without delay.** An adverse event is reported to the senior embryologist and Medical Director immediately, before this form is complete. Patient safety and material protection come first; documentation follows. Root-cause analysis is performed under ELB-SOP-004; reporting obligations and severity grading are defined locally (🔧 Appendix A, ELB-APP-A) and in ELB-POL-005.

## Completion instructions

1. Open a numbered report as soon as the event is identified; record the immediate action and patient care.
2. Escalate to the senior embryologist and Medical Director per ELB-POL-005 without waiting for full completion.
3. Link the corresponding deviation entry (ELB-LOG-023) and initiate root-cause analysis (ELB-SOP-004).
4. Record corrective and preventive actions and confirm their effectiveness.
5. Record to whom and when the event was reported.
6. Close the report only with Medical Director or Quality Manager approval.
