## Purpose

To maintain the master inventory of all cryopreserved material in the Embryology Laboratory under ELB-SOP-036/037: the location of every device by tank, canister and position, the patient it belongs to, and the consented storage period. This inventory is the authoritative map of what is stored, where, and for how long; it underpins access control (ELB-POL-007) and storage consent (ELB-POL-009).

## Scope

Covers all stored embryos, oocytes and sperm belonging to the patient couple (autologous) only. Every entry, removal and relocation is recorded. The inventory is reconciled to physical stock at the frequency defined in Appendix A 🔧. Material is added from the relevant freeze record (e.g. ELB-LOG-013) and removed via warming or disposition (ELB-LOG-017).

## Completion instructions

1. Make an entry for every device placed into storage, transcribed from the freeze record, with full coordinates (tank / canister / position).
2. Record the consent reference, the consented storage period and the renewal due date for every entry.
3. Update the status field on every removal, relocation or disposition, with the date and the governing record reference.
4. Witness all additions and removals against device ID and position.
5. Perform and record periodic reconciliation at the Appendix A 🔧 frequency; raise any discrepancy under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).
6. Flag entries approaching their renewal/expiry date for the consent and disposition process.

## Records & retention

The cryostorage inventory is a permanent controlled record, retained for the life of the stored material and beyond per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001) and the cryostorage security policy (ELB-POL-007). Storage limits and renewal/reconciliation intervals are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
