## Purpose

To record every assisted-hatching procedure performed under ELB-SOP-030: the embryo treated, the method used, the operator, the equipment and the witnessing checkpoints. This worksheet provides the traceable record that links the procedure to the culture record (ELB-LOG-009) and to subsequent transfer or cryopreservation.

## Scope

One record per patient per hatching session. Applies to embryos derived from the patient couple's own gametes only (autologous). Complete contemporaneously with the procedure; do not reconstruct from memory.

## Witnessing (per ELB-SOP-002)

Witnessing is mandatory wherever embryos are identified or moved between vessels. Record the RI Witness electronic match; where electronic witnessing is unavailable, a second embryologist performs and signs a manual check.

| Checkpoint | Verifies | Method (EW / manual) | Operator | Witness | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Embryo dish identity matches worklist before hatching | | | | |
| W2 | Treated embryos returned to correctly labelled dish/position | | | | |

> Identity check before any embryo is removed from its dish. A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure: resolve identity, then re-witness.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity and complete the identification block before handling any embryo.
2. Record method and equipment settings before starting; anchor any setpoint to Appendix A (ELB-APP-A) — do not write bare numbers without a source.
3. Complete one row per embryo treated, including the hatching site and whether the breach was confirmed.
4. Record both witnessing checkpoints with operator and witness identifiers and times.
5. Note any deviation (e.g. unexpected lysis, equipment fault, electronic-witness downtime) and raise it under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).
6. Sign and date below. Update the embryo culture record (ELB-LOG-009).

## Records & retention

This record is part of the patient's laboratory cycle file and is retained per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001) and the cryostorage/consent retention requirements where the embryo is subsequently stored. Locally defined values referenced here are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
