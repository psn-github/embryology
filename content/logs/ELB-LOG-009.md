## Purpose

To record the daily development and morphological grading of each embryo in a cohort, from the fertilised zygote to the blastocyst, using Istanbul Consensus grading. Tracks each embryo to its fate (transfer, freeze, continued culture or discard). Grading follows the morphology grading SOP (ELB-SOP-029).

## Scope

Culture and grading of embryos from autologous gametes. Continues from the fertilisation assessment (ELB-LOG-007); outcomes feed transfer (ELB-LOG-012) and vitrification (ELB-LOG-013).

## Daily development & grading (per embryo)

Use one row per embryo per assessment day. Cleavage stage records cell number, fragmentation and symmetry; blastocyst stage records expansion grade with inner cell mass (ICM) and trophectoderm (TE) grades — Istanbul Consensus.

## Completion instructions

Identify the dish against the cycle by two identifiers before each assessment. Grade each embryo on each scheduled day, recording cleavage parameters or blastocyst ICM/TE grades per Istanbul Consensus. Reconcile the cohort: 2PN entering culture must equal the sum transferred, frozen, continued and discarded.
