## Purpose

To record the receipt of every semen sample entering the laboratory, establishing chain of custody from production to processing. Completed under the semen receipt and tracking SOP (ELB-SOP-018).

## Scope

All ejaculated semen samples received for analysis, preparation, insemination/ICSI or cryopreservation, produced by the male partner of an autologous couple. Donor samples are out of scope and not handled.

## Acceptance / rejection

A sample is recorded as rejected if identity cannot be confirmed, the container carries fewer than two identifiers, integrity is compromised, or it falls outside the locally defined acceptance window for time-since-production (Appendix A 🔧). Record the reason and the action taken; manage under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).

## Completion instructions

Complete one row per sample at the bench on receipt, before processing begins. Confirm two patient identifiers against the request and the container label per ELB-SOP-016. Enter all times in 24-hour format.

## Witnessing & sign-off

Identity confirmation at receipt is a witnessed checkpoint (W) per ELB-SOP-002 — RI Witness electronic match, or a second-person manual check if EW is unavailable.
