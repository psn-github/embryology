## Purpose

To record every embryo vitrification performed under ELB-SOP-033: which embryos were frozen, the kit and protocol used, the straw/device identifiers and the storage position assigned. This record establishes the device-to-embryo link that the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and any later warming (ELB-LOG-015) depend on.

## Scope

One record per patient per vitrification session. Applies to embryos derived from the patient couple's own gametes only (autologous). Vitrification is performed only against valid storage consent (ELB-POL-009 / ELB-FORM-001). Complete contemporaneously.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity and that storage consent is valid and on file before freezing.
2. Record kit lots and protocol adherence; anchor any setpoint to Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
3. Complete one row per embryo with its unique device ID and full storage coordinates.
4. Complete all witnessing checkpoints, including placement and logging to the cryostorage inventory (W3).
5. Update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) with the new device(s).
6. Note any deviation under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).
7. Sign and date below.

## Records & retention

This record is part of the patient's laboratory cycle file and is retained for the full storage period of the embryos and per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001). Storage is governed by consent (ELB-POL-009). Locally defined values referenced here are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
