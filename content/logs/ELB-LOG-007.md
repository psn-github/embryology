## Purpose

To record the assessment of fertilisation on the morning after insemination or ICSI — the count of normally fertilised (2PN) oocytes and of abnormal and unfertilised outcomes — at the time window specified in Appendix A. Completed under the determination of fertilisation SOP (ELB-SOP-028).

## Scope

Fertilisation assessment of autologous oocytes from conventional insemination (ELB-LOG-006) or ICSI (ELB-LOG-008). The fertilised cohort is carried forward to culture (ELB-LOG-009).

## Completion instructions

Identify the dish against the cycle by two identifiers before assessment. Perform the check within the Appendix A window. Count pronuclei and polar bodies; record 2PN, 1PN, ≥3PN, 0PN and degenerate separately — the totals must reconcile against the number of oocytes assessed. Abnormally fertilised zygotes are recorded and are not selected for transfer.
