## Purpose

To record every warming of cryopreserved embryos or oocytes performed under ELB-SOP-035: the device retrieved from storage, the kit and protocol used, and the post-warming survival and (for embryos) re-expansion outcome. Survival and re-expansion are assessed against the thresholds held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).

## Scope

One record per patient per warming session. Applies to embryos and oocytes belonging to the patient couple (autologous) only. Complete contemporaneously, including the retrieval-from-storage step.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity and the intended use before retrieving any device.
2. Record the device retrieved and cross-check it against the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016).
3. Record kit lots and protocol adherence; anchor any setpoint to Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
4. Assess and record survival and (for embryos) re-expansion against the Appendix A thresholds.
5. Complete both witnessing checkpoints with operator/witness identifiers and times.
6. Update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) to reflect the device's removal.
7. Note any deviation (including sub-threshold survival) under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023); sign and date below.

## Records & retention

This record is part of the patient's laboratory cycle file and is retained per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001). Survival and re-expansion data also feed the laboratory's cryopreservation key-performance-indicator review. Locally defined values referenced here are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
