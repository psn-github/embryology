## Purpose

To record every embryo transfer performed under ELB-SOP-032: which embryo(s) were transferred, the number transferred, their grade, the catheter used and the witnessing checkpoints. The number of embryos transferred is a critical, double-witnessed entry; this record is the definitive account of it.

## Scope

One record per patient per transfer. Applies to embryos derived from the patient couple's own gametes only (autologous), whether fresh or warmed (link the warming record ELB-LOG-015 where applicable). Complete contemporaneously.

> Patient identity is confirmed at the bedside by clinician and embryologist together against the dish label and worklist. A failed or absent witness match halts the transfer: resolve identity, then re-witness.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity at the workstation and at the bedside before loading.
2. List the embryo(s) selected and confirm the number against the consented plan.
3. Record the exact number of embryos transferred — this is the key field and is witnessed (W2).
4. After transfer, record the catheter check and the disposition of any surplus embryos.
5. Complete all witnessing checkpoints with operator/witness identifiers and times.
6. Note any deviation (e.g. retained embryo, difficult transfer, electronic-witness downtime) under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).
7. Sign and date below; update the embryo culture record (ELB-LOG-009).
