## Purpose

To record every oocyte vitrification performed under ELB-SOP-034: the mature oocytes frozen, the kit and protocol used, the straw/device identifiers and the storage position assigned. This record establishes the device-to-oocyte link relied on by the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) and any later warming (ELB-LOG-015).

## Scope

One record per patient per vitrification session. Applies to oocytes retrieved from the patient (autologous) only. Only mature (MII) oocytes are vitrified. Vitrification is performed only against valid storage consent (ELB-POL-009 / ELB-FORM-001). Complete contemporaneously.

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity and that storage consent is valid and on file before freezing.
2. Record the maturity summary; only MII oocytes are vitrified.
3. Record kit lots and protocol adherence; anchor any setpoint to Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
4. Complete one row per device with its unique ID, oocyte count and full storage coordinates.
5. Complete all witnessing checkpoints, including placement and logging to the cryostorage inventory (W3).
6. Update the cryostorage inventory (ELB-LOG-016) with the new device(s).
7. Note any deviation under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023); sign and date below.

## Records & retention

This record is part of the patient's laboratory cycle file and is retained for the full storage period of the oocytes and per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001). Storage is governed by consent (ELB-POL-009). Locally defined values referenced here are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
