## Purpose

To record liquid-nitrogen (LN2) top-ups and the routine monitoring of cryostorage tanks — level or weight, temperature and alarm function — so that stored gametes and embryos remain continuously below the safe storage temperature and any loss of LN2 is detected before it threatens the inventory.

## Scope

Applies to every cryostorage and transport dewar held in the Embryology Laboratory under OMK-SOP-EMB-0045. Covers scheduled monitoring checks, manual and automated fills, and alarm tests. Tank contents and slot positions are held on the cryostorage inventory (OMK-SOP-EMB-0045); this log records the tank's physical condition only.

> **CRITICAL — cryostorage safety.** The minimum level, the temperature alert limit and the monitoring and fill frequencies are locally defined (🔧 Appendix A, OMK-APP-EMB-A). A tank below its minimum level, out of temperature range, or with a failed alarm is a critical event: act immediately, escalate, and raise a deviation (OMK-SOP-EMB-0005 / OMK-SOP-EMB-0005). Handle LN2 per the GHS hazard controls in OMK-SOP-EMB-0045 (asphyxiation and cold-burn risk).

## Completion instructions

1. Complete one row at each monitoring check and each fill, at the frequency set in Appendix A.
2. Record level/weight and temperature, and mark whether each is within the Appendix A limits.
3. When filling, record the volume or duration and the supply source.
4. Test and confirm the tank alarm at the scheduled interval and after any service.
5. If a tank is below minimum, out of range or alarming, transfer or protect the inventory per OMK-SOP-EMB-0045, escalate per OMK-POL-EMB-0007, and raise a deviation.
