## Purpose

To record every embryo biopsy performed for preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) under ELB-SOP-031, with full traceability from the embryo to the labelled tubing/PCR tube sent to the genetics laboratory. The tubing identifier is the single point at which an embryo and its genetic result are linked; this record is the audit trail for that link.

## Scope

One record per patient per biopsy session. Applies to embryos derived from the patient couple's own gametes only (autologous). Complete contemporaneously with the procedure.

> A failed or absent witness match halts the procedure. Loading and dispatch witnessing protect the embryo–result link and the chain of custody (ELB-SOP-017).

## Completion instructions

1. Confirm patient/cycle identity and PGT type before handling any embryo.
2. Record method and equipment lots; anchor any setpoint to Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
3. Complete one row per embryo, assigning a unique tube/tubing ID and recording the embryo's fate (vitrification record ELB-LOG-013 where applicable).
4. Complete all three witnessing checkpoints, including the dispatch reconciliation (W3).
5. Complete the chain-of-custody block at dispatch.
6. Note any deviation under ELB-POL-006 and the deviation log (ELB-LOG-023).
7. Sign and date below.

## Records & retention

This record is part of the patient's laboratory cycle file and is retained per the recordkeeping SOP (ELB-SOP-001). The tubing-traceability and chain-of-custody entries are retained for the life of any stored embryo and to support reconciliation with the genetics report. Locally defined values are held in Appendix A (ELB-APP-A).
